Jane Hedengren broke almost every record Katelyn Tuohy ever set. High school records, course records, NCAA records. Nine national high school records in one year at Timpview, the NXN course record, then a freshman year at BYU with collegiate records in the 5000 and 10,000. But this year she got sick before NCAAs, lost at the Cowboy Jamboree and missed Pre-Nationals. Tuohy, Mary Cain, Abby Steiner… so many phenoms before her fell off. Will Jane be next, or is she different?

So what y'all think? Is Jane Hedengren the next great American distance runner, or the next one to fall off? Let me know in the comments.
