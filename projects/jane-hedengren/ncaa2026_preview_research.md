## History angles
- **Last American woman to win: Parker Valby (Florida), 2023, 18:55.2.**
  - Before her: Katelyn Tuohy (NC State), 2022, 19:27.7 (**verified**: NC State won the team title that year too).
  - Doris Lemngole (Kenya, Alabama) won 2024 and 2025.
  - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_women%27s_cross_country_championships ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NCAA_Division_I_cross_country_championships ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NCAA_Division_I_cross_country_championships
- **Only BYU woman ever to win: Whittni Orton, 2021.** NCAA.com says she "became the first Cougar woman to win the title." A Jane win would make her the 2nd BYU woman ever and the 1st American winner since Valby in 2023. (NCAA.com rivalry piece)
- Tuohy's 2022 win "ended a 37-year Wolfpack drought." (NCAA.com)
- **A freshman woman has finished 2nd in each of the last 3 years:** Lemngole 2023, Kosgei 2024, Hedengren 2025. (NCAA.com contenders)
- **The last time the meet was in Terre Haute (2019),** Arkansas beat BYU by 6 points (96–102). (T&FN teams; Wikipedia)

## Story spine (10 lines, strongest verified facts)
1. A year ago, freshman Jane Hedengren finished 2nd at NCAAs (18:38.9), beaten only by Doris Lemngole. Lemngole has since turned pro with On.
2. Since then: NCAA indoor 3000 + 5000 champion, NCAA records at 10,000 (30:46.80) and 5000 (14:50.50).
3. Then illness: only 3rd in the 10k and 9th in the 5k at NCAA outdoors, as Iowa State's Kirwa and New Mexico's Kosgei beat her in the 10k.
4. This fall, her first loss came on 26 Sep. Oklahoma State's Billah Jepkirui ran her down at the Cowboy Jamboree; Jane was 3rd.
5. Sick again, she missed Pre-Nats on the NCAA course. BYU beat No. 1 NC State 84–88 without her.
6. On 31 Oct she went back to that same Stillwater course, led from the gun in the rain, and won the Big 12 by 10 s over Jepkirui.
7. Then she skipped regionals ("We rested a few women"). BYU finished 3rd and needed an at-large bid. She arrives having raced once in 7 weeks.
8. Waiting for her: NC State's Hannah Gapes of New Zealand, undefeated, with course records at Pre-Nats (18:57.6, first under 19 on this course) and at regionals, where she broke Tuohy's mark.
9. The team race: BYU is ranked No. 1, and BYU or NC State has won every title since 2020. NC State won last year by 16 points.
10. If Jane wins, she is only the 2nd BYU woman ever to win (after Whittni Orton, 2021) and the first American champion since Parker Valby in 2023.
