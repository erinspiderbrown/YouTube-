## 1. Cold open
Y'all remember Katelyn Tuohy. The greatest high school runner ever… and then she disappeared.

Well, there's a girl from Utah who broke almost every record Katelyn Tuohy ever set.

High school records. Course records. NCAA records.

Her name is Jane Hedengren. She's 20 years old.

And right now… she's starting to show some of the same signs.

So is Jane Hedengren the next Katelyn Tuohy? Lemme tell y'all.

## 2. The kid from Provo
Jane Hedengren. Timpview High School. Provo, Utah.

Her dad was an All-American runner. So it's in the blood.

December 2024. Nike Cross Nationals. She wins it in a course record. She broke Katelyn Tuohy's course record.

Then 2025 was crazy.

March. She runs the 5000 in 15:13. Fastest high school girl ever.

3 days later, the mile. 4:26. Another national record.

April. She runs 14:57 in the 5000. The first high school girl ever under 15 minutes.

June. The mile in 4:23.50. Katelyn Tuohy's best in high school was 4:33. Jane ran 10 seconds faster.

Then 9:17 in the 2 mile. Then 8:40 in the 3000.

9 national high school records in 1 year.

## 3. Same story, new name
Now this where it gets familiar.

Katelyn Tuohy had a Nike Cross Nationals title. Jane got one.

Katelyn signed with adidas. Jane signed an NIL deal with Nike.

Katelyn went to NC State and won NCAA titles. Jane went to BYU to run for Coach Diljeet Taylor.

And in college? She didn't slow down. She sped up.

## 4. Freshman year
October 2025. Her first college race. Course record.

She wins the Big 12. Course record again.

NCAA cross country. 2nd place. As a freshman. The fastest freshman in meet history.

Then indoors. December. 14:44 in the 5000. A collegiate record.

February 2026. The Millrose Games. 8:34.98 in the 3000.

And guess whose NCAA record that broke? Katelyn Tuohy's.

March. NCAA indoors. She wins the 5000. Then she wins the 3000. First freshman to ever win both at the same meet.

April. NCAA record in the 10,000. 2 weeks later, NCAA record in the 5000.

## 5. The first cracks
But then… something changed.

NCAA outdoors, June 2026. She's sick going into the meet. 3rd in the 10,000. 9th in the 5000.

September. The Cowboy Jamboree. She hadn't raced in almost a month. 3rd.

The first regular-season loss of her college career.

October. The Pre-National meet. Biggest race of the regular season. Jane don't run. Her coach said she was on the tail end of an illness.

Now don't get me wrong. She came back and won the Big 12 again. By 10 seconds.

But sick at NCAAs. Sick in October. A loss in September.

Where have we heard that before?

## 6. Remember Tuohy
Katelyn Tuohy was the best high school runner ever.

She won in college. Then she turned pro.

And then it was injuries. Health issues she said she was keeping to herself. Then she stopped racing.

Nobody's saying that's Jane. Jane's coach is literally doing the opposite. She cut back her training.

Her coach said, "more is not necessarily better." She said, "Sometimes less is actually more."

That's a coach trying to protect her.

## 7. What's different
And here go the difference.

Tuohy turned pro and left her college team early.

Jane is still at BYU. With the same coach. With a team around her.

And when they asked Kara Goucher, an Olympian, who she would compare Jane to? She said Usain Bolt. She said, "There's no one comparable to her in my mind."

November 21. NCAA cross country championships.

Last year she got 2nd.

This year she's the favorite.

If she wins, she's on her way to being something we ain't never seen.

If she don't… people gon' start asking the same questions they asked about Katelyn Tuohy.
