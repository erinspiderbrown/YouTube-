# Edit style: copied from what performs in the "What happened to the last X winners" format

References (studied with vidIQ video_watch):
- The Longest Yard, "What Happened To The Last 21 Heisman Trophy Winners?" https://youtu.be/TNmGnM61MIw — 1.49M views on an 8.9K-sub channel (~16x outlier). Main template.
- Sports Illustrated College, "The Heisman Winner Who Went UNDRAFTED" https://youtu.be/9Q8QGGn8wS0 — 232x outlier.
- Also in the format: NFL Throwback "What Happened to EVERY Heisman Winner Since 2000" (2.6M), Walking The Line "Last 15 FCS Heisman Winners" (27x).

## What they do (and what we'll copy)
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:20):** hero shot + 2–3 word title pop-ups, then a success-vs-bust contrast cut, then the **roadmap grid**: a tile for every winner (year + face), camera zooms into the first tile, whoosh into year one.
2. **Segment = 60–90 s, 4 beats:** name splash + year stamp → college dominance (race clip + stacked stats) → turning point → "where is he now" verdict.
   - Between segments: back to the grid; the finished tile gets a **fate stamp** (WORLD CHAMP / BANNED / INJURED / STILL GRINDING / BACK TO COLLEGE …). That is the tracker that keeps people watching.
   - Next-player tease + a dark silhouette dropping in before the name card.
3. **Graphics:** bold condensed sans (Anton), white with thick black stroke; stats stacked on the left in big numbers with a red or blue accent; big centered year stamp; school-color outline on name cards. Low points: picture goes **black-and-white** with red bold text (BANNED, HERNIATED DISCS).
4. **Footage:** broadcast race footage, cut every 1.5–2.5 s, slow-mo only on finishes/celebrations; photos as cut-outs over a blurred background with slow Ken Burns; **news headline screenshots** with a highlighter wipe on the key phrase.
5. **Sound:** dark trap / hip-hop instrumental (808s, hats) ducked low under the voice (user wants dark, never upbeat — keep it moody); whoosh on every pop-up, sub-drop on reveals and stamps, glitch hit on bans/injuries, shutter click on photos. Crowd noise from the broadcast mixed low under race clips.
6. **Retention:** mid-video fake YouTube "like + subscribe" click animation (~40% in); ending recap montage + big on-screen comment question.
7. Hard cuts mostly; no neon/HUD this time.
