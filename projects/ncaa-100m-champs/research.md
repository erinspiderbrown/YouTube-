# What happened to the last 10 NCAA men's 100m champions (research v1, 7 Nov 2026)

List: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_meters_at_the_NCAA_Division_I_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships (2020 cancelled, COVID)

| Year | Champion | School | Time | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | Jarrion Lawson | Arkansas | 10.22 | 100/200/LJ triple (first since Jesse Owens). 2017 world LJ silver. June 2018 positive for trenbolone, 4-yr ban 2019; CAS cleared him Mar 2020 (contaminated beef, "no fault"). Missed 2019 Worlds. 2022 US indoor LJ title. Little since. TO VERIFY: 2023-26 |
| 2017 | Christian Coleman | Tennessee | 10.04 | 9.82 collegiate record. 2019 world champ (9.76). Whereabouts ban, missed Tokyo. Back: 2024 world indoor 60 gold, 2023 + 2025 world 4x1 gold. Feb 2026 arrested (paraphernalia, resisting) with Sha'Carri Richardson; pleaded not guilty (TMZ) |
| 2018 | Cameron Burrell | Houston | 10.13 | Son of Leroy Burrell and Michelle Finn-Burrell, Carl Lewis's godson. Died Aug 2021, age 26 (reported suicide, CBS Sports) |
| 2019 | Divine Oduduru | Texas Tech | 9.86 | 9.86/19.73 double. "Athlete 2" in the Eric Lira Rodchenkov Act case (with Okagbare). 6-yr AIU ban to Feb 2029, results from Jul 2021 erased. Maintains innocence |
| 2021 | Terrance Laird | LSU | 10.05 | 100/200 double. Back injury (herniated discs), missed 2022. 2023 comeback (20.06 LA GP). Sparse since. TO VERIFY |
| 2022 | Joseph Fahnbulleh | Florida (Liberia) | 10.00 | Olympic 200 finalist 2021 and 2024 (7th Paris). 2024 African champ 100/200. Liberian 200 record 19.83. Still racing DL 2025-26 |
| 2023 | Courtney Lindsey | Texas Tech | 9.89 | Pro (Nike). 19.71 (2024), 9.82 (2025). 5th at 2026 USAs |
| 2024 | Louie Hinchliffe | Houston (GBR) | 9.95 | Carl Lewis athlete. Paris Olympic 100 finalist. Turned pro (ASICS), 2025 wrecked by stress fracture, hamstring, father's death. 2026: gave up pro deal, NCAA-reinstated, back at Houston with Lewis. Withdrew from Euro 100 final |
| 2025 | Jordan Anthony | Arkansas | 10.07 | Football WR (Kentucky/A&M/Arkansas). Quit football, turned pro (Adidas). First football player to win the Bowerman. 2026 world indoor 60 champ |
| 2026 | Kanyinsola Ajayi | Auburn (NGR) | 9.72w | 9.84 NR at regionals (#2 NCAA all-time). Won London DL, 3rd DL final, Commonwealth bronze |

Sources: Wikipedia pages for each athlete; NBC/ESPN/AW (Lawson CAS); CBS Sports, theGrio (Burrell); ESPN, Guardian NG, Flashscore (Oduduru); T&FN (Laird, Lindsey); Diamond League profiles; ProFootballNetwork (Hinchliffe); WholeHogSports, Olympics.com, Yahoo (Anthony); NCAA.com, Premium Times (Ajayi).
