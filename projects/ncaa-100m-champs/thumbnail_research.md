## Patterns to copy
1. **A row of 3 champions, each with a one-word fate label** (Heisman 21: 1.5M, 16x, copied by others). Each label is a different outcome, so the viewer wants to know which is which. This is the best match for our story: WORLD CHAMP / BANNED / INJURED.
2. **Boom vs bust split** (#1 QBs: 1M, 12x). Two faces, green success tag vs red failure tag, years in the corners.
3. **A lineup of champions + "WHAT HAPPENED?" / "WHERE ARE THEY NOW?"** in big caps (NFL #1 picks 2.75M; FCS Heisman 28x; Hell's Kitchen 145x). The question text does the work. Faces are real and look sharp.
4. **Year-stamped panels** (NCAA's own 100m and women's 100m videos: 8.6x and 13x). Huge yellow years under each sprinter. Native to track.
5. **Big repeated number** ("26 YEARS 26 BUSTS", "37 YEARS OF FAILURE", "5 YEARS LATER"). For us, "10 CHAMPS" with a tally.
- Colours across the winners: yellow pills/bars with black text, red for the negative outcome, green for the positive one. Real photos, never illustrations (except the copycat).
- Titles: "What Happened To The Last N ___?" and "What Happened To Every ___ Since YEAR?" dominate. "Where Are They Now?" works as a suffix/bracket. "What REALLY Happened" adds a little. "Curse" titles have no big wins in this niche.
