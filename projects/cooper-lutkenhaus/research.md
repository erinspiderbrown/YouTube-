# Cooper Lutkenhaus: research (25 Nov 2026)
Northwest High School, Justin, Texas. Dad George is a longtime HS running coach (AP). Turned 17 Dec 2025 (AP: "turns 17 in December" in Aug 2025). Now a HS senior.

## High school
- May 2024 (freshman): 1:49.84 freshman class record (FloTrack title, text only). June 2024 Brooks PR: freshman record; Texas state record 1:47.58 (Dallas News 12 Jun 2024).
- Jan 2025: indoor Texas state record (Dallas News 25 Jan 2025). Feb 2025 Millrose: HS indoor 800 record (MileSplit/T&FN).
- May 2025: Texas 6A state title 1:47.04 (sophomore).
- Jun 2025 Brooks PR: 1:46.26, breaks Michael Granville's HS record 1:46.45 (Bell Gardens, 1996, stood 29 years) (MileSplit, T&FN).
- 21 Jun 2025 Nike Outdoor Nationals: 1:45.45 in the rain, new HS record (Dallas News).
## 2025 pro + Tokyo
- 3 Aug 2025 USAs Eugene: 2nd in 1:42.27 (U18 world record / U20 record per sources), behind Donavan Brazier 1:42.16, ahead of Bryce Hoppel. Made Worlds team at 16: first 16-year-old American at Worlds (NBC/AOL).
- Aug 2025: signs pro with Nike at 16, gives up HS + NCAA eligibility, keeps HS coach (AP, CBS Texas). Texas bars NIL under 17.
- Sep 2025 Tokyo: 7th in heat, 1:47.68, ran wide in outside lanes, out. Called it "the worst race tactics I've had", a learning experience (AP via SM Daily Journal).
