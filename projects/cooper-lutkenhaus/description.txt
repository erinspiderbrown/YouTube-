Cooper Lutkenhaus is still in high school, and he's already a world champion. From a small town outside Fort Worth, he didn't start running until 7th grade. As a freshman he broke the Texas state record, as a sophomore he broke a 29-year-old national high school record, and at 16 he ran 1:42.27 to make the World Championship team. Then as a high school junior he won World Indoors over spring break, beat the Olympic champion in Oslo, and won USAs by more than a second. This is how Cooper came onto the scene.

Facts: Texas state record 1:47.58 as a freshman (2024); 1:46.26 at Brooks PR (June 2025), breaking Michael Granville's 1:46.45 national high school record from 1996; 1:45.45 at Nike Nationals; 2nd at 2025 USAs in 1:42.27 (U18 world record) behind Donavan Brazier's 1:42.16, first 16-year-old American on a World Championship team; signed with Nike at 16 (August 2025); out in the Tokyo heats (1:47.68); 2026: U20 600m world record at Millrose, U20 indoor 800m world record, US indoor title, World Indoor gold in 1:44.24 (youngest World Indoor champion ever), won Stockholm (1:42.70) and Oslo (1:42.08, world U20 record, over Emmanuel Wanyonyi), 2nd at the Prefontaine Classic behind Brandon Miller, US champion by 1.07 s; skipped the Ultimate Championship for his senior year.
