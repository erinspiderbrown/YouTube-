Cooper Lutkenhaus is 17, still in high school, and already a world champion. He broke a 29-year-old high school record, ran 1:42.27 at 16, signed with Nike, got humbled in Tokyo, then came back and won World Indoors, beat the Olympic champion in Oslo and won USAs by over a second. Mary Cain, Athing Mu and Alan Webb were all supposed to be the one. So is Cooper different, or is he the next prodigy to fall off?

Facts: Texas state record 1:47.58 as a freshman (2024); 1:46.26 at Brooks PR (June 2025), breaking Michael Granville's 1:46.45 HS record from 1996; 1:45.45 at Nike Nationals; 2nd at 2025 USAs in 1:42.27 (U18 world record) behind Donavan Brazier's 1:42.16; signed with Nike at 16 (August 2025); 7th in his Tokyo heat (1:47.68); 2026: U20 600m world record at Millrose, U20 indoor 800m world record, US indoor title, World Indoor gold in 1:44.24 (youngest World Indoor champion ever), won Stockholm (1:42.70) and Oslo (1:42.08, world U20 record, over Emmanuel Wanyonyi), 2nd at the Prefontaine Classic behind Brandon Miller, US champion in 1:43.48 by 1.07 s; skipped the Ultimate Championship for his senior year.
