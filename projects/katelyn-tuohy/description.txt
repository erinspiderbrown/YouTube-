At 15, Katelyn Tuohy won Nike Cross Nationals by 40 seconds and her county gave her her own day. She broke national high school records in the 5000, the mile and the 3000, became the first 3-time NXN champion, then won 4 individual NCAA titles at NC State and signed with adidas. Then came the slide: injuries, 12th at the Olympic Trials, 19th at USAs. After the Boston 5K in April 2026, she didn't race again for months, and nobody explained why.

So what went wrong? Let me know in the comments: was it the pressure, the injuries, turning pro too early, or is she about to come back?
