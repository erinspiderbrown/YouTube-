- "I did Camel City… cuz it was local, close to home and… I could stay in my own bed and really take care of myself." (~1:55)
- "I got some answers, so I'm excited to kind of like keep working with my people and trying to figure things out… I think I'm in a good place." (~2:15)
- Q: running injuries or other stuff? "Uh, just other stuff, yeah. Um, you know, kind of keep that to myself." (~2:33) ← **key soundbite**

### Is "disappearance" accurate?
Partly. She has not "disappeared" in a literal or mysterious-person sense. She was still an adidas pro and was publicly named in the 31 Oct 2026 Dash field. But from **18 April to late October 2026 (about 6.5 months) she ran no races**. That covered the road 5K champs she said she would run, the whole outdoor track season and USATF Outdoors in her home state. It came after she publicly cited undisclosed "non-running" health issues, and **no statement explaining the gap was found**. Her results also show a steady decline from 2025 (19th at USAs, 16th at USATF XC, 15th at USATF Indoors 2026) plus at least two announced races she didn't appear in (Sir Walter 2025, Dash 2025). A safer framing is "vanished from racing" or "went quiet", not literal disappearance. Avoid implying anything sinister about the health issue. Confirm the Oct 31 result before publishing, because if she ran, the story ends with a "return".
