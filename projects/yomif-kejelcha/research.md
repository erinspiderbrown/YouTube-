### How the race unfolded
- Six men were clear by 5 km: Sawe, Kejelcha, Kiplimo, Tola, Kipruto, Geleta. 5 km 14:14 (2:00:03 pace). (WA-LDN, WIKI-LDN)
- 10 km 28:34, 15 km 43:10, **halfway 1:00:29** (target was 60:30). (WA-LDN, WIKI-LDN)
- Pacers shielded the leaders from the wind until about **25 km**, when Sawe started to push. (WIKI-LDN) Pacers are not named in any source we found. **[FLAG]**
- 30 km 1:26:03, projected about 2:01. (WA-LDN, WIKI-LDN)
- By ~29-30 km only Sawe and Kejelcha were left; Kiplimo had dropped (about 21 s back at that stage per WA). (WIKI-LDN, WA-LDN)
- **30-35 km in 13:54**: the move that put sub-2 on. Projection 2:00:29. (WA-LDN, WIKI-LDN)
- **35-40 km in 13:42**, both men still together. (WA-LDN)
- **Decisive break: about 41 km / "one mile to go"**, "after the 25th mile". Sawe went clear and ran 40 km to finish (2.195 km) in 5:51 alone. (WA-LDN, WIKI-LDN)
- Sawe's 2nd half: **59:01**. (WA-LDN)
- Kejelcha: "For 40km, I controlled the pace well, but after that, it was too hard. He was faster." (EURONEWS) "I felt I ran a relaxed and controlled race up until 41K, and then my legs were done." (LME) He ran "stride for stride" with Sawe for 41 of 42.2 km (LME).
- **[FLAG]** Marathon Handbook says Sawe broke away "at 35 km". That conflicts with WA/Wiki, which have the two together through 40 km. Go with WA: the break came around 41 km.
- **[FLAG]** The widely reported 4:12 24th-mile split for Sawe was likely a timing error; mika:timing removed it from the data on 1 May. Don't use it. (WIKI-LDN)

### The missed bottles (strong human beat)
- His team says he **missed two bottles**: one slipped at 25 km, and he didn't see the table at 35 km. That was 19 g of carbohydrate. He "ran out of energy at km 41". Right after the finish he said to his strategist: **"I'm sorry, Alfonso. I missed two bottles and lost the race."** (FEED) **[FLAG]** Single source, and the author is his own fuel/nutrition team (an Unusual Fuel sponsor angle). Attribute it: "according to his nutrition team".
- His team's view going in: "We knew that, among the contenders, we were probably the least efficient athlete for the distance." (FEED)

### Expectations before the race
- He predicted 2:02-2:03 and called a sub-2 debut "not possible" (NPR). "I thought I'd be more around 2:01 to 2:04, but my coaches believed in me" (LME). "I was ready to run in two hours and something" (EURONEWS).
- Build-up: the Feed says ~11-12 weeks of specific preparation from January; NPR says a six-month camp in Addis. **[FLAG]** Inconsistent; probably a 6-month base in Addis with a 12-week marathon block.
- Sawe had a back problem before the race (pre-race press conference) and did extra drug testing paid for by Adidas. (WIKI-LDN)

### Shoes

- Sawe: also Adidas, a new super shoe per a Runner's World headline cited on WIKI-LDN. **[FLAG]** model not confirmed.

- "Lost by 11 seconds" ≈ $175,000 less than the winner, before appearance fees.
