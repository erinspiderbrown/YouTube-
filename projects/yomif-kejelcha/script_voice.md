## Part 2
Yomif was born August first, nineteen ninety-seven, in Showa, in the Oromia region of Ethiopia, the fifth of nine kids. And the story go, when he quit school in the ninth grade to run, his daddy put him out the house. He went and joined a club outside Addis Ababa anyway, and by sixteen he was world youth champion in the three thousand. At seventeen he won the world under-twenty title in the five thousand, and the Youth Olympics too. And at eighteen he was world indoor champion against grown men. Then he did it again two years later. So dis kid was a winner. Dat part important.

## Part 3
But outdoors, on da big stage, somethin' kept happenin'. Fourth at Worlds in twenty fifteen, just off da podium. Fourth again in twenty seventeen. Then in twenty nineteen at Millrose, he went after Hicham El Guerrouj indoor mile world record, a record from nineteen ninety-seven, da year Yomif was born. He ran three forty-eight forty-six. Da record was three forty-eight forty-five. One hundredth of a second. Now he did come back a month later and smash it, three forty-seven oh one. But remember dat number. Point oh one. 'Cause dat's gon' be da story of his life.

## Part 4
Later dat same year at Worlds in Doha, da ten thousand. He went on da bell lap, took da lead, and ran wide da whole last bend while Joshua Cheptegei sat on da inside. Cheptegei ran him down in da last hunnid. Twenty-six forty-eight to twenty-six forty-nine. Silver, by under a second. In twenty twenty-three it got crazy. He lost one race to Mohamed Katir by three hundredths. He lost another one to Jakob Ingebrigtsen by one hundredth.
