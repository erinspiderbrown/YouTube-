Yomif Kejelcha ran 1:59:41 in his first ever marathon. Under two hours. The fastest marathon debut in history, the second fastest marathon ever run in a race, an Ethiopian record… and he still lost, by 11 seconds, to Sabastian Sawe's 1:59:30 world record. And it wasn't the first time. 0.01 off the mile world record. 0.98 behind Cheptegei in Doha. 0.06 behind Gressier in Tokyo. Is he a choke artist, or the unluckiest runner ever?

Facts: born August 1, 1997, Showa, Oromia, Ethiopia. World Youth 3000m champion (2013), World U20 5000m and Youth Olympic champion (2014), World Indoor 3000m champion (2016, 2018). 4th at Worlds 5000m (2015, 2017). Millrose 2019 mile 3:48.46 (WR was 3:48.45), then 3:47.01 indoor mile WR in Boston (March 3, 2019). Doha 2019 10,000m silver, 26:49.34 to Cheptegei's 26:48.36. 2023: 0.03 behind Katir (Florence 5000m), 0.01 behind Ingebrigtsen (Pre Classic 3000m). Paris 2024 10,000m 6th. Tokyo 2025 10,000m silver, 28:55.83 to Gressier's 28:55.77. Valencia Half 2024: 57:30 WR. London Marathon, April 26, 2026: Sawe 1:59:30 (WR), Kejelcha 1:59:41, Kiplimo 2:00:28; halfway 1:00:29, 30-35 km 13:54, 35-40 km 13:42. Buenos Aires Half, August 23, 2026: 56:51 WR. Withdrew injured from the Valencia Marathon (December 6, 2026). The missed-bottles detail comes from his nutrition team (The Feed).
