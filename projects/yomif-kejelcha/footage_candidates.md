## Facts to anchor the script (checked against sources found)
- **London, 26 Apr 2026 (his marathon debut):** Sabastian Sawe 1:59:30 (WR, first legal sub-2), Kejelcha 1:59:41 (2nd, the fastest debut ever, 11 s back), Jacob Kiplimo 2:00:28. Halfway was 60:29. Sawe's second half was 59:01 and 30K came in 1:26:03. Then they split 13:54 and 13:42 for the next two 5Ks, and the 30–40K section took 27:36. Kejelcha said his legs "stopped" at exactly **41 km** (missed a bottle at 25K, per his nutritionist Alfonso Beltrá). Both men wore the adidas Adizero Adios Pro Evo 3. There was a ~6 mph tailwind on the last 10K (LetsRun's Jonathan Gault).
- Before the race (Marathon Handbook, Friday): asked about 1:59, he said "No, I don't think so. For this marathon it's not possible."
- Team: coach **Gemedu** (captions write "Gamedu"; get the full name before it goes on screen) in Addis Ababa, in the group with Olympic champion **Tamirat Tola**. Advisor/nutritionist **Alfonso Beltrá** (Santamadre CEO, Madrid). Manager **Monica** (surname not confirmed). Sponsor **adidas**. Mileage went from 140–150 km/week on the track to ~200 km/week for the marathon.
- Childhood (CORREDOR interview): he grew up in a village at 2,500 m+ in Oromia, ran ~3 km to school every day and herded cows, horses and sheep. His **father was not happy** about running and wanted school. His **mother**, still in the village, pushed him. They had no TV, only radio. He supports Chelsea.
- **Near-misses:**
  - Millrose, 9 Feb 2019: 3:48.46, missing El Guerrouj's 3:48.45 indoor mile WR by 0.01 s.
  - Boston, 3 Mar 2019: 3:47.01 indoor mile WR. He was coached by Alberto Salazar (NOP) then. Salazar was later banned, so handle that carefully or leave it out.
  - Doha 2019 10,000m: silver, 26:49.34 to Cheptegei's 26:48.36.
  - Tokyo 2025 10,000m: silver, outkicked by **Jimmy Gressier** on the line. It was his last track race. He said his coach told him to kick at 200 m but he went at 300.
  - Also 2x world indoor 3000m champion (2016, 2018).
- **Roads:**
  - Valencia half, 27 Oct 2024: 57:30 WR.
  - 10K road WR 26:31 (Castellón 2025, ratified 1 Jun 2026).
  - Buenos Aires half, 23 Aug 2026: **56:51** WR (some outlets say 56:50; use the ratified figure).
  - **Next: Valencia Marathon, 6 Dec 2026** (Valencia announced it in May). This gives the video a natural open ending.
