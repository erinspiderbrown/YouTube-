## 1. The fall
June 24, 2024. Hayward Field. The Olympic Trials final.

She's the defending Olympic champion. She holds the American record. And she just ran the fastest time in the semis.

200 meters in... she goes down.

[SOUNDBITE: fan video from the stands, the crowd gasps]

She gets up. She finishes. Last place. 22 seconds behind the winner.

3 years earlier she was the best 800 meter runner on the planet.

And since August 1, 2025? She ain't raced one time.

So what happened to Athing Mu? And where is she right now? Stay to the end, 'cause the answer ain't what you think.

## 2. Trenton
Athing Mu grew up in Trenton, New Jersey.

Her family fled a war in what's now South Sudan. She's number 6 of 7 kids. The first one born in America.

Her big brothers ran for the Trenton Track Club. So at 6 years old, she's out there with 'em.

And here go the crazy part. She never ran for her high school team. Not one race. Just the club.

Her coach, Al Jennings, coached in Trenton for about 40 years. He said she had more talent than any athlete he ever coached.

## 3. 16 with braces
February 24, 2019. The USA Indoor Championships in New York. Grown pros.

Athing Mu is 16. A high school junior. Braces on her teeth.

She runs the 600 in 1:23.57. American record.

[SOUNDBITE / GRAPHIC: LetsRun headline "16-Year-Old Athing Mu Has Ribbons in Her Hair, Braces in Her Mouth… and an American Record"]

And one of the pros she beat that day was Raevyn Rogers. Remember that name. 'Cause 5 years later, it's gon' matter.

## 4. The rise
2021. She goes to Texas A&M. For 1 season.

Collegiate record in the 800. NCAA champion in the 400. 49.57.

Then she turns pro. Signs with Nike. Gives up 3 years of college.

June 2021, the Olympic Trials. She wins in 1:56.07.

[SOUNDBITE: Trials/Tokyo race call, 8 seconds max if NBC]

August 3, 2021. Tokyo. The Olympic final.

She takes the lead from the gun... and nobody ever touches her.

1:55.21. American record. Gold.

She's 19 years old. The first American woman to win the Olympic 800 since 1968.

Then she anchors the 4x400. Another gold.

[SOUNDBITE: her family in Trenton watching / the Trenton parade]

Trenton throws her a parade. They light up the bridge red, white and blue.

## 5. The cracks
2022. World Championships. Eugene, Oregon.

She holds off Keely Hodgkinson by 0.08 seconds. World champion.

[SOUNDBITE: fan video, last lap in Eugene]

That made her the youngest woman ever to hold Olympic and world titles in an individual track event.

She's 20. Undefeated in over 20 straight races as a pro.

But here go what nobody knew. She said later she ran that whole year with a messed-up hip.

And 3 months later, she makes a move that people still argue about. She leaves her college coach. And she moves to LA to train with Bobby Kersee. A legend. But mostly known for sprinters and hurdlers.

## 6. The doubt
March 2023. She goes on a podcast and says something you don't hear from a world champion.

[SOUNDBITE: The Pivot, "There's probably three moments where I really was like, I don't even know if I want to do this"]

3 times. She thought about quitting 3 times.

That summer she runs one 800 before Worlds. One. Her coach told the LA Times she might not even go.

She deletes social media. She goes to Budapest anyway.

And for the first time as a pro... she loses. Bronze.

[SOUNDBITE: CITIUS, Budapest, "people can just take a chill pill sometimes… let us be athletes"]

But 3 weeks later, she shuts everybody up. 1:54.97. A new American record. Beats Keely.

So she still had it. Which makes what happened next even harder to understand.

## 7. The Trials
2024. A few weeks before the Olympic Trials, she tears her hamstring.

Sports Illustrated reported she was on crutches. Doing workouts in the pool. Cleared to run about a week before Eugene.

She makes the final anyway. Fastest time in the semis.

Then the final. She's cutting in on the backstretch... and her foot clips the calf of the runner in front of her.

Raevyn Rogers. The same woman she beat at 16.

Rogers stays up. Mu goes down.

[SOUNDBITE: fan video from the stands, the fall]

She finishes last. 2:19.69.

Her team files a protest. Denied. They appeal. Denied.

And 6 weeks later in Paris, Keely Hodgkinson wins the Olympic gold. The gold Athing Mu came to defend.

[SOUNDBITE: Keely, "I was really gutted to see her fall. I want to beat people fair and square."]

And she doesn't really talk about the fall in public. Not for almost 5 months.

## 8. The comeback
March 2025. She marries Yegor Nikolayev. A Russian runner who ran the 1500 at the 2012 Olympics.

And she tries to come back.

Her first race? A 5000. She steps off after about 3K. That was the plan.

Then a 1500. 4:10. Second in her heat.

Then the Prefontaine Classic. Her first big 800 since the fall.

Last place. 2:03.44.

[SOUNDBITE: CITIUS, Pre 2025, "There's been a little bit of turmoil of just my relationship with track"]

August 1, 2025. The US Championships. Semifinal.

1:59.79. She doesn't make the final.

[SOUNDBITE: CITIUS, USAs 2025, "if you're not mentally in it, then your body won't be in it"]

That's the last race Athing Mu has run.

## 9. The silence
September 2025, she tells Bobby Kersee she's leaving.

She and her husband move to Dallas. Kersee called it "a life decision." No hard feelings, he said.

She never explained it herself.

2026 comes. No races. Nike says she's still with Nike. A source told LetsRun she's working with a new coach. Nobody knows who.

People started making videos. "Where is Athing Mu?"

## 10. The answer
October 15, 2026.

She posts on Instagram.

She's pregnant. Her and Yegor's first baby.

"Can't wait to meet our little Sunshine."

So that's where she went. She's 24. About to be a mom. Living in Dallas.

And she still holds the American record in the 800.

## 11. The end
So what really went wrong?

The hip? The hamstring? The coach? The fall? Or did she just stop loving it a long time ago?

She told us herself. 3 times she thought about quitting. And she still won an Olympic gold, a world title, and broke the American record.

LA 2028 is less than 2 years away. She ain't said if she's coming back.

Do y'all think we see Athing Mu on a starting line again? Tell me in the comments.
