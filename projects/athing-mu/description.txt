At 19, Athing Mu won Olympic gold in the 800m in an American record. At 20 she was world champion. Then came the doubt, a coaching change, a torn hamstring and the fall at the 2024 Olympic Trials. She hasn't raced since August 1, 2025. This is what happened, in her own words, and where she is now.
