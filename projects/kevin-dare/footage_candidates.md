# Kevin Dare: YouTube footage candidates

Research date: 27 Nov 2026 (vidIQ YouTube search + transcripts, plus web search). Nothing downloaded.

## Fact check from this search
- **Hometown: State College, Pennsylvania** (not Rockford, Illinois). He went to State College Area High School and won the 2000 PIAA state pole vault title (ESPN, Penn State Athletics, StateCollege.com).
- He won the pole vault at the 2001 USATF Junior Nationals (ESPN 2002 report says "last June").
- Died 23 Feb 2002 at the Big Ten Indoor Championships, University of Minnesota Field House, Minneapolis. He was attempting 15-7 (well below his PR) and landed head-first in the metal plant box. The rest of the meet (day 2) was cancelled.
- Aftermath: father **Ed Dare** and Penn State AD **Tim Curley** pushed for pit and box padding and helmets. The Dare family backed the first pole vault helmet (ASTM F2400 helmet spec followed) and the first "Softbox". A 2003 AP report (Vindicator archive) says the first soft boxes were produced that November and **Penn State was the first customer**. The Big Ten extended landing pads and made box collars mandatory. The Kevin Dare Foundation started in 2004. Penn State holds the Kevin Dare (HS) Invitational every January, and the team gives a "Kevin Dare Most Inspirational Award".
- Jan Johnson (USATF pole vault safety, 1972 Olympic bronze) was skeptical of helmets (neck-flexion worry); Ed Dare disputed that. Good tension for the safety-debate chapter.
