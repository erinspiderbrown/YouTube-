# Kevin Dare: research (27 Nov 2026)
Sources: Penn State release 23 Feb 2002 (gopsusports.com), Indiana Daily Student 25 Feb 2002, Daily Cardinal 2002, Daily Pennsylvanian 25 Apr 2002, NCAA News 13 May 2002, Minnesota Daily 20 Feb 2003, GoPSU "Life Back on Track" 27 Feb 2012, StateCollege.com (Bill Horlacher) 23 Feb 2017.

## Who
- Kevin James Dare, 19, Penn State sophomore pole vaulter, from State College, PA. State College Area High ("State High"); also played football.
- Family: father Ed, mother Terri, brother Eric (14 months older; javelin, football DB). Roommate/closest friend at PSU: Dave Bollinger.
- 2000 PIAA state champion (14-9, won a jump-off on a cold rainy day).
- 2001 US Junior champion, 16-6 3/4 (June 2001); won Team USA vs Great Britain; Pan Am Junior Championships, Argentina (fall 2001).
- PSU: 5th at 2001 IC4A (team won the title, coach Harry Groves). 2002 season best 16-4 3/4 (Jan 12, Ashenfelter Indoor Track). Cleared 17 ft in practice (DP).

## The accident
- Sat Feb 23, 2002, Big Ten Men's Indoor Championships, University of Minnesota Field House, Minneapolis, ~2:40 pm CT (PSU).
- Bar at 15-7, his first jump at that height (StateCollege). Had moved up to a 16-ft pole from 15-ft (DP).
- Minnesota asst Mario Sategna: he "swung upside down and sort of stalled", "I believe he thought he had cleared the bar".
- Came down headfirst on the metal box (IDS, DP, StateCollege, MN Daily). (PSU release: "hit his head on the ground"; NCAA News: "missing the landing pad".)
- Parents in the stands; Bollinger closest. Eric in State College, told by phone by Ed.
- EMTs on site; Hennepin County Medical Center; pronounced dead, massive head trauma.

## The meet
- Pole vault cancelled. Sat night coaches (incl. Groves) voted to continue; athletes could withdraw; moment of silence before 3000m/DMR.
- Sunday: commissioner Jim Delany cancelled the rest ("I think everyone involved was given time to sleep on it, and things changed"). Not rescheduled. Athletes gathered around the track while Groves spoke.
- Tim Curley (PSU AD): "Kevin was an outstanding, personable young man... It's just a terrible tragedy and our hearts go out to the Dare family."
- Groves: "Kevin was a great athlete, and he had a great future in front of him." "Our team is devastated losing one of their best friends."

## Aftermath / safety
- 2002 also: HS vaulters Jesus Quesada (Clewiston, FL) and Samoa Fili (Wichita, KS) died. DP: 20 deaths since 1980.
- Ed Dare (DP): "Had I known what I know right now, Kevin would not be jumping." Safety "tremendously outdated" (NYT via StateCollege). Founded Vault for Life.
- Eric (DP): "We always knew it was a dangerous sport... not dangerous if we just improve some things."
- Ed Dare + Tim Curley hosted a 2-day pole vault safety meeting in State College, May 7-8, 2002.
- Big Ten, Aug 2002: longer landing pad, mandatory box collars (MN Daily). NFHS deeper pads. Feb 2003 Gill "soft box" (cuts impact ~2/3). Kevlar vault helmet in development with Ed Dare/ASTM (2003).
- Jan Johnson (USATF safety chair): "I don't know that Kevin Dare's accident was preventable" / blamed technique and coaching.

## Legacy
- Kevin Dare Foundation (2004); Kevin Dare Memorial Fund Scholarship + Life Back on Track Scholarship (HS athletes with debilitating illness); Heart of a Lion award at the Kevin Dare Invitational (Penn State, January). Endowed PSU scholarship.
- Eric married Caitlin; 3 kids, a son named after Kevin. 2017: rates vault safety "probably a 6" out of 10, wants helmets. Bollinger: "He was a natural leader."
