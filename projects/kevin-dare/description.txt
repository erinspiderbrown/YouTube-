Kevin Dare was 19, a Penn State pole vaulter and the 2001 US junior national champion. On February 23, 2002, at the Big Ten Indoor Championships in Minneapolis, with his parents in the stands, he went up for a jump at 15-7 and never came back down the way he was supposed to. This is who Kevin was, what happened that day, and how his family changed the pole vault forever.

Rest easy, Kevin.

Facts: Kevin James Dare, from State College, PA (State College Area High School). 2000 PIAA state pole vault champion (jump-off); 2001 US junior champion at 16-6¾; competed for the US vs Great Britain and at the Pan American Junior Championships in Argentina. Penn State: 5th at the 2001 IC4A Championships (team title). February 23, 2002, Big Ten Indoor Championships, University of Minnesota Field House: on his first attempt at 15-7 he stalled and came down head-first on the metal plant box; he died at Hennepin County Medical Center. The pole vault was cancelled that day and the rest of the meet the next morning. In 2002, high school vaulters Jesus Quesada (Florida) and Samoa Fili (Kansas) also died. Ed and Terri Dare and Penn State AD Tim Curley hosted a pole vault safety meeting in May 2002; the Big Ten lengthened landing pits and required box collars that August; high school rules followed; the soft box came in 2003. The Kevin Dare Foundation funds scholarships, and Penn State hosts the Kevin Dare Invitational. Sources: Penn State Athletics, NPR (April 2002), Indiana Daily Student, Daily Pennsylvanian, NCAA News, Minnesota Daily, StateCollege.com (2017).
