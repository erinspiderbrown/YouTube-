Shelby Houlihan was the fastest American woman ever in the 1500 and the 5000. Then in June 2021 she was banned for 4 years after testing positive for nandrolone, and she said it came from a pork burrito bought at a food truck. The Court of Arbitration for Sport said her explanation was possible but unlikely and upheld the ban. She has always said she never doped. She came back in 2025 with a World Indoor silver, a US title and 4th at the World Championships. So do y'all believe the burrito?
