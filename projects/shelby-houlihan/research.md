# Shelby Houlihan research (20 Nov 2026)
- Born 8 Feb 1993, Iowa. Arizona State; NCAA 1500 champion 2014 (Wikipedia mirror; confirm with ASU if needed). Bowerman Track Club, coach Jerry Schumacher, from 2015.
- Rio 2016: 11th, Olympic 5000 final (Arizona Republic via Wikipedia).
- 2018: 5000 American record 14:34.45, Heusden (T&FN, Running Magazine).
- Doha 2019 Worlds: 1500 4th, 3:54.99 American record.
- 10 Jul 2020: 14:34.45 -> 14:23.92 at a Bowerman time trial, Jesuit HS, Portland (LetsRun, Diamond League site, USATF).
- Test: Dec 2020 (USATF list 15 Dec; some reports 14 Dec), nandrolone. Ban 4 years from 14 Jan 2021; announced by her on Instagram 14 Jun 2021, days before Olympic Trials.
- Explanation: pork burrito (ordered carne asada) from a food truck near her Beaverton home ~10 h before the test; boar offal. CAS: she was credible but explanation "possible but unlikely"; source not established (SI, CBC, LetsRun, NPR).
- USATF let her enter Trials pending appeal; World Athletics/AIU said no; name removed from start list (ESPN). Swiss Federal Tribunal appeal failed (2022, infobae/Around the Rings).
- Lost Nike support; teammate Gabriela DeBues-Stafford left BTC because the club stayed with Houlihan.
- Return: eligible 13/14 Jan 2025. Razorback Invitational 3000 8:31.56 (meet + facility record). World Indoors Nanjing 22 Mar 2025: 3000 silver 8:38.26 (Hailu 8:37.21, Hull 8:38.28). 2025 US 5000 champion. Tokyo Worlds 20 Sep 2025: 5000 4th 14:57.42, led at halfway (Chebet 14:54.36, Kipyegon 14:55.07, Battocletti 14:55.42). Called it her "revenge tour".
- 2026: The TEN (Mar) 10,000 debut win 30:50.10, #10 all-time US. USATF outdoor 5000 2nd 14:57.88 (Emily Mackay 14:55.82). Skechers signed her Sept 2026 (first shoe deal since the ban, ~2 years after return), age 33, targeting 2027 Worlds Beijing.
Sources: Wikipedia; SI 15 Jun 2021; NPR; CBC; ESPN trials start list; LetsRun 2021/2022/2025/2026; World Athletics Nanjing and Tokyo reports; CBS/ESPN/Fox 2025; Skechers press release; Canadian Running Sept 2026.
