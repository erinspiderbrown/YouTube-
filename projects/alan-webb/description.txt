May 27, 2001. An 18-year-old high school senior from Reston, Virginia lined up against pros at the Prefontaine Classic and ran 3:53.43, breaking Jim Ryun's 36-year-old high school mile record. 25 years later, nobody has touched it. Alan Webb was supposed to save American miling. He won the 2004 Olympic Trials, ran 3:46.91 for the American mile record in 2007, and still never won a single Olympic or world medal.

So what went wrong? Let me know in the comments: was it the hype, the training, or did he just peak in high school?
