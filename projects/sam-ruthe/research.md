## Bio basics

- **Born 12 April 2009** [V2]. Sources: World Athletics profile https://worldathletics.org/athletes/new-zealand/sam-ruthe-15131581 ; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ruthe ; Athletics NZ https://athletics.org.nz/sam-ruthe-rewrites-history-youngest-ever-to-run-sub-4-mile/
  - Age now (Nov 2026): **17**.
- **Hometown: Tauranga, Bay of Plenty** [V2]. Sources: Athletics NZ (above) ; NZ Herald https://www.nzherald.co.nz/nz/world-record-breaking-tauranga-runner-sam-ruthe-full-of-potential-says-coach/HXZ6LEVHNNER3GEYC6HHSCJE74/
- **School: Tauranga Boys' College.** He was in Year 11 in Dec 2025.
  - He is leaving at the end of 2026 and skipping his final year to go full-time [V2].
  - Sources: NZ Herald (27 Dec 2025) https://www.nzherald.co.nz/bay-of-plenty-times/news/from-school-races-to-a-nike-deal-how-sam-ruthe-became-a-world-record-running-teen/NJSZIEQ7YFAWFF5T44Z5F6NCPU/ ; 1News (28 May 2026) https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/05/28/sam-ruthe-closing-the-books-on-school-to-become-full-time-athlete/ ; the TBC principal is quoted in the NZ Herald 20 Mar 2025 piece above.
- **Primary school:** Pillans Point School. His first competitive race was at age 5, in his first three weeks there, and other kids beat him [V1]. Source: NZ Herald, 27 Dec 2025.
- **Birth:** He had infant respiratory distress syndrome and was "on life support for his first five days", then on oxygen therapy [V1].
  - Source: NZ Herald via Wikipedia, https://www.nzherald.co.nz/sport/olympics/athletics-running-on-after-a-hellish-year/77WRIIOA5SAONGDHEWQ37OJ4PI/ (not opened directly).
  - Strong cold-open or story material. Confirm the wording before using it.
- **Family (all are public athletes, named in the press):**
  - **Father Ben Ruthe:** described as a "former national record holder over 800m and 1000m" [V2: NZ Herald 20 Mar 2025 + 27 Dec 2025].
    - He is the one quoted most in the press and handles race planning.
    - Whether those were open or age-group records is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
  - **Mother Jess Ruthe:** held national cross-country titles and was a top runner "from the 1500m up" [V2: NZ Herald + Stuff].
  - **Younger sister Daisy Ruthe:** also a runner. She ran a 2:49.34 1000m PB (5th) at the same Tauranga Twilight meet on 3 Jan 2026 [V1: Athletics NZ https://athletics.org.nz/weekly-roundup-5-january-2026/].
    - The parents held both kids back from serious training until age 13 [V2: NZ Herald 27 Dec 2025 (Ben quote) + Tauranga Athletics president, NZ Herald 20 Mar 2025].
  - **Grandmother Rosemary Wright (née Stirling):**
    - Won **800m gold at the 1970 Commonwealth Games in Edinburgh, running for Scotland** [V2: RNZ https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/585655/sam-ruthe-s-next-goal-after-setting-record-for-mile ; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemary_Wright].
    - Born in Timaru, NZ, in 1947. Came 7th in the 800m at the 1972 Munich Olympics.
    - Moved to NZ with her husband in the early 1980s.
    - **Story beat:** the 2026 Commonwealth Games were in Glasgow, Scotland. Ben said "Sam would love to do that for his nan as well". Sam then had to pull out injured.
  - **Grandfather Trevor Wright:** Olympic marathoner (per Wikipedia / Stuff / Athletics Weekly). Which country he ran for and the details of his career are **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Coach: Craig Kirkwood** [V2: RNZ, NZ Herald, Athletics NZ selection list].
  - Tauranga-based; also coaches two-time Olympian **Sam Tanner**, Sam's training partner.
  - Kirkwood raced against Sam's parents "back in the day" and watched Sam grow up.
  - He took Sam into his junior squad in Year 9 (about early 2023) (NZ Herald, 20 Mar 2025).
  - T&FN says Kirkwood was 13th at the 2001 Chicago Marathon [V1]: https://trackandfieldnews.com/records-not-phenom-ruthes-only-summer-vacation-take-away/
  - Ben on Kirkwood: "the best coach in the world one kilometer away" (same T&FN piece).
- **How he started:** Ben says Sam was "running laps of the tent at Ohope Beach when he wasn't even 2". There was no structured training until 13, partly to protect his relaxed "float" style (NZ Herald, 27 Dec 2025).
- **Other sports:** He played **football (soccer)**. A lateral-movement football injury caused his April 2026 stress fracture [V2: RNZ 13 Apr 2026 https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/592174/athletics-sam-ruthe-off-radar-after-suffering-leg-fracture ; Marathon Handbook ; RNZ 23 May 2026].
  - In rehab he set PBs in 200m and 400m swimming and beat his 20-minute bike power (RNZ, 23 May 2026).
- **Training load:** Kirkwood keeps it age-appropriate. About 60-80 km a week per a podcast summary [V1: https://johngetstrong.substack.com/p/how-16-year-old-sam-ruthe-ran-a-348].
  - Sam's longest run was 17 km as of Feb 2026 [V1: Athletics Illustrated https://athleticsillustrated.com/the-sam-ruthe-interview-learning-the-sport-loving-the-process-and-running-a-348-88-mile-at-age-16/].
  - Coach line: "He trains like a 16-year-old, not like a fully professional" (quoted in The Conversation, coach unnamed).
- **Sponsor: Nike.** He signed in **April 2025**. He posted on Instagram that it was an "honour to be part of such a legendary brand" [V2: NZ Herald 27 Dec 2025 ; The Conversation https://theconversation.com/how-far-can-teenage-kiwi-running-star-sam-ruthe-go-what-science-and-history-tell-us-275214 ; LetsRun results list him as "Nike"]. Terms were not disclosed.
  - Suunto has an athlete page for him (https://us.suunto.com/pages/athletes/sam-ruthe). Whether that is a paid deal is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **College:** In Feb 2026 he said "I really like the NCAA and the education, opportunities and support it provides... I would start in the autumn of 2028" [V1: Athletics Illustrated, 5 Feb 2026].
  - By May 2026 the plan had shifted to leaving school and basing himself in Europe in 2027 (1News, 28 May 2026).
  - Whether NCAA is still on the table is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
  - Taking a pro Nike deal at 16 would normally rule out NCAA eligibility. Check whether the deal is structured as NIL-safe.

## Timeline

### 2024 (age 15)
- **Nov 2024, Auckland: 3000m in 8:09.68.**
  - It was a world age-15 best plus NZ U17/U18 records [V2: Wikipedia ; NZ Herald 20 Mar 2025 says "broke the 15-year-old world best for 3000m in November"].
  - Stuff: https://www.stuff.co.nz/sport/360496179/auckland-runner-sam-ruthe-15-sets-worlds-best-3000m-time-his-age-group (body not readable).
  - He lowered it twice more that summer.

### 2025 (age 15-16)
- **Jan 2025, Cooks Classic, Whanganui: mile in 4:01.72**, his first big mile [V2: Wikipedia ; CBS says his previous best was 4:01.72].
- **1 Feb 2025, Potts Classic / NZ 3000m Championships, Hastings: won the senior national 3000m title in 7:56.18.**
  - It was a world age-15 best.
  - He became the **youngest NZ senior national champion in any event**; the next-youngest was 21 [V2: NZ Herald 27 Dec 2025 ; Wikipedia ; Rekortan].
- **9 Feb 2025, Sir Graeme Douglas International, Auckland: 1500m in 3:41.25.**
  - This beat **Jakob Ingebrigtsen's world age-15 best of 3:42.44** (Bislett 2016) [V2: Athletics Weekly https://athleticsweekly.com/news/sam-ruthe-improves-jakob-ingebrigtsens-world-age-15-best-over-1500m-1039998685/ ; Wikipedia].
  - Ruthe: "I wasn't exactly targeting that."
- **9 Mar 2025, NZ Track & Field Championships, Caledonian Ground, Dunedin: 1500m DEAD HEAT with Olympian Sam Tanner, both 3:44.31.**
  - The title was shared; it was Tanner's 5th national title and Ruthe's first.
  - Ruthe kicked in the straight and dipped at the line. Officials deliberated for several minutes [V2: NZ Herald https://www.nzherald.co.nz/sport/sam-tanner-15-year-old-sam-ruthe-tie-in-dramatic-1500m-final/H3LL5X6CJND7HKFPAUPX3UDW5I/ ; ODT https://www.odt.co.nz/sport/other-sport/teen-and-olympian-dead-heat-championships ; Scoop/ANZ https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/CU2503/S00120.htm].
- **19 Mar 2025, ACA Mile Night, Go Media (Mt Smart) Stadium, Auckland: 3:58.35, the YOUNGEST SUB-4 MILE EVER** (aged 15 years 341 days; first 15-year-old ever) [V2: Athletics NZ ; Stuff ; CBS/Reuters ; Wikipedia].
  - Pacing: Ethan Smolej led through 800m in about 2:00. **Sam Tanner** then paced, moved to lane 2 in the home straight and ran side by side with him for the last 80m.
  - **Official result: Tanner 1st in 3:58.29, Ruthe 2nd in 3:58.35** [V1 on the placings: NZ Herald via search snippet https://www.nzherald.co.nz/bay-of-plenty-times/news/kiwi-runner-sam-ruthe-becomes-youngest-ever-to-break-four-minute-mile/HUO2I2HZUFCNRFJOXNITV5OUYE].
  - Ben Wall ran 3:59.00. Ruthe became the 49th New Zealander under 4 minutes and Wall the 50th.
  - Rain fell through the afternoon and stopped around 8pm. Over 800 people were in the stadium and **44,000+ watched the YouTube livestream** (Athletics NZ).
  - Records: beat Ingebrigtsen (3:58.07 at 16y 250d, Pre 2017) and Cam Myers (3:55.44 at 16y 259d, Feb 2023) as the youngest sub-4 miler. It also broke Tanner's NZ U19/U20 mile record of 3:58.41.
  - Note: Athletics NZ gives his age as 15y 11m 6d; the date maths gives 15y 11m 7d. Use "15" or "25 days before his 16th birthday".
- **29 Mar 2025, Maurie Plant Meet, Melbourne, John Landy Mile (run as 1500m): 3:40.12 PB, 7th.**
  - Cam Myers won in 3:34.98 [V2: RNZ https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/556593/teen-prodigy-sam-ruthe-sets-another-world-age-best-at-melbourne ; Wikipedia].
  - RNZ calls it another world age best.
- **Apr 2025: signed with Nike** (see above).
- **5 Jul 2025, Prefontaine Classic, Hayward Field, Eugene: 13th in 4:00.65** in the non-Diamond League "Promotional" mile, won by Ethan Strand in 3:48.86 [V2: World Athletics results https://worldathletics.org/competitions/diamond-league/calendar-results/7203941/result ; T&FN].
  - Marathon Handbook wrongly calls it the Bowerman Mile.
  - **Footage is Diamond League/NBC, so BANNED.**
- **12 Jul 2025, Sound Running Sunset Tour, Occidental College, Los Angeles: 1500m in 3:39.17, 5th.**
  - His first sub-3:40. It set NZ U17/U18 records, 0.4s off Tanner's U19/U20 marks [V2: 1News https://www.1news.co.nz/2025/07/13/kiwi-teen-athlete-sam-ruthe-breaks-two-more-nz-records-in-the-us/ ; Wikipedia].
  - Some sources typo it as 3:49.17.
- **6 Dec 2025, NZ Secondary Schools Champs, Hastings: 1500m in 3:38.62**, in a SEMI-FINAL.
  - It broke Richard Potts' 1989 record by about 8 seconds [V2: 1News https://www.1news.co.nz/2025/12/06/sam-ruthe-shatters-nz-schoolboy-1500m-record/ ; RNZ https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/581074/running-prodigy-sam-ruthe-shatters-schoolboy-1500m-record ; NZ Herald].
- **7 Dec 2025, same champs: 800m final in 1:46.81**, a schools record [V2: Rekortan ; NZ Herald].
- **20 Dec 2025, Night of 5s, AUT Millennium, Auckland: 5000m debut in 13:40.48**, an NZ U20 record.
  - Sam Tanner beat triathlete Hayden Wilde and Ruthe [V2: Rekortan https://www.rekortan.com/news/sam-ruthes-remarkable-summer ; Stuff via Wikipedia https://www.stuff.co.nz/sport/360916842/sam-tanner-beats-hayden-wilde-and-teen-sensation-sam-ruthe-5000m-showdown].

### 2026 (age 16-17)
- **3 Jan 2026, Tauranga Twilight, Tauranga Domain (home track): 1000m in 2:17.82, 1st.**
  - NZ U19/U20 records; the old U20 record was 2:22.0. Described as a "world's best for a 16- and 17-year-old".
  - It came close to Snell's 2:16.6 and Walker's NZ record of 2:16.57 [V2: Athletics NZ https://athletics.org.nz/weekly-roundup-5-january-2026/ ; Rekortan].
  - Wikipedia says the record paperwork was not forwarded, so it is not ratified.
- **17 Jan 2026, Potts Classic, Hastings: 800m WIN in 1:45.86 PB over Sam Tanner (1:45.94).**
  - NZ junior record; now 7th on the NZ all-time list [V2: NZ Herald https://www.nzherald.co.nz/bay-of-plenty-times/sport/running-prodigy-sam-ruthe-edges-sam-tanner-in-recordbreaking-800m/UJSSKWM7BNBO7G75AJ5FO2WPQI/ ; Rekortan ; WA report].
- **24 Jan 2026, Cooks Classic (NZ Mile Champs), Cooks Gardens, Whanganui: 3:53.83, 2nd.**
  - He led until the final 120m, then Tanner (3:53.36) passed him.
  - **Fastest mile ever by a 16-year-old**, beating Cam Myers' 3:55.44. He moved past Peter Snell's 3:54.04 to 7th on the NZ all-time list [V2: Athletics NZ https://athletics.org.nz/weekly-roundup-27-january-2026/ ; RNZ https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/584901/teen-athletics-star-sam-ruthe-smashes-mile-world-record-for-his-age ; WA report].
- **31 Jan 2026, John Thomas Terrier Classic, Boston University (indoor): MILE in 3:48.88, WON. THE BIG ONE** [V2: World Athletics https://worldathletics.org/news/report/sam-ruthe-mile-boffey-800-boston-university ; NZ Herald https://www.nzherald.co.nz/bay-of-plenty-times/news/sam-ruthe-breaks-nz-mile-record-with-sensational-run-in-boston/DMUJVYRQOZBTXNYN47WG6HI7KQ ; LetsRun ; RNZ].
  - **World U18 best** for both the mile and the 1500m split (**3:33.25**). 3rd-fastest U20 ever.
  - 11th on the all-time indoor list, 0.01 behind Josh Kerr.
  - **Broke Sir John Walker's outright NZ mile record of 3:49.08 (Oslo 1982), which had stood 44 years**, and Nick Willis' indoor NR of 3:51.06 (2016).
  - Race: Pieter Sisk (BEL, 2024 Olympian) was 2nd in 3:50.31 (Belgian record) and Davis Bove 3rd in 3:51.08.
  - Ruthe sat behind Sisk, took the lead at or after the bell and **closed in a 28.38 last lap**.
  - Tanner was meant to help but dropped out with a tight leg. Ruthe was 3 years younger than anyone else in the race.
  - He had flown in 3 days earlier after about 50 hours of travel.
  - It met the Commonwealth Games A standard.
- **14 Feb 2026, ASICS Sound Invite, JDL Fast Track, Winston-Salem NC (indoor): mile in 3:52.46, 7th.**
  - **Cole Hocker** won in 3:45.94, an American indoor record and #2 indoor mile ever. Cooper Teare was 2nd [V2: CITIUS https://citiusmag.com/articles/sam-ruthe-352-mile-learning-experience-cole-hocker ; NZ Herald ; LetsRun results].
  - He got boxed at the back and ran in lane 2. This is the "loss / learning" beat (see Quotes).
  - One LetsRun recap says 3:52.16 **[UNVERIFIED; 3:52.46 has 2+ sources]**.
- **22 Feb 2026, Saucony Battle for Boston (BU, indoor): 3000m in 7:43.16, 2nd.**
  - A 13-second PB, behind Arkansas' Ernest Cheruiyot (7:40.53). WA lists it as an Area U18 record and NZ U18/U20 records [V2: WA profile ; LetsRun ; Rekortan].
- **7 Mar 2026, NZ Track & Field Champs ("Track Stars"), Douglas Track & Field, Auckland: 1500m WIN in 3:41.43**, leading from start to finish and winning by 6+ seconds.
  - It was his 2nd NZ senior 1500m title. Tanner was out with an Achilles injury [V2: Rekortan ; Wikipedia ; Stuff ; Athletics NZ YouTube].
  - He saluted the crowd on the way in (Stuff headline).
- **30 Mar 2026: named in his first NZ team** for the World U20 Champs in Eugene (1500m, coach Kirkwood) [V2: RNZ https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/591085/teen-running-star-sam-ruthe-selected-for-first-new-zealand-team ; Athletics NZ https://athletics.org.nz/12-kiwis-named-in-initial-selection-for-2026-world-athletics-under-20-championships/].
- **He had also been announced for the Bowerman Mile, Pre Classic, 4 July 2026**, against Hocker and Myers [V2: Marathon Handbook https://marathonhandbook.com/sam-ruthe-bowerman-mile/ ; Bring Back the Mile ; Rekortan]. He did not run it because of injury.
- **April 2026: STRESS FRACTURE of the fibula (lower leg) from playing football.**
  - Announced about 13 Apr 2026 with an Instagram post saying he'd be "off the radar for a while" [V2: RNZ 13 Apr ; NZ Herald ; 1News].
  - Rehab was biking and swimming. "In the last three days I've probably biked a little over nine hours" (1News).
- **~mid-May 2026: second scare.** He woke with a sore foot. An MRI showed no second fracture, and Ben said he got the all-clear [V2: RNZ 23 May https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/sport/596098/sam-ruthe-gives-up-on-commonwealth-games-focus-moves-to-u20-champs ; NZ Herald].
- **23 May 2026: withdrew from Commonwealth Games consideration** (Glasgow, 23 Jul-2 Aug) to target the World U20s [V2: RNZ ; NZ Herald https://www.nzherald.co.nz/sport/athletics/sam-ruthe-withdraws-from-commonwealth-games-consideration-due-to-stress-fracture/4F5F3JIYJ5DTROMXJIKIJJ63A4/ ; 1News https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/05/23/still-a-big-season-ahead-sam-ruthe-passes-up-on-comm-games/ ; Stuff].
- **28 May 2026: announced he is leaving school** (Tauranga Boys') at the end of 2026 to be a full-time athlete, with a Europe base planned for 2027.
  - Partly triggered by World Athletics' tougher 2027 Worlds entry standards and ranking-points system (1News).

  - Also withdrew from the World Road Running team (September).
  - Athletics NZ: "Sam is prioritising his long-term development and is excited to continue building a strong winter foundation", with a "return-to-performance programme" aimed at the NZ summer [V1 on the exact date: Marathon Handbook (22 Jun 2026) citing RNZ https://marathonhandbook.com/teen-mile-star-sam-ruthe-pulls-out-of-world-u20-championships/ ; also listed in search results].
  - Find the RNZ original to confirm the date.

## Setbacks / criticism

- **Fibula stress fracture, April 2026** (football). It took out his whole northern summer: Pre Bowerman Mile (4 Jul), Commonwealth Games in Glasgow (his grandmother's Scotland), World U20s (his first NZ team) and the World Road Running team. A sore-foot scare in May (MRI clear). See Timeline for sources.
- **Tactical "loss" in North Carolina (14 Feb 2026):** 7th, boxed in lane 2 while Hocker set an American record. Merber: "It's just not racing." Ruthe himself owned it ("you've got to be a bit more aggressive").
- **Lost to training partner Tanner** at Cooks Classic 2026 (led to 120m out). He was also officially beaten by 0.06 in his own sub-4 race.
- **"Too much too soon" worries:**
  - The Conversation (Dylan Hicks, Flinders Uni, 12 Feb 2026) says "early excellence does not guarantee senior dominance" and that many prodigies fade because "the system around them demands too much too soon". It links to the Mary Cain / Nike Oregon Project story. https://theconversation.com/how-far-can-teenage-kiwi-running-star-sam-ruthe-go-what-science-and-history-tell-us-275214
  - Counterpoint: his camp's whole philosophy is restraint. No training until 13, age-appropriate load, about 60-80 km a week, longest run 17 km. Kirkwood: "easy to get carried away and overload his training".
- **School vs racing:** he said school was "decreasing my performance" and he's leaving it with a year to go. Could be framed as a gamble.
- **NCAA vs pro:** at 16 he said he liked the NCAA (autumn 2028). He's now on Nike and heading for a Europe base, so college looks unlikely **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- I found no controversies (doping, conduct, etc.).
- Records nuance: the 1000m best (2:17.82) was not ratified because the paperwork wasn't sent. The 3:48.88 and 3:33.25 are indoor/short-track marks.
