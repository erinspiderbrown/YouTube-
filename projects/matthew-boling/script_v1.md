## 1. Cold open
April 27, 2019. Houston, Texas. A high school regional meet.

A senior from Strake Jesuit lines up in the 100… and runs 9.98.

9.98. In high school.

Now the wind was way over the legal limit, so it don't count as a record. But it ain't matter. By the next morning that clip was everywhere. ESPN, CBS, Fox, Sports Illustrated. Everybody callin' him "White Lightning." People sayin' he the next Usain Bolt.

His name was Matthew Boling.

And 7 years later, ain't nobody talkin' 'bout him like that. So what happened?

## 2. The hype was real
Now first, let's get 1 thing straight. This kid was not a fluke.

That same season, at the Texas state meet, he run 10.13. Legal wind. That's the US high school record.

He won the long jump too. He was jumpin' 26 feet.

And then he anchor the 4x400. He get the stick over 20 meters behind… and run a 44.74 split to win it. In high school.

He won Gatorade National Athlete of the Year. And that summer at the Pan Am U20 Championships, he took 4 gold medals. 100, 200, and both relays, and both relays set world U20 records.

Oh, and the nickname? He said he ain't even like it. He didn't want his race being the story. He just wanted to run.

So he committed to Georgia. And everybody figured this the start of somethin' crazy.

## 3. Georgia
His 1st year at Georgia, COVID shut the whole season down.

2021, he come back and win the NCAA indoor title in the 200. 20.19. And for a minute, it look like the hype was right on schedule.

Then the Olympic Trials.

He go out in the semis of the 100. 14th. And in the 200? 9th. 1 spot. 1 spot from the final.

That was the 1st time it hit. This ain't high school no more.

## 4. He actually got faster
And here go the part people forget.

2022, he run 9.98 again. Except this time? Legal wind. And a couple weeks later he run 19.92 in the 200.

He won the SEC in the 200, took 2nd at NCAAs.

So he ain't slow. He a sub-10, sub-20 sprinter.

But at USAs? 6th. And in America, 6th don't make the team. You can be top 10 in the world in the 200 and still be stayin' home, 'cause the US is just that deep.

## 5. The switch
2023. He win the indoor NCAA title in the 200 again.

But outdoors, he don't even make the final.

So he try somethin' different. The 400.

At USAs he finish 6th again. But this time it got him on the relay pool for Worlds in Budapest.

And mane… he ran the 3rd leg of the mixed 4x400. Gold medal. World record.

He ran the heat of the men's 4x400 too, and they won that. So on paper? Matthew Boling a 2-time world champion.

That September he leave Georgia, skip his last year, and go pro. Moves to South Carolina to train with Alleyne Francique. Sayin' he wanna be on the world stage.

## 6. Paris
2024. The Olympic year. This the one everybody been waitin' on since that video in 2019.

He run 44.84 in the 400. That's a PR. He get a World Indoor silver on the relay, and a World Relays gold.

Then the Trials.

In the 400, he make the final… and finish 8th. Last.

In the 200? 9th again. No final.

And they don't pick him for the relay.

No Paris.

## 7. Now
2025, he show up at Grand Slam Track in Philly. 7th in the 200, 20.87. 5th in the 400.

This year he ran 45.51 in the 400.

The kid that ran 9.98 at 18, his best 100 today is still 9.98.

## 8. So is it really a downfall?
Now I'ma keep it real with y'all.

Matthew Boling a 2-time world champion. He ran sub-10 and sub-20. Most people who ever put on spikes would kill for that career.

But that ain't what we was promised. We was promised the next Usain Bolt. And that's the problem.

He went viral at 18 off a time that wasn't even legal. Before he ever ran a college race, the whole internet already decided what he was supposed to be. And everything after that got measured against 1 clip with a 4.2 tailwind.

And the US? It's the hardest team in the world to make. 6th in America can be top 10 in the world. But it still mean you stayin' home.

So was it a downfall? Or was the hype just too big for anybody?
