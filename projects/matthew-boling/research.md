# Matthew Boling: research (10 Nov 2026)

Sources: Wikipedia (Matthew_Boling), World Athletics profile (14774788), ESPN (turns pro, Sept 2023), CBS News / SI / Fox (2019 viral run), FloTrack (2021 NCAA indoor 200m), 2025 Philadelphia Slam (Wikipedia), Canadian Running (mile stunt), timinginc (2026 Ed Murphey Classic).

## Verified timeline
- Born 20 June 2000, Houston. Twin brother Michael. Strake Jesuit. Started in the high jump, then the long jump and 400m.
- 2018 World U20s (Tampere): ran the heat of the US 4x400 (team won silver).
- Committed to Georgia, Nov 2018.
- Mar 2019: long jump 26 ft 3.5 in (8.01 m) at Texas Relays.
- **27 Apr 2019: 9.98 in the 100m, wind +4.2 m/s** (legal limit +2.0). Region 6A-3 meet. Went viral (CBS, Fox, SI, ESPN). Nickname "White Lightning", which he said he didn't like.
- May 2019, Texas 6A state: **10.13 wind-legal, US high school record**. Won the long jump. **44.74 anchor on the 4x400 from over 20 m back** (3:10.56).
- Gatorade National Boys T&F Athlete of the Year 2019. Track & Field News HS Athlete of the Year.
- Jul 2019, Pan Am U20s: won the 100 (10.11) and the 200 (20.31). Ran on the 4x100 and 4x400, both World U20 records. 4 golds.
- 2020: SEC indoor 200 2nd (20.71); the season was cut short by COVID.
- 2021: DQ'd in the SEC indoor 200 final (lane violation). **NCAA indoor 200 champion, 20.19.** Outdoor: NCAA 6th in the 100, 5th in the 200. **Olympic Trials: 14th in the 100 semis, 9th in the 200 semis.** No final.
- 2022: **100m 9.98 (+1.6), Gainesville, 16 Apr. 200m 19.92 (-0.9), Atlanta, 23 Apr.** Both are still his PBs. Indoor long jump 8.25 m. SEC 200 champion (20.01). NCAA outdoor 200 2nd (20.13). USATF 200 6th (20.15), no Worlds team.
- 2023: **NCAA indoor 200 champion again (20.12).** NCAA outdoor: out in the 200 semis. USATF **400m 6th (45.17)**. **Budapest Worlds: 3rd leg of the mixed 4x400, gold, world record 3:08.80.** Also ran the men's 4x400 heat (gold). World Athletics lists him as a **2× world champion**.
- Sept 2023: turned pro and gave up his last college year. Moved to South Carolina to train with **Alleyne Francique** (ESPN). His Georgia coach was Caryl Smith-Gilbert.
- 2024: World Indoors 4x400 silver. World Relays mixed 4x400 gold. **400m PB 44.84** (Gainesville, 19 Jul 2024). **Olympic Trials: 8th in the 400 final, 9th in the 200 (no final), not picked for a relay. No Paris.**
- 2025: in the World Indoor 4x400 relay pool. Gamecock Invite, April: 10.25 / 20.51 (DirectAthletics). **Grand Slam Track, Philadelphia: 7th in the 200 (20.87), 5th in the 400 (45.21), $12,500.** Ran a sub-6 mile as an off-season training stunt (Canadian Running).
- 2026: 45.51 in the 400 at the Ed Murphey Classic, 10 Jul (timinginc).
