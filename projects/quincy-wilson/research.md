## 1. Family and background

- **Parents: Monique and Roy Wilson.** Both were college athletes. Monique was a high school and college basketball and soccer star. Roy played football at the Naval Academy and served in the Navy. When Roy retired from the Navy, the family moved from Chesapeake, VA to Gaithersburg, MD so Quincy could run for Bullis. Sources: [Washingtonian, Jun 24 2024](https://washingtonian.com/2024/06/24/4-things-to-know-about-quincy-wilson-the-16-year-old-maryland-track-star/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner)).
- **Sister: Kadence Wilson** (older). Virginia state track champion who ran for James Madison University ([Washingtonian](https://washingtonian.com/2024/06/24/4-things-to-know-about-quincy-wilson-the-16-year-old-maryland-track-star/)). **[UNVERIFIED]** A search snippet of Maryland's 2026-27 roster also lists a "Wilson, Kadence" ([UMD roster](https://umterps.com/sports/track-and-field/roster)). She may have transferred to Maryland, but the page didn't load to confirm.
- **Cousin: Shaniya Hall.** Bullis class of 2020, ran at the University of Oregon ([SI, Jul 2 2025](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/quincy-wilson-reflects-on-historic-track-journey-and-olympic-gold-at-just-17-01jz4qnqtx59)).
- **How he started:** began athletics at age 8, in the 8U age group. He recalled running in his grandmother's big backyard ([SI, Jul 2 2025](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/quincy-wilson-reflects-on-historic-track-journey-and-olympic-gold-at-just-17-01jz4qnqtx59)). At 8 he was 4th in the 400 at the AAU Junior Olympic Games. The next year (age 9) he won the first of five AAU national titles ([NBC News, Aug 2024](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/sports/quincy-wilson-16-set-become-youngest-male-us-track-olympian-history-rcna165800)).
- **Coach Joe Lee:** a former college runner turned youth pastor who came to Bullis in 2014 to build the program ([Washingtonian](https://washingtonian.com/2024/06/24/4-things-to-know-about-quincy-wilson-the-16-year-old-maryland-track-star/)). Wilson says Lee "pushes me on and off the track" and makes grades a priority ([SI, Jul 2025](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/quincy-wilson-reflects-on-historic-track-journey-and-olympic-gold-at-just-17-01jz4qnqtx59)).
- **Mentor:** Justin Gatlin. Wilson: "He's been a great mentor for me." ([SI, Jul 2025](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/quincy-wilson-reflects-on-historic-track-journey-and-olympic-gold-at-just-17-01jz4qnqtx59))
- **School:** kept an A average all through high school ([Bullis, Jul 14 2026](https://www.bullis.org/alumni/alumni-news/article/~board/bullis/post/bullis-alum-quincy-wilson-26-makes-history-with-third-gatorade-maryland-player-of-the-year-award)). CNN profile (Feb 5 2025): "straight-A student, 'more focused in the classroom than on the track'" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/05/sport/quincy-wilson-olympics-high-school-spt-intl), headline only; page blocked).

## 2. Youth / AAU (to 2022)

- **March 2023, New Balance Nationals Indoor (Boston):** won the 400m, **46.67**, as the only freshman in the field. It was his first national HS title ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/quincy-wilson-career-highlights); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).
- **April 2023, Penn Relays:** 4x400 split 45.06 per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner)) **[UNVERIFIED]**. Bullis finished 6th ([T&FN](https://trackandfieldnews.com/penn-relays-hs-bullis-snaps-19-year-u-s-4x4-drought/)).
- **June 2023, New Balance Nationals Outdoor:** 2nd in the 400 per Wikipedia. This conflicts with later claims of an unbeaten NB streak (see Disputed).
- **USATF U20 Championships, Eugene (summer 2023):** 4th in the 400m, 46.12 (Wikipedia). CITIUS says he ran **45.87** at "the U.S. championships" as a freshman, which was probably the semi ([CITIUS](https://citiusmag.com/articles/new-balance-announces-nil-roster)) **[time conflict]**.
- **September 20, 2023:** signed a **New Balance NIL deal at 15**, one of the youngest US athletes with a major-brand deal ([CITIUS](https://citiusmag.com/articles/new-balance-announces-nil-roster)).

## 4. 2024 (sophomore): records, Trials, Paris

### Indoor
- **Jan 2024, VA Showcase:** 500m in 1:01.27, 0.02 off the HS record ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/quincy-wilson-career-highlights)).
- **Feb 2024:** 200m 21.02 (Virginia Beach). 600m 1:17.36 at Millrose, the #2 HS boys' 600 ever ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).
- **March 2024, NB Nationals Indoor:** **45.76**, a national HS indoor record and world U18 indoor best. It broke Elzie Coleman's 20-year-old HS record by 0.16 ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/quincy-wilson-career-highlights); [MileSplit](https://www.milesplit.com/articles/345493/quincy-wilson-torches-4576-national-record-at-nbni)). He also anchored Bullis to a 3:11.87 national-record indoor 4x400 (Wikipedia).

### Outdoor (HS)
- **Mar 29, 2024, Florida Relays:** 45.19 ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/quincy-wilson-career-highlights)).
- **Apr 5, 2024:** 800m in 1:50.44, Myrtle Beach (Wikipedia).
- **Apr 2024, Penn Relays:** prelim anchor **44.37**, called the fastest HS 400 split ever at Penn. Final split 44.69; Bullis was 3rd in 3:13.10 and Kingston College won in 3:11.86 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).
- **May 2024, Puma East Coast Invitational:** won in a PB 45.17 over Jayden Horton-Mims (45.82) ([MileSplit](https://pa.milesplit.com/articles/347719/watch-jayden-horton-mims-challenges-quincy-wilson-in-400m)).
- **June 15, 2024, NB Nationals Outdoor (Philadelphia):** **45.13**, a PB and meet record (still the meet record) ([MileSplit](https://www.milesplit.com/articles/349116/bullis-star-quincy-wilson-clocks-4513-sets-another-record); [Wikipedia NBNO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Balance_Nationals_Outdoor)).
- **June 27, 2024:** Gatorade Maryland Boys Track POY, the first of three ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/quincy-wilson-career-highlights)).

### U.S. Olympic Trials, Eugene (age 16, 19 days after finishing sophomore year)
- **Round 1, Friday June 21, 2024: 44.66.** This broke **Darrell Robinson's 44.69 (1982)**, the US high school record and world U18 best, which had stood 42 years ([LetsRun](https://letsrun.com/news/2024/06/sophomore-quincy-wilson-runs-44-66-to-break-high-school-400m-record-at-2024-olympic-trials); [SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/watch-bullis-schools-quincy-wilson-breaks-42-year-old-world-record-01j15rv1hef2)).
- **Semifinal, Sunday June 23, 2024: 44.59.** He was 3rd in his heat and 4th-fastest overall, and broke the U18 best again ([AOL/Register-Guard](https://www.aol.com/16-old-quincy-wilson-sets-023636087.html); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/rcna158675)).
- **Final, Monday June 24, 2024: 6th, 44.94.** Quincy Hall won in 44.17, ahead of Michael Norman (44.41) and Chris Bailey (44.42) ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/rcna158675); [Olympics.com](https://olympics.com/en/news/world-bronze-medallist-quincy-hall-clips-michael-norman-400m-final-usa-trials-personal-best-effort)).
- Quotes (NBC News, Jun 24 2024):
  - "I've never been this happy a day in my life when it comes to track." (after the 44.59)
  - "I broke it twice in two days. It means a lot to me, because it means the hard work is paying off."
  - "I didn't get out the way that I wanted to, but like my coach said, the race starts at 300."
  - "If you look at me, I'm not as strong, so it's 100% heart inside." (Olympics.com headline: "All I'm doing is heart.")
  - Michael Norman: "A 16-year-old is going out here competing like a true competitor."

### Relay pool selection
- Joe Lee got a call late on a Sunday night from the relay-pool committee. Wilson and Lee confirmed to ESPN the next day (around June 30/July 1, 2024) that he was in the 4x400 pool. USATF's roster became official July 8, 2024 ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/olympics/news/2024-u-s-olympic-track-and-field-trials-16-year-old-quincy-wilson-makes-4x400m-relay-pool-bound-for-paris); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/olympics/story/_/id/40474265/quincy-wilson-16-becomes-youngest-us-male-track-olympian); [FloTrack article](https://flotrack.org/articles/12695922-quincy-wilson-has-been-added-to-the-us-olympic-relay-pool)).

### Holloway Pro Classic
- **July 19, 2024, Gainesville FL: 44.20**, a PB and U18 world best, beating Olympian Bryce Deadmon (44.23) ([FloTrack article](https://flotrack.org/articles/12740981-quincy-wilson-sets-new-world-u18-record-in-400m-with-4420-in-gainesville); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).

### Paris 2024
- **Friday Aug 9, 2024, men's 4x400 round 1:** Wilson ran leadoff in **47.27** (FloTrack had 47.30), the second-slowest of the 32 runners in that heat, and handed off in 7th. Chris Bailey's 44.14 anchor brought the US from 7th to 3rd in **2:59.15**, enough to advance ([ABC/AP](https://abcnews.go.com/Sports/wireStory/teen-sprinter-quincy-wilsons-1st-race-olympics-learning-112704816); [ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/olympics/story/_/id/40790092/us-4x400-relay-team-advances-quincy-wilson-1st-olympics-race)).
- **Age:** 16 years, 214 days. He became the **youngest US male track & field Olympian**, passing Jim Ryun (17, Tokyo 1964). Historian Bill Mallon also cited Arthur Newton (17, 1904) ([ABC/AP](https://abcnews.go.com/Sports/wireStory/teen-sprinter-quincy-wilsons-1st-race-olympics-learning-112704816); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/sports/quincy-wilson-16-set-become-youngest-male-us-track-olympian-history-rcna165800)).
- **Saturday Aug 10, 2024, final:** Vernon Norwood, Bryce Deadmon, Chris Bailey and Rai Benjamin won gold in **2:54.43**, an Olympic record, over Botswana. Wilson got gold for running the heat. NBC News calls him the youngest male Olympic T&F gold medalist (see Disputed).
- Quotes:
  - "I wasn't 100% myself, but my team came out here and did it for me." (AP, Aug 9 2024)
  - "It's going to be motivation for me to come back and give my team a better chance of what they had today." (AP)
  - "I knew I had a great three legs behind me." / "I knew it wasn't just myself, because by myself we would be in last place." ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/sports/quincy-wilson-16-set-become-youngest-male-us-track-olympian-history-rcna165800))
  - Vernon Norwood: "I'm super proud of him." (AP)
- **Hidden injury:** Wilson later revealed a hamstring injury picked up soon after making the team: "While running a 200 on the curve, I felt something real quick. I tried to push through, but my whole hamstring grabbed up." He was getting treatment every day ([EssentiallySports, via search snippet](https://www.essentiallysports.com/us-sports-news-track-and-field-news-hamstring-incident-almost-ruined-quincy-wilsons-olympic-dream-as-track-and-field-stars-surprising-injury-revealed/); page now 410).
- **"Did he deserve the medal?" debate (early 2025):** teammates defended him ([Pulse Sports, Feb 22 2025](https://www.pulse.co.ke/story/rai-benjamin-and-grant-holloway-reaffirm-why-quincy-wilson-deserved-his-olympic-gold-medal-2026101508123361473)).
  - Rai Benjamin: "I don't get that argument—that you didn't do anything." He also called Wilson "pivotal in getting us through the rounds."
  - Grant Holloway: "It takes a different type of dog to go out there, still get the baton around the track."
  - Wilson on the hamstring: "I promise you, nobody would've stepped on the track."

### End of 2024
- **Dec 2024, US Marine Corps Holiday Classic (The Armory):** 600m in 1:17.19, a meet record ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).
- Awards: **USATF Youth Athlete of the Year 2024** ([USATF](https://www.usatf.org/News/2024/Wilson-s-world-U18-best-earns-him-USATF-Athlete-of)) and **T&FN 2024 HS Boys Athlete of the Year** ([T&FN](https://trackandfieldnews.com/article/2024-hs-boys-athlete-of-the-year-quincy-wilson/)).

## 5. 2025 (junior): highs, first losses, missed Tokyo

- **Jan 27, 2025, VA Showcase:** 500m 1:02.49, 2nd to rival **Andrew Salvodon** (1:00.49, new HS record) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Wilson_(runner))).
- **Feb 1, 2025, New Balance Indoor Grand Prix (Boston, pro meet):** won the 400 in **45.66**, beating Olympians and lowering the HS indoor record and U18 best ([FloTrack article](https://www.flotrack.org/articles/13630922-quincy-wilson-takes-down-olympians-lowers-indoor-hs-400m-national-record)).
- **Feb 23, 2025, USATF Indoor Championships:** 5th in the final in **46.13**. Chris Bailey won in 45.21 ([FloTrack article](https://www.flotrack.org/articles/13758685-quincy-wilson-finishes-5th-at-usa-indoor-track-and-field-championships-2025)).
- **March 2025, NB Nationals Indoor:** won the 400, his 3rd straight NBNI title ([MileSplit MD](https://md.milesplit.com/articles/356682/bullis-stars-quincy-wilson-sydney-sutton-sweep-new-balance-400m-titles-nbni-2025)).

### Outdoor
- **Apr 25, 2025, Penn Relays:** anchored in **43.99**. Bullis (Mickey Green, Cam Homer, Colin Abrams, Wilson) ran **3:06.31**, breaking Hawthorne (CA)'s 1985 HS record of 3:07.40, which had stood 40 years. They still finished **2nd** to Kingston College (3:05.93) ([SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/maryland/olympic-gold-medalist-quincy-wilson-runs-blistering-anchor-leg-to-break-40-year-national-record-at-penn-relays-01jsqayv9v44); [T&FN](https://trackandfieldnews.com/penn-relays-hs-bullis-snaps-19-year-u-s-4x4-drought/)).
- **Spring 2025:** signed with talent agency WME (William Morris Endeavor) days after the Penn split ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/olympics/news-quincy-wilson-signs-william-morris-endeavor-days-clocking-fastest-4x400m-split-high-school-history)) **[UNVERIFIED, page blocked]**.
- **May 3, 2025, Puma East Coast International (Maryland):** **LOST** to Jayden Horton-Mims, a Florida signee, who ran 45.24 for US #1. Wilson ran about 45.46 **[UNVERIFIED time]**. It set off "has he plateaued?" talk online ([SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/national/olympic-star-quincy-wilson-dethroned-by-jayden-horton-mims-for-u-s-no-1-in-400-meter-01jtbnyhd6x3); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/high-school-sports/news-florida-signee-follows-brandon-arrington-jr-s-footsteps-beats-olympic-gold-medallist-quincy-wilson-gain-top-national-ranking)).
- **June 21, 2025, NB Nationals Outdoor (Franklin Field):** won the 400 in **45.37** by almost a second (Jayden DeLeon 2nd, 46.33; Andrew Salvodon 4th). Hecklers yelled Salvodon's name at him while he was in the blocks; he answered with a "death stare" and won. BroBible called it his 6th straight NB 400 title ([BroBible](https://brobible.com/sports/article/quincy-wilson-hecklers-new-balance-nationals-track-400-win-full-race); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/us/olympics/news-new-balance-nationals-outdoor-championships-2025-results-quincy-wilson-wins-sixth-national-title-day-3)).
- **July 12, 2025, Ed Murphey Classic, Memphis (World Athletics Continental Tour Silver): 44.10.** A US HS record, a world U18 best (his 4th time lowering it) and a PB. He beat Bryce Deadmon (44.39) and Delano Kennedy (JAM, 45.00); it was joint 4th-fastest in the world that season ([World Athletics](https://worldathletics.org/news/report/wilson-world-u18-400m-best-mccallum-memphis); [SI](https://www.si.com/high-school/national/quincy-wilson-shatters-u18-world-record-with-44-10-400m-at-ed-murphey-classic-01k030vfjcag)). ESPN calls 44.10 the 2nd-fastest time ever by a U20 athlete ([ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/college-sports/story/_/id/47090210/quincy-wilson-taking-track-talents-maryland)). Wilson said he "shot out like a bullet" ([Sportskeeda](https://hindi3.sportskeeda.com/us/olympics/news-shot-like-bullet-quincy-wilson-gets-real-setting-new-u18-400m-world-record-ed-murphy-classic)).
- **Aug 1, 2025, USATF Outdoor Championships, Eugene, semifinal 2:** **4th in 45.39, out**. He missed the final and the **Tokyo 2025 World Championships**, despite entering with the 3rd-best US time of 2025. No source shows him in the Tokyo relay pool ([Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/17-old-quincy-wilson-faces-001731689.html); [WatchAthletics results](https://www.watchathletics.com/page/6884/men-s-400m-results-usatf-outdoor-championships-2025)).
  - Marcus Thompson (The Athletic) on X: "He was NOT HAPPY." / "It's probably my favorite part about him. He can't stand losing and he doesn't hide it."

And I'm ready to do it at the University of Maryland." ([UMD Today](https://today.umd.edu/olympic-gold-medalist-signs-with-umd-track-and-field-program))
  - Wilson (per press): "There's nothing like being close to my family." ([Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/quincy-wilson-olympic-gold-medalist-222542529.html))
  - Valmon: "Quincy is a generational athlete who has the upside to continue to thrive at the top of our sport." / "His support system here at Maryland, in his home state, is unmatched." / "I am excited to get to work on this next phase of his journey."
  - Student radio WMUC called it a "shocking move" ([WMUC](https://wmucsports.net/olympian-quincy-wilson-commits-to-maryland-track-and-field-in-a-shocking-move/)).
  - Note: he chose college/NCAA instead of turning pro, and kept his New Balance NIL deal.

## 9. Narrative angles

1. **"Is he living up to the hype?"** The arc: 44.66 / 44.59 at 16 → Olympic gold at 16 → 44.10 at 17 → **no improvement in 2026** (SB 44.62, best HS-season open 45.48). He lost his US U20 title and the World U20 title to a high school rival who was a 45.38 runner a few months earlier. Phrase it as a question: did he peak at 17, or is this a normal plateau before the jump to college and pro?
2. **The rival: Jayden DeLeon.** Also from Maryland (Montgomery Blair HS) and headed to LSU. The arc: lost NBNO 2025 by almost a second (46.33) → lost NBNI 2026 by 0.009 → beat Wilson at USATF U20 (44.52 vs 44.84) → beat him again for World U20 gold (44.47 CR vs 44.62) → they ran together on the gold-medal relay. A Maryland vs LSU NCAA rivalry is coming in 2027.
3. **Other rivals and threats:** Jayden Horton-Mims (beat him May 2025, Florida signee); Andrew Salvodon (500m HS record, the name hecklers used at NBNO 2025).
4. **The "did he earn the gold?" debate:** he ran a 47.27 leadoff on a hidden hamstring injury, and the US was 7th at his handoff. Teammates Rai Benjamin and Grant Holloway defended him.
5. **Firsts and youngest:** youngest US male T&F Olympian (beat Jim Ryun's 60-year-old mark), Darrell Robinson's 42-year HS record, a New Balance deal at 15.
6. **Stayed home:** chose Maryland and Valmon (an Olympic 4x400 champion) over the SEC/Pac powers, with LA 2028 as the stated goal.
7. **Missed Tokyo 2025:** out in the USATF semis (45.39), visibly upset.
8. **Comparisons:** Darrell Robinson (the 1982 HS phenom whose record he broke), Michael Norman (Arcadia record), Jim Ryun (youngest US Olympian before him), Obea Moore. It also fits the channel's "America's ___ Ever... What Went Wrong?" angle, but carefully: nothing has really gone wrong yet; it's "plateau + rival".
