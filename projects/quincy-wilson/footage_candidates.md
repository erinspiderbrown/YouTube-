## Facts to anchor the script (checked against sources found)
- Born 2008. Military family: dad served 25 years in the Navy. They lived in Connecticut, Kansas, Florida, Georgia and Virginia, then Maryland. Youth clubs: Fort Meade High Steppers, Track 757 (Virginia Beach), Great Bridge. He moved back to Maryland for 9th grade at **Bullis School** (Potomac, MD). He calls himself "PG County"; local TV calls him a Bowie native. Coach: **Joe Lee**, who left Bullis for Good Counsel in 2026. Sister **Cadence** runs at James Madison. GMA names his parents Roy and Mo'Nique (verify spelling before it goes on screen).
- 2023 (freshman): NBNI 400 m champion in 46.67 as a 15-year-old. Penn Relays 4x400 anchor split 45.06.
- 2024: Penn Relays prelim anchor of 44.37 (6th to 1st after Bullis fell), 44.69 in the final (3rd behind Kingston College). Olympic Trials: 44.66 in round 1 (U18 world record and HS record, breaking a 42-year-old mark), 44.59 in the semis, 6th in the final (44.94). Named to the relay pool. 44.20 at the Holloway Pro Classic. **Paris**: led off the 4x400 heat (47.27 split; hamstring injury in training days before). Got gold as a heat runner and was the youngest US male track Olympian ever.
- 2025: 43.99 Penn anchor split, Bullis 3:06.31 HS national record but 2nd to Kingston College. Ed Murphey Classic 44.10 (4th U18 world record). Committed to **Maryland** on 24 Nov 2025, picking it over USC, UCLA, Texas A&M and South Carolina. Maryland's coach is Andrew Valmon.
- 2026 (senior): NBNI 45.37, a new HS indoor record, beating Jayden DeLeon by 0.009 s for a 4-year sweep. Arcadia meet record 45.48 (the old record was Michael Norman's). **Penn Relays 4x400 win**, the first US team to win since 2007. USATF U20s: 2nd to DeLeon (44.52 vs 44.84). World U20 Eugene: silver behind DeLeon in 44.62 (DeLeon 44.47 championship record), plus 4x400 gold. Freshman at Maryland in fall 2026.
