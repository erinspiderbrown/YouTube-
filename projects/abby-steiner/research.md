# Abby Steiner: research (12 Nov 2026)

## Verified facts
- **Background:** born November 24, 1999, Dublin, Ohio. Dublin Coffman High School. All-state soccer forward (Ohio Premier SC). Started track in 8th grade. Tore her ACL in high school (out most of a year). 4 state records; 14 individual + 2 relay state titles. (Wikipedia)
- **Kentucky:** dual-sport scholarship. 2018 soccer: 19 starts, 2 goals, 5 assists, then track only. Graduated May 2022 (kinesiology/exercise science); deferred PT school. (Wikipedia)
- **NCAA titles:** 2021 indoor 200 (22.38), 2022 indoor 200 (22.16), 2022 outdoor 200. "4-time NCAA champion" (OutKick/FOS).
  - US indoor record 200m: 22.09, College Station, Feb 26, 2022.
  - **Collegiate record 21.80 (+1.3), NCAA outdoors, Hayward Field, June 11, 2022.**
  - 100m PB 10.90 (June 9, 2022).
- **USA champion:** 200m, **21.77**, June 26, 2022, Hayward Field (Clark 2nd 21.92). World's fastest that year until Shericka Jackson's 21.55. (TFN, World Athletics)
- **2022 Worlds, Eugene:** 200m final 5th (22.26). **Gold 4x100** (41.14) and **gold 4x400** (3:17.79). 2-time world champion.
- **Awards:** 2022 Bowerman Award (Dec 15, 2022), Honda Sports Award.
- **PUMA:** officially signed July 14, 2022 (PUMA release). **$2 million widely rumored/reported, never confirmed**; on July 5, 2022 she posted that the details are "between my sponsor and me".
- **Injuries:** foot/heel problems from 2023. 2023 USAs 4th (22.07); missed the 2023 Worlds in Budapest injured. Haglund's deformity surgery on both feet (heel bone removed, Achilles reattached). 2024 Olympic Trials 200m 6th (22.24): no Paris. Symptoms returned in the left foot; third surgery on the left foot in 2025. **Surgery count: "at least three" (Canadian Running, Yahoo, FOS); UPI says five.** Use "at least 3".
  - Exact year of the both-feet surgery differs by source (end of 2023 vs after the 2024 Trials). The script avoids a date.
- **Aug 19, 2025:** "Taking a step back from running for the time being to give myself the time I need to get healthy." Passed the NSCA CSCS exam; master's in exercise science at the University of South Carolina. Did not compete in 2025.
- **Lawsuit:** filed **April 24, 2026**, Middlesex County Superior Court, Massachusetts, against **PUMA and Mercedes-Benz Grand Prix (Mercedes F1)**, which the suit says helped design the spikes.
  - Shoes named: Deviate Nitro Elite 2, Deviate Nitro Elite 3, evoSPEED Tokyo Nitro.
  - Claims: carbon-fiber plate + nitro foam "changed the foot and ankle mechanics during running", caused bone stress injuries; shoes "defective", "unsafe"; the companies knew and failed to warn. She says she "only recently" linked the shoes to the injuries.
  - Seeks more than $1.25 million, medical bills, lost earnings, "the loss of full enjoyment of life and disfigurement". Jury trial demanded.
  - **PUMA:** "Puma is aware that a case has been filed. Unfortunately, we cannot comment on active litigation." / "However, we strongly deny any allegation that our performance products cause injuries." It points to world records set in its shoes.
  - Mercedes: "nothing to comment re ongoing legal proceedings".
  - **Not proven in court; no trial date** (as of the April 2026 reports).
