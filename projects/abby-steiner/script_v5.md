## 1. Cold open
June 2022. Hayward Field.

A white girl from Ohio lines up in the 200… against the fastest women in America.

And she beat all of 'em.

[SOUNDBITE: the 21.77 race call]

21.77. US champion. At that moment, the fastest time in the whole world that year.

4 weeks later she a 2-time world champion.

2 years later… her career was basically over.

Her name is Abby Steiner. This the downfall of Abby Steiner. And what went wrong.

## 2. Why she was different
Now let's just keep it real.

In sprinting today, the top of the women's 200 is mostly Black women. America. Jamaica. The Caribbean. That's just facts. Go look at any Olympic final.

So when a white girl from Dublin, Ohio starts running with them… and beating them… people noticed.

Y'all remember Matthew Boling. "White Lightning." Same thing. The hype was different.

And Abby? Abby actually backed it up.

## 3. The soccer girl
And here go the crazy part. Track wasn't even her first sport.

She was a soccer player. All-state forward.

She ain't start running track till 8th grade. Then in high school she tore her ACL and missed most of a year.

Came back anyway. 14 state titles.

Kentucky gave her a scholarship for both. Soccer and track. Her freshman year she started 19 soccer games.

Then she made a choice. No more soccer. Just track.

## 4. The rise
2021. She win the NCAA indoor title in the 200.

2022. She run 22.09 indoors. The American record.

Then June 11, 2022. The NCAA outdoor final. 21.80.

[SOUNDBITE: the 21.80 race call]

The fastest a college woman has ever run the 200. Ever.

2 weeks later, same track. US Championships. 21.77. She beat everybody.

Then the World Championships. Same track again.

5th in the 200. But in the relays? Gold in the 4x100. Gold in the 4x400.

[SOUNDBITE: Abby after the relay gold]

Then they gave her the Bowerman. The biggest award in college track. And PUMA signs her. Reportedly a 2 million dollar deal.

She 22 years old. A 2-time world champion. The sky was the limit.

## 5. The fall
2023 started good. She ran an American record indoors in the 300.

But then… her heels.

USAs 2023. She finish 4th in the 200. No individual spot at Worlds.

Then her season got shut down early.

August 2023, surgery on her left foot. September, surgery on her right foot.

2024. Olympic year. She fight her way back to the Trials and make the 200 final.

6th. No Paris.

[SOUNDBITE: Abby crying after the Trials]

Then her left foot started hurting again. Another surgery. At least 3 surgeries total.

2025, she didn't run 1 race.

August 2025, she posted it herself. She said she was "taking a step back from running for the time being to give myself the time I need to get healthy."

## 6. What went wrong
So what went wrong? Let's break it down.

Number 1. 2022 was the longest season of her life.

College indoors in the winter. College outdoors in the spring. USAs in June. Then Worlds in July. Prelims, semis, finals, relays. She was racing from January all the way to the end of July.

At Worlds, after the relay gold, somebody asked her if she was finally done. She said, "We'll be done after this meet."

Number 2. Her heels.

What she had is called Haglund's. That's a bone bump on the back of your heel that rubs on the Achilles. Every step, it's grinding.

She had it in both feet. So both feet got surgery. Left foot in August. Right foot in September.

Number 3. The clock.

She told reporters at the Trials, the 1st time she even jogged on a track again was around the beginning of December.

December. And the Olympic Trials is June. That's 6 months to go from surgery to trying to make the Olympic team. In the deepest sprint country in the world.

Number 4. She trained through it.

Right before the Trials, her foot started acting up again. And she said they "thought it was best to train through" it.

Then after the Trials… the pain came back. Another surgery.

And number 5. She blames her old spikes. She even took her shoe company to court over it. But that's a whole other video.

## 7. Where she at now
So where Abby Steiner at now?

Not on a track.

She in grad school. University of South Carolina. Master's in exercise science.

The girl who was the fastest college woman ever… is in a classroom.

And think about this. Her 21.77 would've medaled in the Paris Olympic final. She was 22. Sprinters peak around 26, 27.

She should be in her prime right now.
