In 2022, Abby Steiner was the fastest woman in America in the 200. She ran 21.80, the fastest a college woman has ever run, won the US title in 21.77, and left Eugene a 2-time world champion at 22. Two years later her career was basically over. Both heels. At least 3 surgeries. And then she just disappeared.

So what went wrong? Let me know in the comments: was it the season, the surgeries, or did she rush back?
