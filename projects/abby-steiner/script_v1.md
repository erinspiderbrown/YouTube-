# Abby Steiner: script v1 (for the user to read)

The angle: America's fastest college woman ever. 2 world golds. A PUMA deal. Then her feet broke down, and in April 2026 she sued PUMA, saying their spikes ended her career. Same build as the Boling video: real clips play between your parts.
About 850 words, about 6 min of your voice; with the real clips it should land around 9 min. Read each part in one take. If you mess up, pause and say the line again.
Numbers are written as numbers. Change any line to how you'd really say it.

---

## 1. Cold open
June 11, 2022. Hayward Field. The NCAA 200 meter final.

A senior from Kentucky comes off the curve… and runs 21.80.

[SOUNDBITE: the race call]

21.80. The fastest a college woman has ever run the 200. Ever.

2 weeks later she win the US title. 4 weeks after that, she a 2-time world champion.

Then PUMA signs her. Reportedly a 2 million dollar deal.

And 4 years later?

She ain't racing no more. And she suing PUMA… saying their spikes ended her career.

Her name is Abby Steiner. Lemme tell y'all what happened.

## 2. The soccer girl
Abby Steiner from Dublin, Ohio.

And track wasn't even her first sport. She was a soccer player. All-state forward.

She ain't start running track till 8th grade. Then in high school she tore her ACL and missed most of a year.

Came back anyway. 14 state titles.

Kentucky gave her a scholarship for both. Soccer and track. Her freshman year she started 19 soccer games.

Then she made a choice. No more soccer. Just track.

Best decision she ever made.

## 3. The run
2021. She win the NCAA indoor title in the 200.

2022. She run 22.09 indoors. That's the American record. She win the NCAA indoor title again.

Then outdoors. Hayward Field. 21.80. Fastest college woman ever.

[SOUNDBITE: Abby after the 21.80]

2 weeks later, same track, the US Championships. She run 21.77. US champion. At that moment, the fastest time in the whole world that year.

Then the World Championships. Same track again.

5th in the 200. But in the relays? Gold in the 4x100. Gold in the 4x400.

[SOUNDBITE: relay race call]

Then they gave her the Bowerman. The biggest award in college track.

She 22 years old. A 2-time world champion. And she just getting started.

## 4. The deal
July 14, 2022. PUMA makes it official. Abby Steiner is a PUMA athlete.

Now PUMA ain't never say the number. But it was widely reported at 2 million dollars.

Abby said that's between her and her sponsor.

Either way… PUMA was betting big on her. And everybody thought it was a smart bet.

## 5. The feet
But in 2023, something wasn't right.

Her heels.

She still made it to USAs. 4th in the 200. 22.07.

But she ain't make it to Worlds in Budapest. She was hurt.

Then came the surgery. Both feet. They took bone out of her heels… and reattached her Achilles.

[SOUNDBITE: Abby on the surgery]

2024. Olympic year. She fight her way back to the Trials. And she make the 200 final.

6th. 22.24. No Paris.

That was her last race.

The pain came back in her left foot. Another surgery. At least 3 surgeries total.

August 2025, she posted it herself. She said she was "taking a step back from running for the time being to give myself the time I need to get healthy."

She went back to school. Master's degree. Exercise science.

The fastest college woman ever… and she ain't run a race since.

## 6. The lawsuit
Then April 24, 2026.

Abby Steiner sues PUMA. And the Mercedes Formula 1 team.

Yeah. The F1 team. The lawsuit says they helped design the spikes.

She names the shoes. The Deviate Nitro Elite 2. The Deviate Nitro Elite 3. And the evoSPEED Tokyo Nitro.

Her lawsuit says the carbon plate and the foam "changed the foot and ankle mechanics during running." It says they caused bone stress injuries. It calls the shoes "defective" and "unsafe."

And she says she only recently put it together. That it was the shoes.

She asking for more than 1.25 million dollars. Medical bills. Lost earnings. And "the loss of full enjoyment of life."

## 7. PUMA's side
Now PUMA ain't going quiet.

They said: "We strongly deny any allegation that our performance products cause injuries."

And they pointed out that athletes been setting world records in those same shoes.

So nothing's been proven. No trial date. It's her word against PUMA.

Now I'ma keep it real with y'all.

Every pro sprinter on the planet running in carbon plates right now. Every one.

So if Abby wins this, it ain't just about her. It's about every spike every company make.

But if PUMA wins… then this is just what sprinting does to your body. And she just ran out of luck.

So which one is it?
