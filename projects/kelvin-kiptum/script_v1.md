# Kelvin Kiptum: script v1 (for the user to read)

Working title: "The Fastest Marathoner Ever... Gone At 24"
Structure (new, different from Mary): it opens on the 5 days between his record becoming official and the crash,
goes back to the boy herding cows in Chepkorio, rides the 3 marathons up to the record, comes back to that night,
then the questions nobody answered, and ends in April 2026, when somebody else finally breaks 2 hours... and it wasn't him.
No "Lemme tell y'all", no chapter numbers read out, no "So what y'all think". About 950 words, ~6 1/2 min of your voice.
Read each part in one take. If you mess up, pause and say the line again. Facts are in research.md.

---

## 1. Five days
February 6, 2024. It's official. Kelvin Kiptum is the fastest marathon runner in history. 2:00:35.

He's 24 years old. He's only run 3 marathons in his life. And he won all 3.

In 2 months he's going to Rotterdam to try something no human has ever done in a real race. Run a marathon under 2 hours.

5 days later, he's gone.

## 2. Chepkorio
Kelvin grew up in Chepkorio, a little village up in the hills of western Kenya. Around 8,500 feet up.

He was an only child. His family farmed, and his job as a kid was taking the cows out.

And while he's out there with the cows, he sees the runners. Grown men training on the forest trails by his house. Some of 'em barefoot.

So he starts following 'em.

At 13 he runs his first half marathon. Finishes 10th.

No academy. No shoe deal. Just a kid from the village chasing the men up the hill.

## 3. The coach
One of the men who saw him back then was a coach from Rwanda named Gervais Hakizimana.

At first Kelvin was the kid who'd jump in and run with his group. Later, Gervais became his coach.

And Kelvin trained like nobody else. Some weeks over 250 kilometers. That's more than 150 miles in 7 days.

2020, he runs a half marathon in Valencia in 58:42.

Now people know his name.

## 4. 3 marathons
December 2022, Valencia. His first marathon ever. 2:01:53.

The fastest marathon debut in history. By a lot.

April 2023, London. 2:01:25. Course record. 16 seconds off the world record.

And the world record belonged to Eliud Kipchoge. The greatest marathoner ever. The man everybody in Kenya looked up to.

Then October 8, 2023. Chicago.

Kelvin runs the second half of that race faster than anybody had ever run the second half of a marathon. He's out there by himself.

2:00:35.

He takes 34 seconds off Kipchoge's world record. And he wins by almost 3 and a half minutes.

He's 23 years old. And the first sub-2 marathon in a real race? Everybody thinks it's his. Not if. When.

## 5. Rotterdam
After Chicago, the plan is set. Rotterdam, April 14, 2024.

One of his training partners said he wasn't just going for under 2. He was going for 1:58.

On February 6, World Athletics makes the record official.

And Kelvin goes back home to train.

## 6. That night
February 11, 2024. A Sunday night, around 11 o'clock.

Kelvin is driving on the road between Eldoret and Kaptagat. That's the road where half of Kenya's best runners train.

His coach Gervais is in the car with him. And a young woman named Sharon.

The car leaves the road. It goes into a ditch and hits a tree.

Kelvin dies right there. He's 24.

Gervais dies right there too.

Sharon survives, hurt bad.

Kenya wakes up Monday morning to the news. And nobody can believe it.

## 7. The questions
And then the questions start.

Kelvin's dad, Samson, told reporters that a few days before the crash, 4 men he didn't know came to the house asking where Kelvin was.

He asked the government to find out who they were.

Police picked up 4 men for questioning. Then they let 'em go.

A year later, his dad said he still hadn't seen the police report.

Officially it's a car accident. But his family still wants answers.

## 8. Kipchoge
And then, people online came for Eliud Kipchoge.

No evidence. Nothing. Just people saying Kipchoge had something to do with it, 'cause Kelvin took his record.

Kipchoge said his family got death threats. He said he was scared for his kids. And he lost a lot of his friends.

The greatest marathoner ever, being blamed for a car crash he had nothing to do with.

## 9. Goodbye
February 23, 2024. They bury Kelvin on his farm in Naiberi, not far from where he grew up.

The president of Kenya is there. The president of World Athletics is there.

He left behind his wife, Asenath, and their 2 little kids.

In April, Rotterdam runs without him. Before the start, the whole field stands there for a minute of silence.

## 10. Under 2
April 26, 2026. London.

A Kenyan named Sabastian Sawe crosses the finish line in 1:59:30.

The first man ever to run a marathon under 2 hours in a real race. The record is gone.

The thing Kelvin was supposed to do... somebody finally did.

## 11. The end
Kelvin Kiptum ran 3 marathons. He won all 3. He held the world record for 2 years and 6 months.

He never got to Rotterdam. He never got to the Olympics. He never got to find out how fast he really was.

So when Sawe crossed that line in London... was he running against the clock?

Or was he running against Kelvin?
