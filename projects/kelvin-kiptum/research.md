# Kelvin Kiptum: research (17 Nov 2026)
Sources: Wikipedia; Marathon Handbook timeline; Standard (KE), Citizen Digital, K24, Nation; BBC Sport Africa / ESPN (Kipchoge, May 2024);
Reuters (Rotterdam plan, Dec 2023); Pulse Sports / People Daily (estate dispute, Feb 2025); randalolson / The Running Channel (Sawe 2026).

- Born 2 Dec 1999, Chepsamo village, Chepkorio, Elgeyo-Marakwet, Kenya (~2,600 m). Only child of Samson Cheruiyot and Mary Kangongo.
  Herded the family's cattle; followed barefoot runners on forest trails. Started running ~13 (2013): 10th in his first half marathon (Eldoret).
  (A LetsRun report notes he once claimed a 1996 birth year: say "24" as officially reported.)
- Coach: Gervais Hakizimana (Rwandan), worked with him from 2020 (reportedly in his group since 2013).
- Halves: Lisbon 2019 59:54; Valencia Half 2020 58:42.
- Marathon 1: Valencia, 4 Dec 2022, 2:01:53, fastest debut ever, course record (60:15 second half).
- Marathon 2: London, 23 Apr 2023, 2:01:25, course record, 16 s off the WR.
- Marathon 3: Chicago, 8 Oct 2023, 2:00:35 WORLD RECORD (Kipchoge's 2:01:09 from Berlin 2022 beaten by 34 s); won by almost 3.5 min. Age 23.
- Training: 250-280 km a week (pre-London 2023).
- Next: Rotterdam, 14 Apr 2024, announced attempt at the first sub-2 marathon; a training mate said he was going for 1:58.
- 6 Feb 2024: World Athletics ratifies the record. 11 Feb 2024, ~11 pm: crash near Kaptagat on the Eldoret-Ravine road; Kiptum was driving,
  lost control, left the road into a ditch, hit a tree. Kiptum (24) and Hakizimana died at the scene; passenger Sharon Chepkurui Kosgei survived with serious injuries.
  Police said the front of the car was intact and the airbags did not deploy. Investigation "ongoing".
- Father Samson Cheruiyot: four unidentified men came to the house days before asking where Kelvin was; asked the government to investigate.
  Police detained four men for questioning and released them. Feb 2025: father said he still hadn't been given the police report.
- 16 Feb 2024: President Ruto orders a house built for the family. 23 Feb 2024: funeral in Chepkorio, buried at his farm in Naiberi; Ruto and Seb Coe attended.
  A woman who said she had a child with him tried to stop the burial; the Eldoret High Court declined.
- Wife Asenath Cheruto Rotich; two children. 2025: his parents and widow in a dispute over the estate; reports say tens of millions of shillings frozen (allegations both ways).
- Kipchoge (BBC, May 2024): falsely accused online of involvement; death threats against his family; scared for his kids; lost most of his friends. "It did not happen."
- 13 Oct 2024: Ruth Chepng'etich dedicates her women's world record (2:09:56, Chicago) to him. Rotterdam 2024 held a minute's silence.
- 26 Apr 2026: Sabastian Sawe (Kenya) runs 1:59:30 in London, the first sub-2-hour marathon in a record-eligible race, breaking Kiptum's record.
  Yomif Kejelcha 1:59:41 behind him.
