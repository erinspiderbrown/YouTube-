**Counts for the script:**
- 15 WRs total: 7 indoor and 8 outdoor (WIKI-MD table).
- Each WR raised the bar by 1 cm (watchathletics 6.31 report: https://www.watchathletics.com/article/13635/armand-duplantis-soars-6-31m-to-break-pole-vault-world-record).
- **Outdoor WR every summer from 2022 to 2025:** 2022 (6.21), 2023 (6.23), 2024 (6.24, 6.25, 6.26) and 2025 (6.28, 6.29, 6.30). That's 8 outdoor WRs in 4 straight summers.
- **2026 is the first outdoor season since 2021 without a WR.**
  - Best outdoor mark: 6.25 in Zurich.
  - He missed 6.32 at Lausanne (2 tries), at Zurich, at Budapest (Gyulai) and at the Ultimate Championship.
  - Sources: WIKI-MD; https://worldathletics.org/competitions/diamond-league/news/lausanne-athletissima-diamond-league-2026-duplantis-karalis; https://worldathletics.org/competitions/world-athletics-ultimate-championship/2026/news/report/report-men-pole-vault-world-ultimate-championship-budapest-26; http://www.china.org.cn/2026-07/15/content_118599936.shtml.
- 2020 and 2021 had no outdoor WR. His 6.15 (Rome 2020) and 6.16 (2022) were outdoor world bests, not WRs (WIKI-MD).
- **Consecutive calendar years with any WR: 5 (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026).** 2021 had none, so the run starts in 2022. Counting 2020, he has set WRs in 6 of the last 7 years (WIKI-MD table).
- So the safe line is: "He broke the outdoor world record every summer from 2022 to 2025. In 2026, for the first time in five years, the summer ended without one."

  - Born 10 Nov 1999, Lafayette, Louisiana (WIKI-MD).

  - Started vaulting at age 4 at the family home. First age-group world best at 7. Cleared 3.86 at 10 (WIKI-MD).
  - Our WIKI-MD read doesn't mention the **backyard pit**. It's widely reported but **not yet sourced here**: find a source (e.g. Netflix/"Born to Fly" doc coverage) before using it.

- **Chose Sweden:** Announced in June 2015.
  - He had planned to vault for the US.
  - Swedish coach Jonas Anshelm invited Greg to coach, which influenced the choice (WIKI-MD).
- **LSU, 2018-19, one season:**
  - Collegiate records: 5.92 indoor, 6.00 outdoor.
  - Won SEC and NCAA indoor titles. Turned pro June 2019 (WIKI-MD).
- **Titles:**
  - Olympic gold: Tokyo 2020 (in 2021, 6.02) and Paris 2024 (6.00, then the 6.25 WR).
  - World outdoor gold: 2022, 2023, 2025. Silver: 2019 Doha.
  - World indoor gold: 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026.
  - European gold: 2018, 2022, 2024, 2026. European Indoor gold: 2021.
  - DL champion 2021-2025 (5 straight).
  - Ultimate Championship 2026.
  - Source: WIKI-MD.
- **Unbeaten 2025:** 16 competitions. First men's vaulter to go unbeaten in two straight years. Named World Athlete of the Year 2025 (WIKI-MD).

## B. EMMANOUIL "MANOLO" KARALIS (GRE)

### B1. Background

- **Born:** 20 Oct 1999, Athens. He's 3 weeks older than Mondo (WIKI-EK).
- **Club:** Olympiacos (since Oct 2021) (WIKI-EK).
- **Family:**
  - Father Charalambos (Haris) is a former decathlete from Pyrgos and his main coach.
  - Mother Sarah is a former long jumper from Uganda. Twin sister Angeliki is a heptathlete.
  - Sources: WIKI-EK; https://worldathletics.org/news/feature/emmanouil-karalis-greece-pole-vault-paris-nanjing-tokyo (18 Jan 2025).

  - Age he started: 12 per one bio site; matched his father's 4.80 PB at 15 per WA. **UNCONFIRMED.**
- **Coaching team:**
  - Father Charalambos, plus Marcin Szczepański (Piotr Lisek's coach). He trains with Lisek in Poland.
  - George Pomaski (Tentoglou's coach) is also involved (WA feature).
- **Firsts and other notes:**
  - At Silesia, Aug 2024, he became the 29th man over 6.00, and the first Greek and first Black vaulter to do it (WA feature).
  - He has spoken about racism and depression, and "one or two years" when he didn't perform as he wanted (WA feature).

## STORY SPINE (10 strongest verified facts)

1. In 2022-2025 he broke the outdoor WR every summer: 8 outdoor WRs (6.21 → 6.30), 15 WRs in all, each by 1 cm.
2. In March 2026 he went 6.31 indoors in Uppsala, his 15th WR, and then won his 4th straight World Indoor title.
3. Stockholm, 7 Jun 2026: Kurtis Marschall beat him in his home stadium, 5.90 to 5.80. That ended a 40-meet streak and was his first loss since 2023.
4. Right after that loss he said "unlucky in sports – lucky in love". He married Desiré Inglander five days later (12 Jun, Château de Castellaras), after a spring civil wedding and a move to Monaco (sold his Stockholm flat, reported 29 Apr).
5. London, Jul 2026: his left thigh "felt really tight", he stopped at 5.95 and Sam Kendricks won. That was his 2nd loss of the summer.
6. Brussels, 4 Sep: the same leg flared up in warm-up and he withdrew from the DL Final, ending a run of 5 straight DL titles.
7. Karalis (born 20 Oct 1999, Athens) won Brussels with a 6.12 meeting record, his first Diamond Trophy: "I'm living my dreams."
8. Karalis went 6.16 and then 6.20 in 2026. That makes him the No. 2 vaulter in history, and he attempted the WR height 6.32 himself in Zürich. He once no-heighted at the 2023 Worlds and went out in qualifying at the 2022 Worlds and Euros.
9. Mondo still won every championship in 2026 (World Indoors, Euros 6.15, Ultimate 6.20), and Karalis was 2nd at all three. Karalis has beaten Mondo only once, at the 2018 World Indoors.
10. No outdoor WR in 2026 (best 6.25), with 6.32 missed at four meets. In October he cancelled his entire 2027 indoor season: "I had far too much going on … not enough time to prepare."
