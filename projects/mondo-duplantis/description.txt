For 4 straight summers, Mondo Duplantis broke the pole vault world record outdoors. In 2025 he didn't lose once. Then 2026 happened: a loss at his home meet in Stockholm, a wedding, a leg injury, a withdrawal from the Diamond League Final, and no outdoor world record for the first time since 2021. Meanwhile Greece's Emmanouil Karalis cleared 6.20 and is now the 2nd-best pole vaulter in history. Is this the end of Mondo's era?
