# Leo & Lex Young: script v1 (user version, digits). Voice version: script_voice.md
Angle: the greatest HS team ever, the twins were the faces of it, and 3+ years into Stanford they're regular college runners. Facts: research.md. No injury talk (Lex's 2025 gap: no reason given, so only "didn't race").

## Part 1
Yesterday at NCAA Cross Country Nationals, Leo Young finished 117th. His twin brother Lex finished 154th. And if you followed high school running a few years ago, that should not make sense to you, 'cause these two was supposed to be the next big thing in American distance running. So what happened to the Young twins?

## Part 2
If you don't know who they are, Leo and Lex are Nico Young's little brothers. Nico's the Olympian from Northern Arizona. The twins grew up going to his meets, and they ended up at the same school he went to, Newbury Park in California. And Newbury Park wasn't just good. Track & Field News said people were calling it the greatest high school cross country team in American history. 4 state titles, a Nike Cross Nationals title, and the twins was right in the middle of all of it.

## Part 3
And individually they was crazy. Leo ran 13:38 for 3 miles in cross country, the fastest high school time ever. At RunningLane nationals in 2021 he ran 14:05 for 5K and Lex ran 14:05 right behind him. Then in 2023 Lex broke the national high school record in the 5000, 13:34.96. Leo ran 3:39 in the 1500. Stanford signed both of 'em, and their freshman year On gave 'em both NIL deals. So on paper, these was 2 future All-Americans at least.

## Part 4
Now here's where it gets real. They got to Stanford in the fall of 2023. That year at NCAA cross country Leo was 185th, Lex was 74th. 2024, Leo was 92nd, Lex was 63rd. 2025, Leo was 72nd, and Lex didn't race cross country at all. Then yesterday, Leo 117th and Lex 154th. That's 4 years and not one All-American between the 2 of 'em, in cross country or track.

## Part 5
And the one that really gets me is Lex. He ran 13:34.96 in the 5000 in high school. His best 5000 in college? 13:35.52. 3 years at Stanford and he still ain't run faster than he did as a high schooler. Then he missed the whole 2025 cross country season and indoor season after that, and Stanford never really said why.

## Part 6
Now I'ma be fair, 'cause I know somebody gon' say it in the comments. Leo did get better on the track. He dropped his mile to 3:56 and his 1500 to 3:35.96, and in 2025 he made the NCAA semifinal and missed the final by 0.26 of a second. Lex ran 27:48 for 10K and got 2nd at ACC. So they ain't bad. They're good college runners. But "good college runner" ain't what everybody was promising when they was in high school.

## Part 7
And it ain't like nobody from that era made it. Their brother Nico went to Northern Arizona and became an Olympian. So you gotta ask, was it Stanford? Was it the pressure of being Nico's brothers? Or was the hype just too much from the start? Honestly I think high school running blew 'em up too fast. You can't be the greatest ever at 17 and then just be regular at 21 without people talking.

So y'all tell me, did the Young twins fall off, or was they overhyped from day 1? Let me know in the comments.
