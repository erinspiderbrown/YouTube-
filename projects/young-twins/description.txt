Leo and Lex Young were the faces of Newbury Park, the team people called the greatest high school cross country team in American history. Leo ran 13:38 for 3 miles, Lex broke the national high school 5000 record in 13:34.96, and both went to Stanford with NIL deals. Yesterday at NCAA Cross Country nationals, Leo finished 117th and Lex 154th. So what happened to the Young twins?
