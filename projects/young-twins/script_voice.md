## Part 1
Yesterday at N C double-A cross country nationals, Leo Young finished a hundred and seventeenth. His twin brother Lex finished a hundred and fifty-fourth. And if you was followin' high school runnin' a few years ago, dat should not make no sense to you, 'cause dese two was supposed to be da next big thang in American distance runnin'. So what happened to da Young twins?

## Part 2
If you don't know who dey are, Leo and Lex is Nico Young little brother. Nico da Olympian from Northern Arizona. Da twins grew up goin' to his meets, and dey ended up at da same high school he went to, Newbury Park in California. And Newbury Park wasn't just good. Track and Field News said people was callin' it da greatest high school cross country team in American history. Four state titles, a Nike Cross Nationals title, and da twins was right in da middle of all of it.

## Part 3
And by deyself dey was crazy. Leo ran thirteen thirty-eight for three miles in cross country, da fastest high school time ever. At RunningLane nationals in twenty twenty-one, Leo ran fourteen oh five for five K, and Lex ran fourteen oh five right behind him. Then in twenty twenty-three, Lex broke da national high school record in da five thousand, thirteen thirty-four point nine six. Leo ran three thirty-nine in da fifteen hundred. Stanford signed both of 'em, and dey freshman year On gave both of 'em N I L deals. So on paper, dese was two future All-Americans at least.

## Part 4
I ain't gon' hold you, when dey got to Stanford I really thought one of 'em was gon' win a national title. But look at da numbers. Fall of twenty twenty-three, N C double-A cross country, Leo was a hundred and eighty-fifth, Lex was seventy-fourth. Twenty twenty-four, Leo ninety-second, Lex sixty-third. Twenty twenty-five, Leo seventy-second, and Lex ain't race cross country at all. Then yesterday, Leo a hundred and seventeenth, Lex a hundred and fifty-fourth. Dat's four years, and not one All-American between da two of 'em. Not in cross country, not on da track.

## Part 5
And da one dat really get me is Lex. He ran thirteen thirty-four point nine six in da five thousand in high school. His best five thousand in college? Thirteen thirty-five point five two. Three years at Stanford, and he still ain't ran faster den he did as a high schooler. Then he missed da whole twenty twenty-five cross country season and da indoor season after dat, and Stanford ain't never really say why.

## Part 6
And it ain't just dem. Stanford as a team had made N C double-A cross country thirty-one years straight, and in twenty twenty-five dat streak ended. Dis year dey made it back and finished seventeenth. So da whole program ain't been what it used to be. When you got two of da biggest high school names in da country and da team still sliding, people gon' start askin' questions.

## Part 7
Now I'ma be fair, 'cause I know somebody gon' say it in da comments. Leo did get better on da track. He dropped his mile to three fifty-six, and his fifteen hundred to three thirty-five point nine six, and in twenty twenty-five he made da N C double-A semifinal and missed da final by point two six of a second. Lex ran twenty-seven forty-eight for ten K and got second at A C C. So dey ain't bad. Dey good college runners. But "good college runner" ain't what everybody was promisin' when dey was in high school.

## Part 8
And look at dey brother. Nico went to Northern Arizona and became an Olympian. Same family, same high school. So you gotta ask, was it Stanford? Was it da pressure of bein' Nico Young brother? Or was da hype just too much from da jump? Honestly I think high school runnin' blew 'em up too fast. You can't be da greatest ever at seventeen and then just be regular at twenty-one without people talkin'.

So y'all tell me. Did da Young twins fall off, or was dey overhyped from day one? Let me know in da comments.
