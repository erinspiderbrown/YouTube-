6. **The protection question (coach Di Sheppard's "If I tried to make him super quick now, I'd break him").** Is the slow build smart, or is he being shielded? The channel already started this ("Ducking the Pros") but never as a proper doc.
7. **The money question.** Adidas US$4–6M, Vegemite deal, an agent at 15, yet his family declines every interview. Nobody has covered it.

### Fact check before scripting: read this first

## 6. Three structures for the times investigation (each with a cold open)

### A. "The Lab" (forensic, Ryan Searl method) — recommended, ~9 min
**Cold open:** fan-shot stands clip of the Sydney 200. The clock runs and stops on **19.67**. Freeze. The wind gauge "+1.7" fades in, then each finisher's PB ticks up next to their lane: 1, 2, 3 … 7. VO: "7 dudes PB'd in the same race. Nah. I gotta look at this."
- Part 1: "Out of nowhere?" His real progression chart (2023 20.87 → Dec 2024 20.04 → 2025 19.84w → 2026 19.67) against what's normal for a top junior. Is the jump unusual?
- Part 2: The 10.00. Conditions, meet size, the 0.30 gap to 2nd, +0.9 (legal, said plainly). Where his other 100s sit.
- Part 3: The 19.67. Wind near the limit, the whole field PB'ing, Murphy's 19.88, Gatlin's "do it overseas" soundbite.
- Part 4: Away from home. Oslo 20.60, Tokyo semi, Ostrava, plus Kennedy beating him at home. The "real level" table.
- Part 5: The forensic bit. Frame-time the 19.67 splits from fan video (method on screen, error margin stated), wind-adjusted estimates (state the model used).
- Verdict: "The times are legal, ain't nobody saying he cheated, but I really feel like 19.67 ain't who he is yet." Number on screen: his "real" range.

### B. "Two Seasons" (home vs away), ~8 min
**Cold open:** split screen. Left: Sydney, crowd roaring, 19.67. Right: Oslo, quiet, 20.60, 6th. VO: "Same dude. Same year. 2 months apart."
- The whole video is a growing table: every 2025–26 race, with home/away, wind, field size and time. Each row gets its clip.
- Middle: why home marks can be faster (wind, meet scheduling, timing, field), using the facts.
- Ending: the table average versus the headline marks, which is the verdict.

### C. "Out Of Nowhere?" (story-led), ~9 min
**Cold open:** 60 Minutes, coach Di Sheppard: *"Watch me, I'm going to make that one a champion."* Cut to the 20.04 "THAT IS NOT HUMAN" race call, then to the headline "faster than Bolt". VO asks: "Did he really come outta nowhere, or did the times get ahead of him?"
- Follows his career in order (Ipswich Grammar → 20.04 → Adidas → Kennedy losses → 19.67 → Oslo → injury), and at every big mark stops on a conditions check (a "stamp" of wind / meet / field, done in clean modern type).
- Ends on the injury and no racing since June: "we won't know his real level 'til 2027."

## 7. The 8 editing and storytelling techniques that matter most here

1. **Race clocks plus a live wind readout** on every race clip, in a clean modern sans: the timer runs with the footage and stops on the official mark, the wind number (+0.9, +1.7, "w" for aided) sits beside it. The whole case lives in these two numbers.
2. **Frame-timing on screen (the Searl method).** Show the frame counter, the start frame (smoke/first movement) and the finish frame, and the error margin in small text. This is what made the 502K video believable.
3. **The "real level" table that builds through the video:** each race is a row (date, meet, home/away, wind, time). Smooth row slide-ins, the headline marks highlighted, the average at the end. Same panel style as the Bolt-age comparison idea.
4. **Field-PB lane callouts:** motion-tracked thin labels on each lane in the Sydney 200 ("MURPHY 19.88 PB", "PB", "PB"…) that pop in, cleanly, as each crosses the line. One shot that makes the point without a word.
5. **Word-synced interview subtitles** on every soundbite (Gatlin's challenge, Di Sheppard, Gout on 60 Minutes, Lyles, Australian press). About 15 per 6 min of VO, per the house recipe.
6. **Split screen home vs away** (Sydney vs Oslo, QSAC vs Ostrava) with both clocks running together. Desaturate the away side slightly at the low point. Speed-ramp into slow-mo at each finish.
7. **Silence before each number.** Kill the dark bed for ~1 s before every reveal (10.00, the 0.30 gap, 19.67, 7 PBs, 20.60). Fresh ElevenLabs score per chapter, ducked under the voice. SFX only on the number reveals, spaced per the house rules.
8. **Evidence chapter cards and clear source credits.** Minimal case-file cards per part ("Part 2 – The 10.00") with a modern digital transition, and every fact credited bottom-right (World Athletics, Australian Athletics results, ABC). This protects the verdict: every claim on screen has a source.
