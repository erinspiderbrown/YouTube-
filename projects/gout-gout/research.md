## Time investigation

Short answer: **the 19.67 is official and ratified.** No source reports a wind-gauge fault, timing fault, short track, wrong lane or hand timing. What *is* true and checkable: (a) the whole final ran huge PBs, far quicker than the same men ran in the heats that day; (b) wind on the day swung around: the women's 200m final just before had an illegal +2.3, and the men's heats ranged from +0.4 to -2.3; (c) Gout has not come near 19.67 again. His next-best 2026 200m was 20.11, in the heat that morning, and his other 2026 200m times were 20.42-20.60. Several claims in the brief are wrong (see each item).

### 1. Season opener: the 10.00 100m: TRUE, legal

- **10.00 (+0.9 m/s)**, Sat **21 Feb 2026**, **Dane Bird-Smith Shield Meet**, QSAC State Athletics Facility, Nathan, Brisbane. It was "Final 1", one of several timed finals (graded heats run as finals). **Wind +0.9 is legal** (limit +2.0).
  https://worldathletics.org/competition/calendar-results/results/7236666 ; https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-02-21/gout-gout-sets-another-record-with-10-second-100m/106372384
- It was his first race of 2026 ("his first race at the distance in 2026", "first 100m race of 2026"). https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/gout-gout-dazzles-with-record-equalling-100m-time
- Records: an Australian U20 record (old: Jake Doran 10.15, 2018); equal-fastest legal 100m by an Australian on home soil (equal with Lachlan Kennedy, Perth, April 2025); 3rd on the Australian all-time list behind Patrick Johnson 9.93 (2003) and Kennedy 9.98 (Nairobi 2025). Doran on Instagram: "It was only a matter of time! Proud it took someone of Gout's calibre to eclipse my mark!" Same AAP link; ABC link above.
- **Weather:** none of the sources reports the weather or conditions that day. **[FLAG]** The meet's other wind readings vary a lot: 100m finals +0.9, -0.3, 0.0, +1.4, +0.4, +1.4, +2.8; 200m finals +2.1, -0.2, +0.8, +0.6, -0.2, +1.1, +1.9 (WA results page above).
- **The field (Final 1, +0.9):** 1 Gout 10.00 (AJR) · 2 Pais Wisil (PNG) 10.30 · 3 Uwezo Lubenda 10.39 · 4 Hugh Kabengele 10.48 · 5 Theodor Young (RSA) 10.53 · 6 Hugh Sharman 10.63 · 7 Samuel Galstuch Magalhaes 10.68 · 8 Mogammad Ebrahim Smith (NZL) 10.71 · Rory Easton DQ. (WA results above)
  - Not a "whole field PB" race: Gout won by 0.30. Wisil had already run **10.24 (+1.2)** at the Queensland Champs in March 2025, so his 10.30 was not a PB. https://worldathletics.org/competition/calendar-results/results/7216844?eventId=10229630
  - PB status of the other finishers: **not found [FLAG]**. Lubenda (b. 2009) ran 10.40 U18 in March and 10.37 in April 2026 (see below), so 10.39 is in line with his level.

## Story beats (setups and payoffs)

1. **Cold open:** the 19.67 crossing the line, then the internet: "clearly fake." (If that quote is the user's own, the open can be the user re-examining their own claim.)
2. **Setup:** Peter Norman's 20.06 stood 56 years. A 16-year-old from Ipswich breaks it in a school meet (20.04, Dec 2024), and the "next Bolt" machine starts: Adidas, 60 Minutes, Bolt asked about him.
3. **Setup:** the wind keeps showing up. 9.99w twice, 19.98w, 19.84w (+2.2): all "illegal" sub-20s and sub-10s. **Payoff:** Gout himself says "I've been chasing it ever since I got that illegal sub-20... a big weight off my shoulders knowing I ran it legally."
4. **The test abroad:** "Do it outside Australia" (Gatlin). **Payoff, part 1:** he already had: 20.02 in still air in Ostrava, 2025. **Payoff, part 2:** in 2026 he went to Oslo and ran 20.60, 6th, schooled by Tebogo.
5. **The investigation:** the final's numbers. Every finalist was 0.26-0.74 s faster than in their morning heat. Seven PBs. Murphy from 20.41 to 19.88. Women's final just before had an illegal +2.3. The gauge on the straight can't see the bend. But: ratified, no equipment fault, and Murphy backed it up with 20.05 in Darwin.
6. **Verdict:** real time, real wind reading, probably "unicorn conditions". LetsRun puts it at about 19.79 in still air: still elite at 18, but not the 19.67 he can be expected to repeat.
7. **The Knighton mirror:** the last teen who ran this fast (19.49, never ratified) is now banned until about 2029. Prodigies don't follow straight lines.
8. **Twist and ending:** the grade-3 hamstring tear, July 2026. No U20 gold, no Glasgow. The real test is now 2027 in Beijing, then LA 2028, then the home Olympics in Brisbane 2032 at 24, when Sheppard says sprinters peak. "If I tried to make him super quick now I'd break him."
