## 1. Coach Di (Diane) Sheppard: what's reported

**Main source: CBS 60 Minutes (Jon Wertheim), broadcast/published 10 May 2026**
https://www.cbsnews.com/news/gout-gout-australian-sprinting-phenom-drawing-comparisons-to-usain-bolt-60-minutes-transcript/
- "She has no formal track background."
- "But her son was a fast kid with big ambitions."
- "in the early 2000s, when he was a student at Ipswich Grammar, she inquired as to how to become a track coach at the school." She was told only school employees could coach.
- "She quit her job at a supermarket and took a job handing out school uniforms, so she'd be eligible."
- When Gout was in the equivalent of 7th grade, about 12 or 13, Sheppard "(then, as now, the school's track coach) spotted him racing classmates." Quotes: "Who's the other kid? Who's that? I want him." / "I looked at him and just went, 'Oh my God.'" / "From that day, I saw him."
- She is Gout's coach, "the only one he's ever had". She told the headmaster she'd make him a star: "He thought I was mucking around." She is also described as a grandmother in her 60s (CBS write-up syndicated on Yahoo: https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fastest-teen-ever-200m-got-230026260.html). Other quotes: "good cop, bad cop" (she is the bad cop), "If I tried to make him super quick now, I'd break him."

**Joseph Deng (800m, Australian record holder), an earlier athlete she coached**
- Born 1998 in Kenya. Moved to Toowoomba in 2004 and to Ipswich in 2010. A friend got him to go to after-school athletics at Ipswich Grammar, where Di Sheppard coached him. He later won a scholarship to the school and finished there in 2016. https://www.igsoldboys.com/?p=23676
- Wikipedia (citing the Athletics Australia profile): Sheppard ran after-school coaching at Ipswich Grammar and kept coaching him on his scholarship. After his 1:46.51 PB "later in 2016" he moved to Melbourne to train with Justin Rinaldi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Deng
- So she coached Deng from about 2010 to 2016, years before Gout started at the school (high school from 2020, per https://www.athletics.com.au/athlete/gout-gout/). [FLAG on the exact years: no source states them; they are inferred from 2010 to 2016.]

**How she found Gout (accounts differ in the details)**
- CBS: she saw him racing classmates at about 12 or 13 (link above). The Yahoo/CBS write-up says she saw him "running around with friends" and called him over.
- Athletics Weekly, 5 Dec 2025: he was 13 and had tagged along with a mate trying out for the school athletics team. Her quote: "I saw him running on the oval and there was just something about him and the way he moved." https://athleticsweekly.com/news/the-extraordinary-rise-of-gout-gout-1040009046/
- AAP, c. Mar 2025: he was at Ipswich Grammar to play soccer, had never raced on a track, and ran in sand shoes against James Grey (a carnival selection trial) and "left him in his dust" (schoolmate Tyson Walker). Sheppard "quickly spotted" him. https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/schoolyard-sprint-that-hurtled-gout-towards-greatness [FLAG: no date on the page.]
- An interhouse carnival at age 12 is mentioned in secondary summaries, but I could not find a primary source for it. [FLAG]

**Other**
- She trains athletes every afternoon on the bottom oval at Ipswich Grammar and has never advertised. She was one of three 2024 Queensland Athletics Coach of the Year winners. https://coachathletics.com.au/coaching-education/the-coaching-approach-of-di-sheppard

## 2. Adidas deal (signed 28 Oct 2024)
- No dollar figure was ever officially confirmed by adidas, Gout or manager James Templeton. I found no official denial either. Templeton's quotes (to the SMH, relayed by the Daily Mail and Pulse) are only about adidas's "great patience" and keeping Gout in Australia, never about the money. https://www.pulse.co.ke/athletics/story/breaking-down-gout-gouts-lucrative-adidas-deal-how-much-is-the-sprint-prodigy-earning-2024122103572898676
- **"Over $6 million"**: attributed to "reports by the Daily Mail" (Pulse Sports, 21 Dec 2024, same link). It reportedly runs to the 2032 Brisbane Olympics, with performance bonuses. Sportface (Italy) attributes the same figure to the Guardian. https://www.sportface.it/atletica-leggera/gout-gout-contratto-record-adidas-nuovo-bolt/2763115 [FLAG: I could not find the Daily Mail original.]
- **"More than $4 million over eight years"**: CBS 60 Minutes, 10 May 2026: "An Adidas deal reportedly pays Gout a base of more than $4 million over eight years." Note the word "reportedly". https://www.cbsnews.com/news/gout-gout-australian-sprinting-phenom-drawing-comparisons-to-usain-bolt-60-minutes-transcript/
- "Multi-year, multi-million-dollar" is the safest wording (Local Ipswich News: https://localipswichnews.com.au/news/gout-gout-farewell/).
- Safe script wording: "reportedly worth millions". Don't state $6M or $4M as fact.
