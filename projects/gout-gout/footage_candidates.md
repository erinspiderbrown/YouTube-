## Facts to anchor the script (checked against sources found)
- **Born** 29 Dec 2007, Ipswich QLD. He's the 2nd of 7 kids of **Bona and Monica**, who fled the South Sudan civil war and came to Brisbane in 2005. The family name is really **Guot**; Sudanese paperwork misspelled it (Sunrise, Dec 2024). His dad runs a dishwashing operation at the local hospital and his mum works in the home (60 Minutes). **His parents decline all interviews.** He bought the family a house (Nova, Dec 2025).
- **Coach Diane "Di" Sheppard**, the only coach he's ever had. She spotted him racing at an Ipswich Grammar carnival when he was about 12. Years earlier she quit a supermarket job and took a job in the school's uniform shop, because only staff could coach. She has no formal track background. **Manager James Templeton.** **adidas** deal (reported 6 years, base of more than $4M over 8 years per 60 Minutes). He graduated from Ipswich Grammar in Dec 2025 with straight A's, was a prefect, and is taking a gap year in 2026.
- **Times:**
  - 2023: 20.87, an Australian U18 record at 15.
  - Lima World U20s (Aug 2024): silver, behind Bayanda Walaza.
  - Australian All Schools, QSAC Brisbane, **7 Dec 2024: 20.04 (+1.5)**. It broke Peter Norman's 20.06 from Mexico City 1968 and beat Bolt's age-16 best of 20.13. His heat was 20.38 (+2.5). In the 100 he ran 10.04w (+3.4) in the heat and 10.17 (+0.9) in the final.
  - 2025: QLD champs 19.98w (+3.6). Perth Nationals 19.84w (+2.2) and 9.99w in the 100. **Ostrava, 24 Jun 2025: 20.02**, his NR at the time. Monaco DL U23 race: won. **Tokyo Worlds 2025:** 20.23, 3rd in his heat, then out in the semi (4th in his semi). His last school race was the GPS 400 in 46.18.
  - **2026:**
    - 21 Feb, Dane Bird-Smith Shield, QSAC: 100m **10.00 (9.995, +0.9)**, an Australian U20 record.
    - 14–15 Mar, QLD champs: 100 10.28w/10.20. 200 semi 20.59, final **20.42 (-2.1)**. He said he was ill.
    - 28 Mar, Maurie Plant Melbourne, in pouring rain: **lost to Lachlan Kennedy 20.38 to 20.43**, the 2nd time Kennedy had beaten him at 200.
    - 12 Apr, Sydney Nationals: heat 20.11 (+0.4), **final 19.67 (+1.7)**, a world U20 record and the first legal sub-20 by an Australian. Murphy ran 19.88 and 7 of the 8 finishers ran PBs.
    - 17–18 Apr, Junior champs: 10.19, 10.44 (-2.2), 10.21.
    - 10 Jun, Oslo DL debut: **6th, 20.60** (Tebogo won in 19.84).
    - 16 Jun, Ostrava 150m: 3rd, 14.96, behind Lyles' 14.67 world best.
    - Early Jul: **grade 3 left hamstring tear** in training in Brisbane. That ended his season: no World U20s in Eugene, no Commonwealth Games (he had already withdrawn in Feb). He plans to return in 2027. World Athletics has since **ratified the 19.67**.
