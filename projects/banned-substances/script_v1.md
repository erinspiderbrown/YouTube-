## 01 · Stanozolol [Ben Johnson 1988 · Dominique Mulamba 2024]
Number one. Stanozolol. Street name Winstrol.

It's a steroid built for speed. More muscle, more strength, faster recovery, and it don't make you puff up with water weight. That's why sprinters loved it.

And the most famous bust in history came from it. Ben Johnson. Nineteen eighty-eight. Olympic hundred final. Nine seventy-nine. World record. Beat Carl Lewis.

Days later, the test come back. Stanozolol. Gold stripped. Record gone. Then he admit to the Canadian inquiry he been usin' steroids for years, so they took his nine eighty-three from the year before too.

And the crazy part? Look at that final. Carl Lewis, Linford Christie, Dennis Mitchell, Desai Williams. All of 'em got tied to a doping story at some point. They call it the dirtiest race in history.

Think that's old news? Paris twenty twenty-four. The sprinter carryin' Congo's flag at the openin' ceremony, Dominique Mulamba. Tested positive for stanozolol the day after his heat. Four years.

## 02 · EPO [Rashid Ramzi · Rita Jeptoo · Asbel Kiprop · Albert Korir]
Number two. EPO.

Your kidneys make a hormone that tells your body, make more red blood cells. EPO is the fake version. More red blood cells, more oxygen to your muscles, and you can run longer before you hit the wall. So this one ain't for sprinters. It's for the distance crowd.

Beijing twenty-oh-eight. Rashid Ramzi win the fifteen hundred. They retest his samples later for a newer version called CERA. Positive. Gold medal gone, handed to Asbel Kiprop.

And Kiprop? Three-time world champ. Years later, he caught with EPO too. Four years.

Rita Jeptoo won Boston and Chicago in twenty fourteen. EPO. Lost both.

And it ain't slowin' down. Over a hundred forty Kinyans been sanctioned since twenty seventeen. This year Albert Korir, former New York Marathon champ, got five years for CERA. And just last month, Olympic marathon champ Peres Jepchirchir got provisionally suspended for EPO. That case still open.

They been testin' for EPO since Sydney two thousand. People still keep gettin' caught.

## 03 · HGH [Tim Montgomery · Blessing Okagbare · Antonio Pettigrew]
Number three. HGH. Human growth hormone.

It don't really make you stronger like a steroid. What it do is help you recover. Tendons, ligaments, muscle, all heal faster, so you can train harder, more often.

Tim Montgomery ran nine seventy-eight in twenty-oh-two. World record. He never failed a drug test. They got him off documents from the BALCO investigation. Record wiped. And in twenty-oh-eight he admit it. HGH and testosterone.

Antonio Pettigrew admit HGH and EPO under oath, and the whole U S A four-by-four from Sydney lost their gold.

And the most recent big one? Blessing Okagbare. Olympic medalist. Tokyo twenty twenty-one, she win her hundred heat, then they pull her before the semis. HGH and EPO. Eleven-year ban.

## 04 · THG [Marion Jones · Dwain Chambers · Regina Jacobs]
Number four. THG. They called it "the clear."

This was a designer steroid. Cooked up in a lab at BALCO for one reason. The drug tests couldn't see it. You could be on it and pass every test.

So how they catch a drug nobody could see? In twenty-oh-three a coach mailed a used syringe to the anti-doping people. Anonymous. Turns out it was Trevor Graham. Coach of Marion Jones, Tim Montgomery and Justin Gatlin. His own athletes' world.

The lab cracked it, built a test, and went back through old samples. Dwain Chambers, European champ, positive. Regina Jacobs, positive.

And Marion Jones? She never failed a test either. In twenty-oh-seven she admit she was on the clear in Sydney. Three golds, two bronzes. All five gone. And she did six months in federal prison for lyin' to investigators.

## 05 · Nandrolone [Linford Christie · Dieter Baumann · Shelby Houlihan]
Number five. Nandrolone.

Classic injectable steroid. Muscle and strength. And here's the twist: your body make a tiny bit of it naturally. So every time somebody get caught, you get the wildest excuses.

Linford Christie, Olympic hundred champ. Positive in nineteen ninety-nine. Two years.

Dieter Baumann, Olympic five thousand champ. Said somebody spiked his toothpaste. Two years.

And the best one? Shelby Houlihan. American record holder. Twenty twenty-one, she said it came from a pork burrito from a food truck. The court ain't buy it. Four years.

## 06 · Oral-Turinabol and Dianabol [East Germany · Heidi Krieger · Athens 2004 throwers]
Number six. And this the one most videos get wrong.

Dianabol is the old-school steroid pill. Made in America in the fifties. Fast strength, fast size. Two Belarusian throwers from Athens twenty-oh-four, Ivan Tsikhan and Iryna Yatchenko, lost their medals on retests for it.

But East Germany? They had they own pill. Oral-Turinabol. Little blue pill made by a state company.

Nineteen seventy-four, the government started State Plan fourteen twenty-five. Up to ten thousand athletes, some of 'em kids, got handed these pills and was told they was vitamins.

They won everything. Some of the world records from that era still standin' today.

And the cost? Shot putter Heidi Krieger got pumped full of these pills as a teenager. Later in life Heidi became Andreas Krieger, and he said the drugs played a part. A lot of these athletes got sick later. A lot of 'em never knew what they was takin'.

## 07 · Clenbuterol [Katrin Krabbe · Carina Horn]
Number seven. Clenbuterol.

It's a asthma drug in some countries and a drug for cows in others. It burns fat and helps you hold on to muscle. Lighter, leaner, same power.

Katrin Krabbe. Nineteen ninety-one, she the double world champ in the hundred and two hundred. Biggest star in Germany. Then nineteen ninety-two, clenbuterol, with two of her teammates. It wasn't even on the list by name back then, so they got her for unsporting behavior instead. Three years. Career over.

And the meat thing is real. In Mexico and China, clenbuterol show up in beef. That's the excuse you hear a lot. South Africa's record holder Carina Horn got caught with it in twenty twenty-two. Second offense. Six years.

## 08 · GHRP-6 [no track case · Marin Premeru GHRP-2]
Number eight. GHRP-6.

This is a peptide. Instead of takin' growth hormone, you take this and it tricks your own body into dumpin' it out. And it's out your system in like a day or two.

Side effect? It make you starvin'. Like, can't stop eatin' hungry.

And the honest truth? Ain't no famous track athlete ever been caught on GHRP-6. The closest was a Croatian shot putter, Marin Premeru, caught on GHRP-2, same family. Four years. The other GHRP-6 cases? UFC fighters.

Now you can take that two ways. Either nobody in track using it. Or it's just that hard to catch.

## 09 · Meldonium [Abeba Aregawi · Endeshaw Negesse · Maria Sharapova]
Number nine. Meldonium.

It's a heart drug from Latvia. It's supposed to make your body burn sugar instead of fat, which take less oxygen. Do it actually make you faster? The science say probably not much. But a lotta athletes was takin' it.

So January first, twenty sixteen, it got banned. And then boom. Positive tests everywhere.

Abeba Aregawi, the twenty thirteen world fifteen hundred champ, positive. The suspension got lifted, but she never ran again. Tokyo Marathon winner Endeshaw Negesse, positive.

And the most famous one wasn't even track. Maria Sharapova. Tennis. Fifteen-month ban.

## 10 · Amphetamine [Justin Gatlin 2001 · Luvo Manyonga]
Number ten. Amphetamine.

Pure stimulant. It floods your brain with dopamine. You feel locked in, aggressive, and you don't feel tired or hurt. And that's the dangerous part. Your body tryna tell you to stop, and you can't hear it.

Justin Gatlin's first strike came from this. Two thousand one, he a college kid. It was his ADHD medicine. Two years, cut to one.

Luvo Manyonga, South African long jumper. Twenty twelve, positive for meth. Eighteen months. Then he came back and won the twenty seventeen world title. Then got banned again in twenty twenty-one for missin' tests.

## 11 · Testosterone [Dennis Mitchell · Justin Gatlin 2006 · Tyson Gay]
Number eleven. Testosterone. The O G. Every steroid on this list is basically a copy of this.

They test it with a ratio. Testosterone against another hormone called epitestosterone. Normal is about one to one. Over four to one and they start lookin' at you.

Dennis Mitchell, Olympic bronze medalist, got caught in nineteen ninety-eight. And his excuse is legendary. He said the night before, he had five beers and sex with his wife at least four times. The U S federation cleared him. The world federation still gave him two years.

Justin Gatlin, strike two. Twenty-oh-six. He the reigning Olympic and world champ, just tied the world record at nine seventy-seven. Testosterone. Four years.

Tyson Gay, one of the fastest men ever, twenty thirteen. He said it was from a cream. The U S team lost their twenty twelve four-by-one silver because of it.

## 12 · Ephedrine [Carl Lewis · Ato Boldon]
Number twelve. Ephedrine.

This one in regular cold medicine and diet pills. Little jolt of energy, little focus. It's only banned on race day, and only over a certain amount.

And here's the wild one. Carl Lewis. Nineteen eighty-eight Olympic Trials. He tested positive for small amounts of ephedrine and two other stimulants. The U S Olympic Committee said it was accidental, from a herbal supplement, and cleared him. Nobody found out till twenty-oh-three.

So the same summer Ben Johnson got caught, the man who got his gold medal had his own positive test. Kept quiet for fifteen years.

Ato Boldon, four Olympic medals, got a warning for ephedrine in twenty-oh-one.

## 14 · Diuretics [Ruth Chepngetich · Ben Johnson 1999 · Steve Mullings]
Number fourteen. Diuretics.

These ain't for speed. They make you pee. A lot. That waters down your urine, so any other drug in there harder to find. That's why they banned all year round. They called masking agents.

The biggest case right now? Ruth Chepngetich. Women's marathon world record holder. Two hours nine minutes. Twenty twenty-five, she test positive for a diuretic called H C T Z. She said she took her housemaid's pills. Three years. The record stand, 'cause it came before the test.

Remember Ben Johnson? Strike three was a diuretic. Nineteen ninety-nine. Banned for life.

Same thing for Jamaican sprinter Steve Mullings. Second offense. Life.

## 15 · Captagon [no track case · Bundesliga]
Number fifteen. Captagon.

It's a pill your body breaks down into two stimulants, amphetamine and another one called theophylline. It was made in nineteen sixty-one as a medicine. Not for sports.

And keep it real, ain't no track athlete on record caught with it. The famous story is soccer. A German goalie wrote a book in the eighties sayin' it was all over the Bundesliga, and team doctors later admitted they gave it to players.

Today it's a street drug in the Middle East. That's where most of it go now.

## 16 · IGF-1 [no track case · Vijay Singh]
Number sixteen. IGF-1.

When you take growth hormone, IGF-1 is the thing that actually do the work. Grows tissue, builds protein.

Ain't no track athlete ever been banned for it. Why? Because your body make it naturally, so it's crazy hard to prove you took extra.

The most famous case was golf. Vijay Singh admitted usin' a deer antler spray that had IGF-1 in it. He got cleared.

So when you hear "nobody's been caught," that don't always mean nobody's usin' it.
