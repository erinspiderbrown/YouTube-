## Facts behind the captions (check before publishing)
- Ben Johnson: stanozolol, Seoul 1988, 9.79 world record and gold stripped.
- Marion Jones: admitted in 2007 to using "the clear" (THG, from BALCO). Her Sydney 2000 medals were stripped.
- Justin Gatlin: testosterone positive in 2006 (4-year ban). His 2001 positive was for amphetamine from ADHD medication.
- Sha'Carri Richardson: THC, 2021 US Olympic Trials, which kept her out of Tokyo.
- Katrin Krabbe: clenbuterol, 1992.
- Elena Lashmanova: GW1516, 2014 sample, 2-year ban.
- LaShawn Merritt: DHEA (from the ExtenZe supplement), 2010.
- Dwain Chambers: THG, 2003.
- Christian Coleman is left out on purpose: his ban was for whereabouts failures, not a substance.
