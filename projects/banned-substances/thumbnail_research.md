## 6. What to do
1. **Keep the grid.** It's the format the 32x original and every 25-60x "every X" outlier use. The grid tells viewers "this is a complete list" before they read anything.
2. **Upgrade the tiles from object to face + drug.** Following the BTS 59x and "13 Track Stars Banned" 26x pattern, each tile shows a real athlete with the substance name as the caption, plus a red "BANNED"/"CAUGHT" stamp. That shows the new hook in the image.
3. **Short caption per tile (1-2 words), big and high-contrast.** The original's captions name effects (RAT POISON, "GHOST" DRUG) because a strange effect is more clickable than a chemical name. Use drug names that people know (STEROIDS, EPO, THC, TESTOSTERONE) or famous ones (STANOZOLOL / THE CLEAR).
4. **One-hero alternative:** the CAMMA "steroids." pattern (369K, 6.8x) is one face with one huge word. For us that's Ben Johnson with 9.79 and STANOZOLOL.
5. **Optional title bar** like OnlyBiology's "EVERY **STEROID** RANKED" (33x), with one keyword coloured. Our neon cyan/orange style can carry it.
6. Avoid: Christian Coleman (his ban was for whereabouts failures, not a substance), and AI-made drug images (the user wants real photos).
