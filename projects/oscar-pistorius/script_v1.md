# Oscar Pistorius voiceover script v1

Working title: **The Olympic Sprinter Who Shot His Girlfriend Through A Door** (vidIQ 96)

Style and settings, the same as the approved Drummond v4 read:
- Same voice settings as Drummond v4: `[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent]` on every paragraph, stability 0.45, similarity 0.85, eleven_v4.
- Target 165–170 wpm, about 9 minutes.
- A real person was killed, so there are no jokes in sections 05–07. Reeva is named and treated as a person, not a plot point.
- Every fact is checked against news and court reporting; see `README.md` → Facts and sources.

## 01 Cold open
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Aight, so check it. Valentine's Day, 2013. Pretoria, South Africa. Sun ain't even up yet.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Inside a house in a gated estate, a man grab a nine millimeter… walk up to a locked bathroom door… and fire four shots through it.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Behind that door was his girlfriend. Reeva Steenkamp. Twenty-nine years old.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And the man holdin' the gun? Six months before that night, he was one of the most inspirin' athletes on the planet. A double amputee who ran in the Olympics. On blades.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] They called him the Blade Runner. Oscar Pistorius.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He say it was a accident. Say he thought she was a intruder. The courts went back and forth on it for years… and the final answer caught a lotta people off guard.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] So how do one of the most inspirin' sprinters in the world end up in prison for murder? Mane… lemme tell y'all.

## 02 Who he was
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Oscar Pistorius was born in Johannesburg in 1986. And he was born without the fibula in both legs… that's the outside bone in your lower leg.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] So at eleven months old, both his legs got amputated below the knee.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] His mama, Sheila, raised him like ain't nothin' was different. The way Oscar told it, in the mornin' she'd tell his brother to put his shoes on… and tell Oscar to put his legs on. That was it. No special treatment.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He played rugby, water polo, all of it. Then in 2002, when he was fifteen… his mama passed. Years later, even at the Olympics, he was still talkin' 'bout her.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Not long after that, he found track. And it ain't take long. 2004, Athens Paralympics. Seventeen years old. He win gold in the 200… and bronze in the 100.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] But Oscar ain't wanna just be the best Paralympian. He wanted to race the able-bodied dudes. He wanted the Olympics.

## 03 The fight to run
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And that's where the sport pushed back.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] January 2008, the IAAF… that's the folks who run track… ruled his carbon fiber blades gave him a advantage. Banned him from able-bodied races.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Oscar took it all the way to the Court of Arbitration for Sport. And four months later, he won. The court said the IAAF couldn't prove them blades gave him a edge.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He missed the time for Beijing… but he ain't quit. And in 2012, he made it.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] London. August 2012. Oscar Pistorius become the first double amputee to ever run track at the Olympics.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He made it out his heat in the 400 and into the semis. He finished last in that semi… but right after the race, Kirani James… the dude who went on to win the whole thing… walked over and swapped name bibs with him.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Then Oscar ran anchor for South Africa in the 4 by 4 final. The whole stadium was on they feet.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Mane, that was the peak. Nike deals, magazine covers, the face of the Paralympics. The most inspirin' story in sports.

## 04 The cracks
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] But if you was payin' attention… you could see some cracks.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] A few weeks after the Olympics, at the London Paralympics, Oscar lost the 200 final to Brazil's Alan Oliveira. By seven hundredths. His first 200 loss in nine years.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And right there on live TV, Oscar went off. Said Oliveira's blades was too long… said it wasn't a fair race. The next day, he apologized for the timin'… but he ain't back off what he said.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Then there was the guns. Oscar was big on guns. And in January 2013, at a restaurant in Johannesburg, a pistol went off under the table where he was sittin'. Restaurant full of people. Kids at the next table. A court later found him guilty for that shot.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] That was one month before Valentine's Day.

## 05 Valentine's Day 2013
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] So who was Reeva Steenkamp?

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Reeva was a model from Port Elizabeth. She had a law degree. She'd been on magazine covers, on TV… and her and Oscar had been datin' for about three months.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] February 14th, 2013. Before the sun come up, inside Oscar's house in Pretoria… four shots go through a locked bathroom door. Three of 'em hit Reeva. She ain't make it.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Here's Oscar's side. He say he woke up in the middle of the night, heard a noise in the bathroom, and thought somebody broke in. Say he grabbed his gun without his legs on, and fired 'cause he was scared for his life. And he say he thought Reeva was still in the bed.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Then he say he realized she wasn't in the bed… and he broke the door down with a cricket bat.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] By then, it was too late.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] The next day, he was charged with murder. And the whole world was stuck on the same question… did he really think it was a intruder?

## 06 The trial
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] March 2014, the trial start in Pretoria. And it's on live TV. The whole country… really the whole world… watchin' every day.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] The prosecutor, Gerrie Nel, went at him hard. He said this wasn't no accident. Said they had a argument that night, and Reeva ran in that bathroom to get away from him.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Neighbors said they heard a woman screamin'. Oscar said that wasn't her… that was him, screamin' for help.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Oscar cried on the stand. Got sick in the courtroom. And in one moment that hit everybody… he read out loud the Valentine's card Reeva wrote him. She never got to give it to him. And it said she loved him.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] September 2014. Judge Thokozile Masipa made the call. Not guilty of murder. Guilty of culpable homicide… that's basically manslaughter. She said he was negligent… but the state ain't prove he meant to kill.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Five years. And after about a year in prison… he got sent home on house arrest.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] But the prosecutors wasn't done.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] December 2015, South Africa's Supreme Court of Appeal threw that verdict out and changed it to murder. And here's how they put it. You fire four shots into a tiny bathroom with somebody in it… you know somebody could die. Don't matter who you thought was behind that door. The judge said Oscar gambled with a life.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Then it went back to Judge Masipa for sentencin'… and she gave him six years. For murder. In a country where the minimum for murder is fifteen.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Prosecutors called that shockingly lenient. They appealed again. And in November 2017, the appeals court more than doubled it. Thirteen years and five months.

## 07 Parole & ending
[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] In 2023, Oscar went up for parole and got denied. Later that year, he went back up… and this time, he got it.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] January 5th, 2024… Oscar Pistorius walked out of prison. But he ain't free free. He on parole till 2029. He gotta do anger management and programs on violence against women. No alcohol. And he ain't allowed to talk to the media.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Reeva's dad, Barry, passed away in 2023… before Oscar ever got out. And her mama, June, said somethin' I can't shake. She said the family… they the ones servin' a life sentence.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] So where I'm at with it? I'ma keep it a buck. I wasn't in that house that night. Nobody watchin' this was. But the highest court that heard this case said it plain… even if he really thought it was a intruder, you don't fire four shots through a locked door and call it a accident.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And that's the part of this story that get me. Everything Oscar did on the track was 'bout provin' folks wrong. They said he couldn't run with able-bodied athletes… he did it anyway. They said the blades was a advantage… he beat that in court.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He spent his whole life fightin' to be seen as more than what people thought he was.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Now? Ain't nobody remember the Blade Runner for London. They remember Valentine's Day.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And Reeva Steenkamp… should still be here.
