Noah Lyles is one of the greatest sprinters ever: 8 world titles, the American record in the 200 and Olympic gold in the 100. But he still doesn't have the one he wants most, Olympic gold in the 200. Two Olympics, two bronze medals. And in 2026 he lost at US Indoors, pulled up in the 200 final at USAs and ended his season with a hamstring strain. So is Noah Lyles falling off?

So y'all tell me: is Noah Lyles falling off, or is he coming back healthy to finally get the job done in LA? Let me know in the comments.

Facts: 4th in the 200 at the 2016 Olympic Trials as a high schooler (20.09, high school record), turned pro out of high school. World 200m champion 2019 (Doha), 2022 (Eugene, 19.31 American record, breaking Michael Johnson's 19.32 from 1996), 2023 (Budapest, 100/200/4x100 triple) and 2025 (Tokyo, 4th straight, tying Usain Bolt). Tokyo 2021 Olympics: 200 bronze (19.74), Andre De Grasse won; spoke openly about depression and medication. Paris 2024: 100 gold by 0.005 over Kishane Thompson; 200 bronze (19.70) after testing positive for COVID, Letsile Tebogo won in 19.46. 2025: withdrew from his Atlanta opener (ankle); Tokyo Worlds 100 bronze behind Oblique Seville and Kishane Thompson. 2026: 3rd in the 60 at USA Indoors (training partner Jordan Anthony won); USA 100 champion in 9.79; pulled up in the 200 final (Garrett Kaalund won in 20.04); ended his season on September 4 with a hamstring strain, missing the Ultimate Championship in Budapest.
