# Noah Lyles: script v1 (user reads it, 26 Nov 2026)
Working title: "The Fall Off Of Noah Lyles"
Read it how you talk. Numbers are digits.

## Part 1
Noah Lyles is one of the greatest sprinters we ever seen. Honestly I got him top 6 or 7 all time. He got world titles, he got an Olympic gold, he got the American record in the 200. But this year he finished 3rd at US indoors, then he pulled up in the 200 final at USAs, and then he shut his whole season down with a hamstring. And the one thing he really wanted, Olympic gold in the 200, he still ain't got it. So is Noah Lyles falling off?

## Part 2
Y'all gotta remember where he came from. 2016 Olympic Trials, he still in high school, and he makes the 200 final and finishes 4th in 20.09. A high school record, running against grown men. And instead of going to college, he turned pro right out of high school. Everybody knew this kid was gon' be special.

## Part 3
And he didn't waste no time. 2019 in Doha, he wins his first world title in the 200. Then in 2022 at Worlds in Eugene he runs 19.31 and breaks Michael Johnson's American record, a record that been sitting there since 1996. And in 2023 in Budapest he did something nobody had done since Usain Bolt. He won the 100, the 200, and the 4x100. At that point he was the face of the sport, and he was talking like it too.

## Part 4
But the one thing that keeps following him is the Olympics in the 200. Tokyo 2021, he was the world champ, the favorite, and he got bronze. Andre De Grasse won it. And he was real open after that about dealing with depression and being on medication, and I respect him for saying that out loud. But on the track, that was the first time he came up short when it mattered most.

## Part 5
Then Paris 2024. And I'm gon' give him his credit, he won the 100. He beat Kishane Thompson by 5 thousandths of a second, one of the closest finishes ever. Olympic champion. But then the 200, his event, the one he said he was gon' dominate, he tests positive for COVID during the Games, runs anyway, and gets bronze again. Letsile Tebogo won it in 19.46. And Noah left that track in a wheelchair. So 2 Olympics, 2 bronze medals in his main event.

## Part 6
And that's the part people don't talk about enough. Yeah he got the 100 gold, but the 200 is his event. That's the race he built his whole name on. Usain Bolt won the 200 at 3 straight Olympics. Noah got 0. And when you're trying to be one of the greatest ever, that's the thing they gon' bring up every time.

## Part 7
Then 2025 is when the injuries start. In May he pulls out of his season opener in Atlanta with a tight ankle, and from what was reported it was the first time in about 6 years he had to pull out of a race with an injury. Now to be fair, he came back and won his 4th straight world title in the 200 in Tokyo, which tied Usain Bolt. But in the 100 he got bronze and lost his world title to 2 Jamaicans, Oblique Seville and Kishane Thompson. And after the 200 he said nothing would ever fill that empty feeling of not having the Olympic gold in the 200. He said that himself.

## Part 8
And then 2026, this is where it really started looking different. US indoors in March, he finishes 3rd in the 60. His own training partner Jordan Anthony beat him. Then at USAs in July he wins the 100 in 9.79, so the speed is still there. But 2 days later in the 200 final, his event, he pulls up halfway with his hamstring. Garrett Kaalund wins it in 20.04, a time Noah would usually run in his sleep.

## Part 9
He said it was just a cramp at first. Then in September he gets on Instagram and says he's done for the year. A small strain turned into other stuff flaring up, and he couldn't train right. So he skipped the big new championship in Budapest, with $150,000 for first place. That's Noah Lyles, the guy who always wanna be on the biggest stage, missing the biggest meet of the year.

## Part 10
So here's my take. I don't think Noah Lyles is washed, he still ran 9.79 this year. But I do think his prime might be behind him. He's 29 now, the injuries keep coming back every year, and the young boys are coming. Kishane Thompson, Oblique Seville, Tebogo, Gout Gout, they all faster than they ever been, and they ain't scared of him no more. And the 200 Olympic gold he wants so bad, he only got one more real shot, LA 2028, and he gon' be 31.

## Part 11
And honestly that's what makes this hard. Top 6 or 7 all time, 8 world titles, Olympic champ in the 100. But if he never gets that 200 gold, I feel like people gon' always say he came up short in the race that mattered most to him. So y'all tell me, is Noah Lyles falling off, or is he gon' come back healthy and finally get the job done in LA? Let me know in the comments.
