# Noah Lyles: research (26 Nov 2026). Angle (user): on top, top 6-7 sprinter all time (user's opinion), but no Olympic 200 gold, injuries the last few years.
## Career (well-documented; re-check any number before final)
- 2016: 4th in the Olympic Trials 200 final as a high schooler (20.09, HS record; Norman 5th 20.14, from our Norman research). Turned pro out of HS (Adidas).
- 2019 Doha: world 200 champion (19.83) + 4x100 gold.
- Tokyo 2021 Olympics: 200 bronze (19.74); Andre De Grasse won. Talked openly about depression/meds.
- 2022 Eugene: world 200 gold 19.31, American record (broke Michael Johnson's 19.32).
- 2023 Budapest: 100 (9.83), 200 (19.52), 4x100 triple gold; first since Bolt 2015. "World champion of what?" comments about NBA.
- Paris 2024: 100 gold 9.79 by 0.005 over Kishane Thompson. 200 bronze (19.70) after testing positive for COVID; Letsile Tebogo won 19.46. Left in a wheelchair; skipped 4x100 (US relay was DQ'd).
- 2025: tight ankle, withdrew from Atlanta City Games in May (first injury withdrawal in ~6 years per reports). Tokyo Worlds: 200 gold 19.52 (4th straight, ties Bolt), Bednarek 19.58, Levell 19.64. 100 bronze; Jamaica's Oblique Seville and Kishane Thompson 1-2, lost his 100 title. Quote: "I don't think any amount of World Championship wins in the 200m will ever give me the empty feeling that I have for that 200m Olympic gold right now."
## 2026
- 28 Feb-1 Mar USATF Indoors (Staten Island): 60m 3rd 6.51; training partner Jordan Anthony won 6.45, Bromell 6.47 (NBC, TFRRS). Lyles handed Anthony the championship belt.
- 24/25 Jul USAs New York: 100 title in 9.79 (world lead, ties PB, 3rd US 100 title), Ronnie Baker 2nd 9.875.
- 26 Jul: pulled up halfway through the 200 final with "somewhat of a cramp" in his hamstring; Garrett Kaalund won 20.04.
- 4 Sep: ended his season on Instagram; a small strain led to other things flaring up; missed the Ultimate Championship in Budapest ($150K first prizes). Aims for Beijing 2027 Worlds and LA 2028. Reuters: "eight-times world champion".
