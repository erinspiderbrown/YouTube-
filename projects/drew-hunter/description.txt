Drew Hunter was the best high school miler America had seen since Alan Webb. He broke Webb's high school indoor mile record, became the first US high schooler under 8:00 in the 3000, ran that famous Penn Relays anchor leg, then skipped Oregon and signed a 10-year deal with Adidas at 18. Ten years later he still has never made an Olympic or World Championship team on the track. So what went wrong?

Facts: 7:59.33 indoor 3000 (Jan 2016) and 3:58.25 indoor mile at the Armory (Feb 2016); signed with Adidas July 8, 2016; US indoor 2-mile champion 8:25.29 (2019); missed the 2021 Olympic Trials with a torn plantar fascia; 4th in the 10,000m at the 2024 Olympic Trials (27:53.35); 4th in the 5000m at the 2025 USAs; US Road 5K champion and 13:14 American road 5K record at the World Road Running Championships, Copenhagen, September 2026.
