# Drew Hunter: research (24 Nov 2026)

Sources: Wikipedia (W), AP/SI/ESPN Feb 2016, Hunter's newsletter "Rethinking my entire running career" (7 May 2026, N),
Colorado Runner 6 Oct 2026 (C), CITIUS Mag podcast 18 Nov 2021 (P), FloTrack article on 2019 Worlds withdrawal (text only, no footage).

## Verified
- Born 5 Sep 1997, Purcellville, Virginia. Loudoun Valley HS. 4th of 9 kids, 5 adopted (W).
- Parents Marc (13:36 5000 at Cleveland State) and Joan Hunter (ran at WVU) coached his HS team, and coached Alan Webb as a freshman at South Lakes (W).
- Jan 2016: 7:59.33 indoor 3000, first US high schooler under 8:00 (SI 31 Jan 2016).
- Feb 2016: 3:58.25 indoor mile at the Armory, broke Alan Webb's HS indoor record (AP/SI/ESPN 6-7 Feb 2016). W says he lowered it to 3:57.81 at Millrose two weeks later.
- 2016 Penn Relays DMR anchor: got the stick ~50 m down, ran a ~4:00 leg, won for Loudoun Valley (4.1M-view FloTrack video; can't use the footage).
- 2016 Gatorade National XC Runner of the Year.
- Committed to Oregon Nov 2015, then signed a 10-year Adidas deal on 8 Jul 2016 and skipped college (W/SI).
- Tinman Elite co-founder, Boulder (C).
- 19 Feb/23 Feb 2019: US indoor 2-mile champion, 8:25.29 meet record (W).
- 2019 USAs 5000: 5th, 13:29.19, then withdrew from Doha Worlds with a foot injury (W, FloTrack article).
- Ruptured plantar fascia, missed the 2021 Olympic Trials (W, P).
- Tinman Elite split with coach Tom Schwartz; his mom Joan took over (P, 2021).
- 6 Nov 2021: US Road 5K champion, 13:53 (W, P).
- 2022-23 USAs: 1500 heat exit 2022; 8th in the 1500 final 2023 (W).
- 2024 Olympic Trials 10,000: 4th, 27:53.35. Top 3 go to Paris (W).
- 2025 USAs 5000: 4th, 13:27.16 (W).
- 2026: US Road 5K champion again; Road Worlds Copenhagen 19 Sep 2026, 11th, 13:14 = American road 5K record (old 13:17 Abdihamid Nur) (C). 4th 5th Ave Mile 3:46.50.
- Never made a US team for an Olympics or outdoor track Worlds.
