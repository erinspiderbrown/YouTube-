## Patterns to copy (applied in thumbnails.py)
1. **The age or number is the hook.** "17-Year-Old" (1089x), "20-Year-Old" (86x), "at 21!" (10x), "after 60 years" (300x). Tebogo's arc carries three ages: **18 → 20 → 21**. This is the year-panels layout the user picked last time, now with ages (option A), and a single-age version (E).
2. **One big, sharp, real face plus a race moment.** Every big outlier pairs a large face with a race frame or a soft stadium background (Speed Academics, Sprint Icons, Omanyala). Options B, C and E.
3. **National and continental pride: gold type plus flag colours.** Omanyala's "after 60 years" at 300x and Botswana's "first ever" Olympic champion video at 12.8x and 1.2M views. Gold gradient type, Botswana light blue, and an Africa silhouette with Botswana marked (B).
4. **Rivalry names pull views.** "Lyles vs Tebogo" (102x), "Ndori beats Tebogo" (271x), "Gout Gout vs Tebogo" (11–38x). That gives the versus split with the real Paris times (D).
5. **A plain "The Rise of Letsile Tebogo" underperforms.** The same title already exists (MisterFil: 59K, about 1x), and World Athletics' doc did 1.1x. The vidIQ score is 83, against 95 for "How Letsile Tebogo Became Africa's First Olympic 200m Champion". Keep "The Rise of…" as a subtitle or after a colon, and lead with the **first-African** fact.
- Banners like "IS HE EVEN HUMAN?" and "EXCLUSIVE" dominate the sprint-compilation niche, but they read as clickbait. They don't fit a respectful doc about a continent's pride, so we take only the structure (face + big number + short line).
- Nothing in the thumbnails uses his mother's death. "Life After Loss" (Kedi Molosiwa) is the only top video that touches it, and only in the title.
