# Thumbnail photo credits / sources

Every image is a real photo or a real broadcast frame. Nothing is AI-generated. The Africa outline is drawn from Natural Earth data (public domain) via world-atlas@2.0.2.
Source files are in the scratchpad `tebogo/src/` (Commons originals in `tebogo/commons/`). Face positions are in `thumbnails.py` (`SRC`).
