# Jakob Ingebrigtsen: verified facts (16 Nov 2026)
Sources: Wikipedia (Jakob Ingebrigtsen, Ingebrigtsen family), World Athletics results, AP/Reuters/AFP via RTE, Irish Times, NBC Sports, CNN, Athletics Weekly, Marathon Handbook, LetsRun, CITIUS. No FloTrack/MileSplit footage (text citations OK).

## Family / childhood
- Born 19 Sep 2000, Sandnes, Norway. Parents Gjert and Tone Eva; 7 children (Kristoffer, Henrik, Filip, Martin, Jakob, William, Ingrid).
- Gjert coached Henrik, Filip and Jakob until 2022. Henrik: 2012 European 1500 champion. Filip: 2016 European 1500 champion.
- TV series "Team Ingebrigtsen" (2016-2021) followed the family. Amazon doc "Ingebrigtsen: Born to Run" (2024).
- 27 May 2017, Prefontaine Classic: 3:58.07 mile at 16, then youngest ever sub-4 (11th). Broken by Sam Ruthe (15) in 2025.
- 2016 European XC junior champion; 2017 European U20 5000 champion.

## Rise
- Aug 2018 European Champs, Berlin, age 17: 1500 (3:38.10) + 5000 (13:17.06). First man to win that double at the Europeans.
- 2019 Doha Worlds: 4th 1500, 5th 5000.
- Tokyo 2020 (Aug 2021): 1500 gold, 3:28.32 Olympic record, age 20.
- 2022 Eugene Worlds: 1500 silver (Jake Wightman, GB, won), 5000 gold.
- 2023 Budapest Worlds: 1500 silver (Josh Kerr, GB, won), 5000 gold.
- Paris 2024: 1500 4th (Cole Hocker gold, Olympic record 3:27.65; Kerr silver; Yared Nuguse bronze). 5000 gold 13:13.66 days later.
- Records: 3000m WR 7:17.55 (Silesia 2024); 2000m WR 4:43.13 (Brussels 2023); two-mile world best 7:54.10 (Paris 2023); indoor mile WR 3:45.14 + indoor 1500 WR 3:29.63 (Lievin, 13 Feb 2025); European outdoor 1500 record 3:26.73 (Monaco 2024).

## Rivalry
- Kerr beat him at 2023 Worlds, 2024 Bowerman Mile (3:45.34 v 3:45.60), Paris 1500. Kerr (Nov 2026 AMA): "I wouldn't say we're buddies!" Jakob after Paris 1500: "I ruined the race for myself."

## Family case
- Oct 2023 (VG): Jakob, Henrik, Filip: "We have grown up with a father who has been very aggressive and controlling and who has used physical violence and threats as part of his upbringing." Gjert's lawyer: baseless; "I have never used violence against my children." Brother Martin defended Gjert.
- 2024: Gjert charged. Trial from 24 Mar 2025, Sandnes, ~6 weeks, 40+ witnesses; Jakob testified.
- 16 Jun 2025 verdict: acquitted of all charges involving Jakob (reasonable doubt); convicted of one count of assaulting daughter Ingrid; 15-day suspended sentence, NOK 10,000 damages. Prosecutors had asked for 2.5 years.

## Body breaks / comeback
- Mar 2025 Nanjing World Indoors: 1500 + 3000 double. Achilles injury from May 2025; missed outdoor Diamond League.
- Tokyo Worlds Sep 2025: out in 1500 heats (8th in heat, 3:37.84); 10th in 5000.
- Feb 2026: Achilles surgery (Stanford).
- 10 Aug 2026 European Champs Birmingham: 5000 gold 13:15.29, first race in ~11 months; 4th straight European 5000, 7th European outdoor title.
- 11 Sep 2026 Ultimate Championship Budapest: 5000 gold 12:59.24 (Hocker 13:00.05, Parker Wolfe 13:00.13), $150,000.
