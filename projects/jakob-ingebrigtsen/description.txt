Jakob Ingebrigtsen broke 4 minutes in the mile at 16, won the European 1500 and 5000 at 17, and became Olympic champion at 20. He was coached by his dad, Gjert, alongside his brothers Henrik and Filip, and the whole family grew up on Norwegian TV. Then came the rivalries with Josh Kerr and Jake Wightman, the world records, and a family split that ended with Jakob testifying against his own father in court. After an Achilles injury and surgery, he came back in 2026 to win the European 5000 and the Ultimate Championship in Budapest.

So what y'all think? Is Jakob Ingebrigtsen the greatest distance runner of his generation? And did his dad make him, or did he make it in spite of him? Let me know in the comments.
