# Mary Cain: research (17 Nov 2026)

Sources: Wikipedia (Mary Cain (athlete)), NYT Opinion video op-ed 7 Nov 2019 ("I Was the Fastest Girl in America, Until I Joined Nike", 20.6M views),
LetsRun, RunBlogRun, Running Magazine / Marathon Handbook / The Oregonian (settlement), Kirkus / T&F News (memoir), AOL (Stanford med school).

- Born 3 May 1996, New York City; grew up in Bronxville, NY. Bronxville HS, class of 2014.
- 2012 (16): US Olympic Trials 800 (18th, 2:04.11). World Juniors 1500 6th.
- Oct 2012: starts training under Alberto Salazar (Nike Oregon Project head coach) while still in high school.
- 2013 (16-17): indoor mile 4:32.78 HS record (Jan 26); 1500 4:04.62 (17 May, Oxy) = American junior record (broke Suzy Favor's 4:09.10) and HS record;
  800 1:59.51 at the Prefontaine Classic (1 June) = US HS + junior record (broke Kim Gallagher, 1982).
- June 2013: 2nd at USA Outdoors 1500 -> Worlds team. Moscow 2013 Worlds at 17: youngest American ever on a Worlds team; youngest ever 1500 finalist; 10th in the final (4:07.19).
- 20 Nov 2013: skips college, turns pro with Nike Oregon Project (still a HS senior). Later attends University of Portland honors program.
- 2014: indoor mile 4:24.11 (US junior record, 24 Jan); US indoor 1500 champion (Albuquerque, 4:07.05); World Junior 3000 gold (24 July, Eugene, 8:58.48).
- Decline: 2015 USAs 8th (4:16.77). 2016 Olympic Trials 1500 11th (4:13.45). Leaves Oregon Project, returns to New York (2016).
- Her allegations (NYT 7 Nov 2019): coaches wanted her "thinner and thinner"; Salazar set a goal of 114 lb; weighed in front of teammates;
  birth control pills and diuretics suggested for weight loss; lost her period for 3 years; RED-S; broke 5 bones; started cutting herself.
  (Quote exact lines from the NYT clip transcript, not from memory.)
- Nike statement: "deeply troubling allegations which have not been raised by Mary or her parents before. Mary was seeking to rejoin the Oregon Project
  and Alberto's team as recently as April of this year." Nike announced an investigation. Salazar denied her claims.
- 30 Sep 2019: Salazar banned 4 years (USADA, doping violations; upheld by CAS 2021). Oct 2019: Nike shuts down the Oregon Project.
- 13 Nov 2019: Sports Illustrated publishes accounts from nine other former Oregon Project runners.
- 2021: US Center for SafeSport bans Salazar for life (sexual and emotional misconduct). Salazar denied.
- 11 Oct 2021: Cain sues Salazar and Nike for $20 million (Multnomah County). 27 Nov 2023: settled, terms not disclosed.
- Founded Atalanta NYC (nonprofit pro team; women runners mentor girls in NYC). Competes for Ireland since Feb 2024.
- Memoir "This Is Not About Running" (Mariner), 28 April 2026. Now a medical student at Stanford.
