On November 7, 2019, Mary Cain posted a 7-minute video that has over 20 million views. Six years earlier she was the fastest girl America had ever seen: a 4:04.62 1500 and a 1:59.51 800 in high school, and a World Championships final at 17. She signed with Nike's Oregon Project and coach Alberto Salazar before she finished high school. This is what she says happened next, what Nike and Salazar said, and where she is now.

The medal she never won, or the video she did make: which one matters more? Let me know in the comments.
