DESCRIPTION
Look at this Oklahoma State team photo. One of these runners got banned four years for blood doping. Who did you pick?

I saw Shea Foster race in person in 2021, as a freshman at Sam Houston State. He was a walk-on who broke three Southland Conference records in one weekend, survived getting hit by an 18-wheeler, and made Team USA for the Paris Paralympics. Then his drug test from the Paralympic Trials came back positive for roxadustat, a drug that makes your body produce more EPO.

Part of this story has never been reported anywhere. I talked to some of his former Oklahoma State teammates, and in this video I share what they told me.
