[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Now most freshmen is eighteen. Solomon Kipchoge was twenty-eight.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And he ain't just old. He been runnin' pro races in Europe. The year before, in France, he ran a half marathon in fifty-nine minutes and thirty-seven seconds. That was faster than the American record at the time.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And the thing is… he ain't break a single rule. The NCAA had no age limit. None.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] He transferred to Washington State, the same school Henry Rono ran for. And in 2025 he finished third at nationals. He was set to turn thirty that May.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] Companies like Scholarbook market East African runners straight to American college coaches. Times, videos, all of it. Coach just gotta pick.

[calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] And the math changed for the schools. The NCAA dropped the SAT and ACT requirement. Scholarships now cover the full cost: school, housin', food. And new roster limits mean every spot gotta count.

[pause] [calm] [relaxed] [Texas accent] So if you a coach and you got one spot… do you take a eighteen-year-old American who might be good in three years? Or a twenty-four-year-old who can score at nationals tomorrow?
