## Verified
- Born 3 Dec 1997. Vista Murrieta HS (California).
- 2015 CIF state meet: 400m 45.19 (= national HS record, tied), 200m state title 20.30; 7 races in 28 hours. 200m 20.24 (state record) at USA Juniors. Gatorade POY.
- 2016 Olympic Trials 200m: 5th in the final (beat Gatlin in a semi). World U20 champion 200m (20.17 CR) + 4x100.
- USC: NCAA indoor 44.52 (Mar 2018, indoor world best); NCAA outdoor 43.61 (8 Jun 2018, Eugene, world lead, collegiate record); 4-time NCAA champion; The Bowerman 2018. Turned pro with Nike (2018), coached by Quincy Watts.
- 20 Apr 2019: 43.45 at Mt. SAC (season opener; one of the fastest ever). 19.70 200m in Rome (Jun 2019). 9.86 100m (Jul 2020): sub-10/20/44 club.
- 2019 Doha Worlds: gold-medal favourite, last in his semifinal, jogging the last ~100 m, injury (coach Watts: tendon).
- Tokyo 2021: 5th in the 400m (summaries; verify time 44.31), Olympic 4x400 gold.
- 2022: US champion; World champion 400m in Eugene, 44.29; 4x400 gold.
- 2023: switched to the 100m under John Smith; out in round 1 at USAs; skipped Budapest Worlds (had a bye as champion), took a break; "setback after setback", tendonitis (World Athletics feature).
- Nov 2023: back to Quincy Watts and the 400m.
- 2024: Olympic Trials 2nd, 44.41 (Quincy Hall won); said he hadn't sprinted all-out since February because of injuries. Paris Olympic final: 8th (last), 45.62. Pelvis injury from 2024 that worsened through Paris (LetsRun 2026 summary).
- 2025: no races. Agent: surgery "for a matter that occurred last season"; USATF: "spent most of 2025 recovering from injury".
- 2026: first race in ~19 months in April (lead-off leg for Gainesville Elite, ~44.7 split), new coach (name not confirmed). Won the 400m at the LA Track Festival (late May). 44.94 at Sound Running (summary). Not in the 8-man USATF 400m final, 25 Jul 2026 (won by Chris Bailey 44.39). Whether he ran the rounds or scratched: NOT confirmed.
