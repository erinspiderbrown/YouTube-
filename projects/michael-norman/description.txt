Michael Norman opened 2019 running 43.45 in the 400, and everybody said the world record was coming. He'd run 20.14 at the 2016 Olympic Trials as a high schooler right behind Noah Lyles, set the collegiate record at USC and won the Bowerman. Then came last in the Doha semi, 5th in Tokyo, a slow win in Eugene, a skipped Budapest, dead last in Paris and no USAs final in 2026. Is he the biggest wasted talent in American sprinting?

So y'all tell me: is Michael Norman the biggest wasted talent in track, or is he coming back for LA 2028? And should he have run for Japan? Let me know in the comments.

Facts: 45.19 at the 2015 CIF State meet; 5th in the 2016 Olympic Trials 200m final (20.14), Lyles 4th (20.09); 44.52 indoor world best and 43.61 collegiate record (2018); 43.45 at Mt. SAC (April 20, 2019); 19.70 (200m) and 9.86 (100m); out in the 2019 World Championships semifinal (tendon injury); 5th at Tokyo 2020 (44.31); 2022 world champion in 44.29, the slowest winning time at Worlds since 2011, with Kirani James 44.48; 2023 switch to the 100m under John Smith, out in the first round at USAs; 2nd at the 2024 Olympic Trials (44.41), 8th in the Paris final (45.62); pelvis surgery in 2025; did not make the 2026 USAs final. Running for Japan is the creator's opinion, not something Norman has said.
