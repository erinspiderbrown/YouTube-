# Candace Hill: research (11 Nov 2026)

## Verified facts
- **Background:** Candace Kathryn Hill, born February 11, 1999, in Conyers, Georgia. Rockdale County High School.
- **The 10.98:**
  - June 20, 2015, at the Brooks PR Invitational, Shoreline, Washington, wind +2.0 (the legal max). Some sources say June 21.
  - Age 16, as a high school sophomore. The first high school girl ever under 11 seconds.
  - Records: age-16 world best, under-18 world best, American under-20 record. It isn't an NFHS high school record because it was out of season.
- **2015 awards:** Gatorade National Girls Track & Field Athlete of the Year, only the 2nd sophomore ever after Marion Jones. Also Track & Field News HS Athlete of the Year, USATF Youth Athlete of the Year, and IAAF Rising Star.
- **2015 World Youth Championships (Cali, Colombia):** 100m gold in 11.08 (championship record) and 200m gold in 22.43 (−0.7, still her 200m personal best).
- **The ASICS deal:** December 17, 2015, a 10-year contract at age 16. She's the youngest American track athlete ever to go pro.
  - The deal reportedly covers college tuition. ASICS didn't disclose the money. It ran through her "peak years", until about 26.
  - Being pro made her ineligible for college track. She had considered USC and Florida.
- **2016:**
  - She missed about 4 months of training.
  - US junior champion in the 100m (11.24) and 200m (22.76).
  - Olympic Trials: 13th in the 100m (11.07 wind-aided) and 14th in the 200m (23.05).
  - World U20 Championships (Bydgoszcz): 100m gold in 11.07 (championship record) and 4x100m gold.
- **2017–2019:** no PBs. 2019 USAs: 14th in the 100m (11.53) and 14th in the 200m (24.28).
- **Coaches:** 5 coaching changes by 2023 (RunBlogRun).
  - 2021: Caryl Smith Gilbert at USC (World Athletics).
  - 2023: Dennis Shaver in Baton Rouge, with Aleia Hobbs and Mikiah Brisco.
- **2021 (World Athletics feature, May 29, 2021):**
  - "I had so many people constantly in my ear saying: 'you made the wrong decision'." "It had me second-guessing myself."
  - "My therapist told me that some of your emotional issues can form into physical pain, and that's true." "If your mind is not in a great space, your body quickly follows suit."
  - "I'm starting to see the light at the end of the tunnel."
  - That season: a 10.99 season best (0.01 off her personal best), and 8th in the Olympic Trials 100m final (11.23).
- **2022:** USAs 200m, 11th (22.50).
- **2023 (RunBlogRun, March 2023):** studying pre-med at LSU (psychology), with 3 years left on her ASICS deal. Goal: "I want to PR in the 100m and the 200m this year."
- **2024:**
  - Fastest 150m of the season (16.29) at the Adidas Atlanta City Games, just ahead of training partner Favour Ofili.
  - **Olympic Trials 100m final: 7th in 11.00** (semi 11.10). Sha'Carri won in 10.71. 200m: out in the semis (22.61).
- **2025:** season best 11.23 (June 14, 2025, Star Athletics Sprint Series, Winter Garden, FL; source EliteTrackr).
- **Her 100m PB is still the 10.98 she ran at 16.**
- Not found: anything on 2026, or whether ASICS renewed her deal. Leave both out.
