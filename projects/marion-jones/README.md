## Facts and sources (checked 6 Nov 2026)
- **Early life:**
  - Born 12 Oct 1975 in Los Angeles. Her mother is from Belize; she holds US and Belizean citizenship.
  - Won the California state 100m four years running. Track & Field News HS Athlete of the Year 1991 and 1992.
  - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **1992 Olympic Trials:**
  - Missed the 200m final by 0.07s. She turned down a relay alternate spot for Barcelona: "when people come to see my gold medals, I want to be able to say I ran for them."
  - [SI Vault 1998](https://vault.si.com/vault/1998/06/29/the-fast-lane-marion-jones-is-the-best-female-sprinter-on-the-planet-a-rising-star-in-the-long-jump-and-a-worldclass-point-guardand-she-hasnt-even-hit-her-stride-yet)
- **1992 missed drug test:**
  - She faced a four-year ban; Johnnie Cochran got it overturned.
  - Sources agree on Cochran but differ on the details, so the script says only "a missed drug test… Johnnie Cochran got her off".
  - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **UNC:** basketball scholarship; NCAA champion with North Carolina in 1994 as a freshman. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones), [SI 1995](https://vault.si.com/vault/1995/02/09/13-marion-jones)
- **World titles:** 100m gold at Athens 1997 and Seville 1999 (plus long jump bronze in 1999). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **Sydney 2000:**
  - Gold in the 100m (10.75), 200m (21.84) and 4x400; bronze in the long jump and 4x100.
  - First woman to win five track and field medals at a single Olympics.
  - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **C.J. Hunter:**
  - Married 3 Oct 1998. Four positive nandrolone tests in summer 2000, revealed during the Sydney Games; he was pulled from his coaching credential. Divorced 2002.
  - [Wikipedia: C. J. Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._J._Hunter), [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/cj-hunter-dies-shot-put)
- **BALCO:**
  - Coach Trevor Graham anonymously sent USADA a syringe of THG ("the clear"), which triggered the BALCO investigation (2003).
  - Victor Conte told ABC's 20/20 (Dec 2004) he gave Jones banned drugs; she denied it for years.
  - [Wikipedia: BALCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_Area_Laboratory_Co-operative)
- **Tim Montgomery:** son Tim Montgomery Jr. born 28 Jun 2003. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **2006:**
  - Her A sample was reported positive for EPO, but the B sample came back negative (6 Sep 2006).
  - She was linked to Montgomery's check-fraud scheme: a check to her was deposited in her account.
- **The plea, 5 Oct 2007 (White Plains, NY):**
  - Pleaded guilty to two counts of lying to federal agents: about steroid use, and about the check-fraud case.
  - She said Graham told her "the clear" was flaxseed oil, and that by November 2003 she knew it was a performance-enhancing drug, yet she lied to agents that month.
  - Outside the courthouse, in tears: "It is with a great amount of shame that I stand before you and tell you that I have betrayed your trust", and "I have let them down. I have let my country down."
  - Retired the same day.
  - [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/marion-jones-pleads-guilty-to-doping), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2007/10/6/marion-jones-admits-steroid-use), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/15067640)
- **Medals:** she handed them back in October 2007; the IOC formally stripped all five on 12 Dec 2007. [NY Sun](https://www.nysun.com/article/sports-marion-jones-is-stripped-of-olympic-medals)
- **Relay teammates:**
  - The IOC stripped them too (April 2008).
  - CAS gave the medals back in July 2010, because no rule in 2000 let a whole relay be disqualified for one doper.
  - [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/marion-jones-teammates-win-back-stripped-medals), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2008/4/10/jones-teammates-lose-medals-2)
- **Sentence:**
  - 11 Jan 2008, Judge Kenneth Karas: 6 months (with 2 months concurrent on the check-fraud count), 2 years supervised release, 800 hours community service.
  - [ABC News](https://abcnews.go.com/TheLaw/story?id=4120748), [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/news/jones-handed-six-month-jail-sentence-1.1219860)
- **Prison:**
  - Reported to FMC Carswell, Fort Worth, on 11 Mar 2008; released 5 Sep 2008.
  - She says she spent 49 days in solitary after an altercation (her own account in a 2024 ABC interview; the script says "she says").
  - [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Sports_News/2008/09/05/Marion-Jones-out-of-prison/83991220637919/), [ABC11 2024](https://abc11.com/post/marion-jones-former-olympic-champion-unc-alum-reflects/15121502/)
- **After:**
  - WNBA Tulsa Shock 2010–11: 47 games, 2.6 ppg, waived July 2011.
  - Book *On the Right Track* (2010). Now a speaker and coach.
  - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Jones)
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - The 2026 rumours and anything about her private life now (not relevant).
  - The disputed 1999 vs 2000 start date for "the clear": the script follows her plea wording ("by November 2003 she knew").
