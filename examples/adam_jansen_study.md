## Packaging
- **Makhloufi thumbnail:** his name huge across the back, his face biting the gold medal, and a serif quote caption of the
  official ruling: "officials ruled he made no real attempt to compete". The same quote-caption device Jumpman uses.
- **Iceberg thumbnail:** the iceberg graphic with the theories as props (shoes, gel, watch, cash, a map of Kenya), the
  athletes' faces, and "IT'S DEEPER THAN YOU THINK".
- **Others:** a red circle on the key detail (a shoe) with an arrow; a single huge stat ("1:59:30"); "EVERYONE LABELLED
  HIM A FRAUD" with an arrow; a tier list ("5000M WR?").

## Iceberg script structure (transcript)
1. **Hook:** a stat that shows the scale ("as many marathon records in 10 years as every other event combined"). Then
   the moment (the sub-2), then the question: "are all these theories as credible as some make them out to be?"
2. **The promise:** "going from the most commonly held explanations… down to the most niche and overlooked theories."
3. **Each tier is a chapter:** explain it, back it with facts (studies, dates, records), give his own verdict ("it's just
   the tip of the iceberg").

## Lessons for The Erin Brown
- **Single-story controversy built around an official ruling and its exact words** = the Gatlin video.
  New thumbnail T3: "STRIKE 1?" plus a serif caption: "He certainly is not a doper." (arbitration panel, 2002).
- **An iceberg in our lane:** "The Track & Field Doping Iceberg", "The Sha'Carri Richardson Iceberg" or "The Track
  Drama Iceberg". RunnerBoi's "Track and Field Iceberg" got 124K, and Adam's got 341K.
- **Hot take on the biggest star, backed by facts:** "Noah Lyles Has Completely Lost It?"-style, only when the facts back it.
- **Skip predictions, "what everyone missed" and interviews.** They flop for him and for us (the Tibz interview got 2.2K).
- **His on-camera format** (talking head + screenshots + footage) is a ready template for the podcast-style videos.
