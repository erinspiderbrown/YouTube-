## What he does
1. **He's fast on history.** The 3.7M marathon and the 2.3M Duplantis video went up within days of the event. When something historic happens anywhere in track, he explains it while people are searching for it. His three biggest aren't sprint videos (marathon, high jump, pole vault).
2. **Script formula** (from the 1:59:30 transcript):
   - **Hook:** a self-aware joke ("Well, duh, it's only two hours"), then the stakes ("a once-in-a-lifetime moment"), then the thesis: "I just don't think people understand how fast this is."
   - **History:** the first record in 1908, the failed and unofficial attempts, and an emotional beat (Kiptum's death at 24) before the event itself.
   - **The event, told through numbers:** split by split, with a turn ("at the distance known as the wall… they sped up").
   - **"Let me lay it down for you":** turn every number into something anyone gets. "Only 625 men ran a faster 5K fresh, out of 8.8 billion people." "Combine the splits and it's a 10K that would have won last year's world title."
   - **A personal test:** he goes out and tries to run the pace himself.
   - **The voice:** first person, casual, jokes, his own opinions ("And I'm skeptical", "Whether you like it or not").
3. **Editing is simple and all real footage:**
   - broadcast race clips
   - results tables and split lists as plain overlays
   - newspaper-style serif quote captions
   - a black title card in red serif ("How fast is a sub-2 hour marathon?")
   - side-by-side results comparisons
   - an occasional emoji
   - no fancy motion graphics: the narration carries it
4. **Thumbnails are real race frames with one annotation:**
   - a red circle, a yellow path line or a dashed bar line
   - or a serif quote caption ("She lapped the entire field", "Quicker than Bolt", "Then they asked me 'Who is your coach,' I told them 'YouTube.'")
   - or times stuck over runners (9.58 / 9.74 / 9.69 / 9.69)
   - big text only sometimes, at most 2–3 words ("THERE ARE LEVELS", "DECADE LONG CHOKE", "8 FEET")
5. **Title formulas:**
   - "I Don't Think You Understand Just HOW ___ Is"
   - "What ___ Exposed About ___"
   - "___ Finally Has A Serious Rival"
   - "What Happened To The Fastest ___ In History?"
   - "The Biggest ___ In ___ History"

   - "The Greatest ___ To Never ___"
