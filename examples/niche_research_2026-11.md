# Niche research: what's working in track YouTube (11 Nov 2026)

Method: YouTube (innertube) channel scrapes of The Erin Brown plus about 25 track channels. Outliers = 3x+ the channel's median, last 75 days.

## The Erin Brown (35.1K subs, median 5.1K)
- **Winners are current drama / exposés:**
  - "I Exposed a Nike Sprinter — Then Everything Changed" 44K
  - "He Wouldn't Leave The Track. So Track Got Rid Of Him" 15K
  - "31-Year-Old NCAA Runner QUITS Team…" 11K
  - "Who's Doping In This Photo? (You Picked Wrong)" 10K
  - the Blaskowski saga, about 10K each
- **Losers:**
  - race recaps of non-US athletes (1.6K)
  - history pieces ("The Only Guy Usain Bolt Feared" 1.7K)
  - interviews (2.2K)
  - Olympian crime story (1.5K)

### Patterns
1. **The giants step outside track:** tech (super shoes), celebrity (IShowSpeed), the steroid spectacle (Enhanced Games),
   money (lifestyle, "athletes are poor"), prodigies ("next Bolt", "16-year-old"), and data history ("fastest man every year").
   Pure track storylines top out around 100–300K.
2. **Titles:** plain words that open a question.
   - "How Fast Is X Actually?"
   - "X Is Not What You Think"
   - "What Happened To Every…"
   - "Why X Are Poor"
   - "The X That Broke Y"
   - "The Sprinter Who…"
   - "…That Shouldn't Exist"
   - "…And It Exposed a Huge Problem"
   - "…But Nobody Listened"
3. **Thumbnails:**
   - one idea, 0–3 words
   - a real, emotional face
   - a physical prop (shoe, gold medal, mansion + car, needle)
   - a comparison of 2 people (Gatlin "shh" + Bolt; Moses + Jones "HE KNEW")
   - a red arrow or "?"
   - bright and polished
4. **Length:** 8–17 minutes.
5. **Small channels hit 100K+ on topic alone:** Ryan Searl (1.1K subs, 109K) and Track Legends Rewind (1.5K subs, 126K).
6. **The best model channel for our format:** Jumpman. It's data-led storytelling on sprint topics, with a 100K–2M hit rate.
