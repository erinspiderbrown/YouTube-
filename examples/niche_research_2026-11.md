# Niche research: what's working in track YouTube (11 Nov 2026)

Method: YouTube (innertube) channel scrapes of The Erin Brown plus about 25 track channels. Outliers = 3x+ the channel's median, last 75 days.

## The Erin Brown (35.1K subs, median 5.1K)
- **Winners are current drama / exposés:**
  - "I Exposed a Nike Sprinter — Then Everything Changed" 44K
  - "He Wouldn't Leave The Track. So Track Got Rid Of Him" 15K
  - "31-Year-Old NCAA Runner QUITS Team…" 11K
  - "Who's Doping In This Photo? (You Picked Wrong)" 10K
  - the Blaskowski saga, about 10K each
- **Losers:**
  - race recaps of non-US athletes (1.6K)
  - history pieces ("The Only Guy Usain Bolt Feared" 1.7K)
  - interviews (2.2K)
  - Olympian crime story (1.5K)

Lesson: the topic does the work, not the subscriber count. The audience is high school and college athletes plus their
parents (a huge, searchable audience), and the format is "What happened to the fastest/greatest high school ___".
His thumbnails are bright and polished, with 2–3 words ("WHERE ARE THEY NOW?", "HUGE PROBLEM", "TOO EARLY!").
