## Thumbnail

## The first 2 minutes
1. **0:00–0:20, cold open made only of clips.** Another creator says the hook stat ("85% of Ben Shapiro's YouTube audience
   has disappeared"), then an enemy insults him, then the subject at his worst ("I'm popular and no one has ever heard
   of you"). No narration yet.
2. **0:21, narrator:** who he was at his peak, as a contrast ("he told the biggest conservative crowd in America
   that political correctness was dying").
3. **Irony with receipts:** his own old clip ("rap… is not actually a form of music"), then "5 years later, he was on a
   rap song himself", then top comments with like counts ("90,000 likes: Ben is the first person to rap slower than he talks").
4. **The state of things now, as numbers:** 85% of his audience gone, his company laying people off.
5. **The thesis:** "plenty of shows grew through that exact climate. Ben's didn't." It all traces back to one topic where
   "the guy who said facts don't care about your feelings" did the opposite of what he preached.
6. **The question, then the brand line:** "So how did the biggest free speech guy end up here? I'm the Internet Janitor, and
   today we're looking at the satisfying downfall of Ben Shapiro." Then straight into the chronology.

## Body
- **Chronology:** rise, an early warning sign ("At the time, it was just a bad day. 4 years later, it was a preview."),
  the turning point, the fallout.
- **The subject's own clips do most of the talking.** Narration links them with short sentences.
- **Receipts on screen:** tweets with the key line highlighted in red, viewer counts, dates, and top YouTube comments with
  like counts.
- **The hypocrisy frame:** what he preached vs what he did.

## Editing
- A cut every ~2 s.
- Kinetic captions in sync with the voice: single white caps words popping on key nouns ("GENDER", "RAP",
  "HARMONY MELODY RHYTHM", "5 YEARS LATER" typed in).
- Quote titles over a dimmed clip ("POLITICAL CORRECTNESS WAS DYING", with the key word in red).
- Split-screen interviews, other creators' reaction clips, and a news anchor for the turning point.
- Plain, no neon.
